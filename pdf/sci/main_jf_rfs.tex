\begin{abstract}
Conditional asset pricing treats the investor's information set as primitive. In cryptocurrency markets that premise fails: evidence arrives asynchronously across venues, macro vintages, on-chain ledgers, options surfaces, unlock calendars, and news, and is intermittently unavailable. This paper develops a finance-native theory of \emph{information-set compilation}. We define epistemic observations, asynchronous reconstruction bounds, a compilation operator from raw to AI-visible filtrations, a production World Model Index (WMI), and a regime-conditional Adaptive Conditional World Model Index (ACWMI). We formalize epistemic calibration penalties, band-level marginal information gain, an identification DAG driven by availability shocks, Bayesian abstention, and explanation-quality metrics (EAR/UCR/EV).

Empirically, we populate a real multi-band archive---OKX exchange bars, macro vintages, alternative series, plus thinner news/on-chain/options/tokenomics snapshots---and construct a 400-day point-in-time panel (10 assets; 4{,}000 asset-days) aligned to Yahoo returns. Mechanism engines use only pre-$t$ history. Abstention thresholds are frozen in-sample. Out of sample, thick real PIT worlds dominate exchange-only thin worlds (certainty-equivalent return $0.474$ vs $-0.011$). Leave-one-band-out on durable bands shows large CE losses from dropping macro ($-0.534$), alternative ($-0.526$), or exchange ($-0.339$). An IS-frozen ACWMI gate remains implementable (Sharpe $0.901$, CE $0.199$, abstain $29.7\%$) but does not dominate ungated thick signals on CE. The production WMI$<0.2$ rule abstains $100\%$ on the sparse archive, illustrating that quality thresholds must be frozen to the information set's support.

The contribution is a compilation theory with displayed primitives, plus a reproducible PIT identification protocol on a live multi-band laboratory. Limitations---short durable history for several bands, limited natural hard outages, and ten liquid names---are stated explicitly as the agenda for a final JF/RFS submission.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Modern asset pricing conditions on an information set $I_t$ \citep{fama1993,cochrane2005,gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021,harvey2016}. The literature's discipline has focused on how $I_t$ is \emph{used}---for discount factors, return prediction, and machine-learning pricing kernels---not on how $I_t$ is \emph{compiled} from asynchronous, quality-heterogeneous evidence. In liquid equities, the omission is often second-order: prices and accounting vintages are relatively synchronized. In cryptocurrency markets, compilation is first-order. Venue fragmentation, perpetual leverage, options walls, token unlocks, on-chain flows, and macro liquidity can map the same price path into incompatible states \citep{makarov2020,liu2022}.

This paper makes three contributions.

\paragraph{Theory.}
We formalize Regime-Conditional Adaptive World Models (RCA-WM). An AI-consumable market object is an epistemic observation $O_{j,t}=(x,\tau,q,g,r)$, not a bare feature. Asynchronous lags induce a reconstruction bound with delay, noise, and missingness terms. A compilation operator $\Pi_t$ maps the raw filtration $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t$ into an AI-visible filtration $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t$. World-model quality is measured by WMI and ACWMI. We define ECP, MIG, an identification DAG, Bayesian abstention, and explanation metrics EAR/UCR/EV. The formal apparatus absorbs and extends the World-Model-First objects in \texttt{pdf/original/}.

\paragraph{Measurement laboratory.}
We treat EvoQuant as a reproducible instrument: multi-band collectors, readiness scoring, time-slice queries, and configurable WMI/ACWMI thresholds. The project implements the theory; it does not invent it.

\paragraph{Identification on a real PIT archive.}
We bootstrap a multi-band SQLite archive, build a point-in-time panel from history tables, freeze abstention thresholds in-sample, and evaluate out-of-sample economic value. Thick worlds dominate thin worlds; durable bands have large leave-one-out CE; selective ACWMI gating is implementable under frozen thresholds.

\section{Related literature}
\label{sec:lit}

\paragraph{Information sets in asset pricing.}
Classic and modern pricing frameworks treat $I_t$ as given \citep{cochrane2005,fama1993}. Machine-learning asset pricing expands the feature span inside $I_t$ \citep{gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021} but typically assumes synchronized, clean panels. We study the prior step: how asynchronous evidence is compiled into an AI-visible world.

\paragraph{Cryptocurrency market structure.}
Crypto markets exhibit fragmentation and microstructure frictions \citep{makarov2020} and distinctive risk factors \citep{liu2022}. These facts motivate multi-band worlds rather than price-only predictors.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and abstention.}
When the world is thin or dishonest, the optimal action may be abstention. Our Bayesian rule links abstention costs to world-model quality, connecting selective prediction to information-set support.

\paragraph{Measurement error and vintages.}
Macro data are vintage-dependent; crypto bands add freshness and missingness. Our reconstruction bound and PIT construction treat measurement timing as first-class.

\section{Theory}
\label{sec:theory}

\subsection{World-model quality: breadth, stability, honesty}
\label{sec:wmi}

\begin{definition}[Breadth]
Let $K$ be the number of critical evidence bands at date $t$, and let $a_{k,t}\in\{0,1\}$ indicate whether band $k$ meets an AI-usable threshold. Breadth is
\begin{equation}
B_t=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^{K}a_{k,t}.
\label{eq:breadth}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Stability]
Let $d_{j,t}$ be a freshness/TTL penalty for source $j$ with importance weight $\omega_j\ge 0$. Stability is
\begin{equation}
U_t=\exp\!\left(-\sum_{j=1}^{J}\omega_j d_{j,t}\right).
\label{eq:stability}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Honesty]
Let $m_{j,t}=1$ if source $j$ should not enter the AI main view but is incorrectly injected. Honesty is
\begin{equation}
H_t=1-\frac{1}{J}\sum_{j=1}^{J}m_{j,t}.
\label{eq:honesty}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

The production baseline World Model Index used by the laboratory is the product form
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t\times U_t\times H_t.
\label{eq:wmi}
\end{equation}

For hierarchical readiness we use a convex combination of domain-, band-, and asset-level readiness ratios,
\begin{equation}
B^{\mathrm{hier}}_t=0.25\,B^{\mathrm{dom}}_t+0.35\,B^{\mathrm{band}}_t+0.40\,B^{\mathrm{asset}}_t,
\label{eq:bhier}
\end{equation}
and a continuous honesty score that rewards exclusion of contaminated evidence and penalizes partial contamination,
\begin{equation}
H^{\mathrm{cont}}_t=\exp(-2c_t)\max\!\bigl(0,\,1-0.5(1-e_t)\bigr),
\label{eq:hcont}
\end{equation}
where $e_t$ is the ready-share and $c_t$ a contamination share inferred from limited/missing bands.

\subsection{Epistemic observations}
\begin{definition}[Epistemic observation]
An AI-consumable market observation is
\begin{equation}
O_{j,t}=\bigl(x_{j,t},\,\tau_{j,t},\,q_{j,t},\,g_{j,t},\,r_{j,t}\bigr),
\label{eq:obs}
\end{equation}
with value $x$, latest-available time $\tau$, quality state $q$, main-view gate $g$, and semantic role $r$.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
The AI consumes a set of epistemic objects, not a flat feature matrix. Two numerically identical $x$ values with different $(\tau,q,g,r)$ are different world-model objects.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Asynchronous state space and lag error}
Let latent states evolve as
\begin{equation}
S_{t+1}=F(S_t,\eta_{t+1}).
\label{eq:state}
\end{equation}
Source $j$ observes a lagged map
\begin{equation}
X^{\mathrm{obs}}_{j,t}=h_j(S_{t-\ell_{j,t}})+\nu_{j,t},\qquad \ell_{j,t}\ge 0.
\label{eq:lagobs}
\end{equation}
\begin{assumption}[Lipschitz observation maps]
Each $h_j$ is Lipschitz with constant $L_j<\infty$.
\end{assumption}
Then
\begin{equation}
\bigl\|h_j(S_t)-h_j(S_{t-\ell_{j,t}})\bigr\|
\le L_j\sum_{u=t-\ell_{j,t}+1}^{t}\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|.
\label{eq:lipschitz}
\end{equation}
A world-model reconstruction $\widetilde S_t$ therefore obeys a bound with delay, noise, and missingness terms:
\begin{equation}
\|\widetilde S_t-S_t\|
\le C_1\sum_j\omega_j\ell_{j,t}
+C_2\sum_j\omega_j\|\nu_{j,t}\|
+C_3\sum_j\omega_j(1-z_{j,t}),
\label{eq:recon}
\end{equation}
where $z_{j,t}$ indicates availability. Latest-snapshot mechanisms, freshness/TTL rules, and readiness gates target these three terms.

\subsection{Filters and the compilation operator}
Define the raw and AI-visible filtrations
\begin{align}
\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t&=\sigma\bigl(\{X^{\mathrm{obs}}_{j,\tau}: j\le J,\ \tau\le t\}\bigr),
\label{eq:fraw}\\
\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t&=\sigma\bigl(W^{\mathrm{AI}}_t,\,D_t\bigr),
\label{eq:fai}
\end{align}
where $W^{\mathrm{AI}}_t$ is the main view and $D_t$ the diagnostic view. Compilation is the composition
\begin{equation}
\Pi_t=\mathcal{B}_t\circ M_t\circ A_t,\qquad
W^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\Pi_t\bigl(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t\bigr),
\label{eq:pi}
\end{equation}
with alignment/snapshot sync $A_t$, quality gating $M_t$, and bundle aggregation $\mathcal{B}_t$.

\begin{proposition}[Compilation is not feature expansion]
\label{prop:compile}
Enlarging $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t$ without a well-defined $\Pi_t$ need not enlarge $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t$ in a decision-relevant way: ungated, stale, or role-incoherent evidence can expand raw span while shrinking usable world quality via $H_t$ and $U_t$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Fix filtrations $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t\subset\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t$ obtained by adding a source $j^\star$ with large lag $\ell_{j^\star,t}$ or $m_{j^\star,t}=1$. Under $M_t$ that fails to exclude $j^\star$, honesty falls: $H_t'=H_t-\Delta$ for some $\Delta>0$. Freshness penalties raise $d_{j^\star,t}$, so $U_t'\le U_t$. Breadth may rise by at most $1/K$, but $\mathrm{WMI}_t'=B_t'U_t'H_t'$ can fall whenever the multiplicative loss in $U_tH_t$ dominates. Since $W^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t)$ inherits the gated objects, the AI-visible $\sigma$-field need not expand in a payoff-relevant way even though the raw $\sigma$-field does. Hence raw span and usable world quality are not monotone in the same direction.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Lag reconstruction bound]
\label{prop:recon}
Under Assumption~1 and bounded increments $\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|\le \bar\delta$, the reconstruction error satisfies
\[
\|\widetilde S_t-S_t\|
\le C_1'\sum_j\omega_j\ell_{j,t}
+C_2\sum_j\omega_j\|\nu_{j,t}\|
+C_3\sum_j\omega_j(1-z_{j,t})
\]
for constants $C_1'=C_1\bar\delta$ independent of the AI model class.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By the Lipschitz property,
$\|h_j(S_t)-h_j(S_{t-\ell_{j,t}})\|\le L_j\sum_{u=t-\ell_{j,t}+1}^{t}\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|\le L_j\bar\delta\,\ell_{j,t}$.
Decompose $\widetilde S_t-S_t$ into the sum of (i) lagged-map bias, (ii) observation noise $\nu_{j,t}$, and (iii) missingness when $z_{j,t}=0$ forces imputation/omission. Triangle inequality and weighting by $\omega_j$ yield the stated bound. Latest-snapshot / TTL / readiness controls are exactly operators that shrink $\ell_{j,t}$, $\|\nu_{j,t}\|$, and $(1-z_{j,t})$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Calibration penalty, MIG, and causal DAG}
If the AI reports confidence $\mathrm{conf}_t$ while $\mathrm{WMI}_t$ is low, define the epistemic calibration penalty
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{ECP}_t=\mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{conf}_t>\bar c\}\,\mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{WMI}_t<w\}.
\label{eq:ecp}
\end{equation}
Thick versus thin information sets differ by conditional mutual information
\begin{equation}
H(R_t\mid I^{\mathrm{thin}}_t)-H(R_t\mid I^{\mathrm{thick}}_t)
=I\!\bigl(R_t;\,I^{\mathrm{thick}}_t\setminus I^{\mathrm{thin}}_t\,\big|\,I^{\mathrm{thin}}_t\bigr).
\label{eq:thickthin}
\end{equation}
Band $k$'s marginal information gain on task $m$ is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{MIG}^{(m)}_{k,t}=I\!\bigl(R^{(m)}_t;\,E_{k,t}\,\big|\,I^{(-k)}_t\bigr).
\label{eq:mig}
\end{equation}
For identification, let $W_t$ be world quality, $A_t$ AI/analysis action quality, $M_t$ market complexity, $O_t$ observable availability shocks, and $C_t$ latent configuration:
\begin{equation}
O_t\to W_t\to A_t,\quad
M_t\to W_t,\quad
M_t\to A_t,\quad
C_t\to (W_t,A_t).
\label{eq:dag}
\end{equation}
Without controlling $M_t$ and $C_t$, ``thicker worlds correlate with better AI outcomes'' is not causal. Availability shocks $O_t$ are the preferred quasi-exogenous lever.

\subsection{Bayesian abstention}
With action set $\mathcal{A}=\{\mathrm{bullish},\mathrm{bearish},\mathrm{neutral},\mathrm{abstain}\}$ and loss $\ell(a,R_t)$,
\begin{equation}
a^\star_t=\arg\min_{a\in\mathcal{A}}\,\mathbb{E}\bigl[\ell(a,R_t)\mid W_t\bigr].
\label{eq:bayes}
\end{equation}
Abstain whenever every non-abstain action has expected loss above a world-dependent cost $c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)$:
\begin{equation}
\min_{a\neq\mathrm{abstain}}\mathbb{E}\bigl[\ell(a,R_t)\mid W_t\bigr]>c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t).
\label{eq:abstain}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[World-conditional abstention]
\label{prop:abstain}
Suppose $\ell(\mathrm{abstain},R_t)\equiv c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)$ and $\ell(a,R_t)\ge 0$ for $a\neq\mathrm{abstain}$. If for every $a\neq\mathrm{abstain}$ one has $\mathbb{E}[\ell(a,R_t)\mid W_t]>c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)$, then $a^\star_t=\mathrm{abstain}$. Moreover, if expected non-abstain loss is weakly decreasing in $\mathrm{WMI}_t$ (better worlds improve feasible actions) and $c_{\mathrm{abs}}$ is weakly decreasing in $\mathrm{WMI}_t$, then the abstention region is a lower set in $\mathrm{WMI}_t$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The first claim is immediate from \eqref{eq:bayes}--\eqref{eq:abstain}: abstain uniquely minimizes expected loss. For the second, let $\underline L(W)=\min_{a\neq\mathrm{abstain}}\mathbb{E}[\ell(a,R_t)\mid W]$. If $\underline L$ and $c_{\mathrm{abs}}$ are both weakly decreasing in $\mathrm{WMI}$, the set $\{W:\underline L(W)>c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W)\}$ is a lower contour set whenever $\underline L-c_{\mathrm{abs}}$ crosses zero at most once from above---the empirically relevant case when worse worlds inflate action loss faster than they inflate the abstention cost. Threshold rules $\mathrm{ACWMI}_t<\bar a$ implement this lower-set structure after IS freezing of $\bar a$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{ACWMI and explanation metrics}
Regime-conditional ACWMI extends \eqref{eq:wmi} to a weighted geometric mean of
$x_t=(B^{\mathrm{hier}}_t,U_t,H^{\mathrm{cont}}_t,S_t,C_t)$ with regime weights $\gamma(r_t)\in\mathbb{R}^5_{+}$:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{ACWMI}_t
=\exp\!\left(
\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{5}\gamma_i(r_t)\log x_{i,t}}{\sum_{i=1}^{5}\gamma_i(r_t)}
\right),
\label{eq:acwmi}
\end{equation}
where $S_t$ is signal integrity and $C_t$ cross-evidence consistency. Explanation quality uses
\begin{align}
\mathrm{EAR}_t&=\frac{\#\{\text{claims bound to evidence}\}}{\#\{\text{claims}\}},
\label{eq:ear}\\
\mathrm{UCR}_t&=1-\mathrm{EAR}_t,
\label{eq:ucr}\\
\mathrm{EV}_t&=\frac{d(\Phi_t,\Phi_{t-1})}{1+d(W_t,W_{t-1})},
\label{eq:ev}
\end{align}
with explanation object $\Phi_t$ and distance $d(\cdot,\cdot)$. Thin and thick worlds also differ in explanation sets:
\begin{equation}
\Phi^{\mathrm{thin}}_t\neq\Phi^{\mathrm{thick}}_t,\qquad
\Pr(\phi^\star_t\in\Phi^{\mathrm{thick}}_t)>\Pr(\phi^\star_t\in\Phi^{\mathrm{thin}}_t).
\label{eq:phi}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[ACWMI factor monotonicity]
\label{prop:acwmi}
Fix $\gamma(r_t)\gg 0$ and $x_{i,t}\in(0,1]$. Then $\partial\mathrm{ACWMI}_t/\partial x_{k,t}>0$ for each factor $k$, and $\log\mathrm{ACWMI}_t$ is concave in $\log x_t$. Consequently, a proportional deterioration in any single factor (e.g., honesty under contamination) cannot be offset one-for-one by raising another factor with the same weight.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let $w_i=\gamma_i/\sum_j\gamma_j$. Then $\log\mathrm{ACWMI}=\sum_i w_i\log x_i$, so $\partial\mathrm{ACWMI}/\partial x_k=\mathrm{ACWMI}\cdot w_k/x_k>0$. Concavity in $\log x$ is immediate. Equal-weight compensation would require $\Delta\log x_k=-\Delta\log x_\ell$, but under contamination shocks that hit $H$ while $B$ rises only by $O(1/K)$, the net $\sum w_i\Delta\log x_i$ is negative whenever $w_H|\Delta\log H|>w_B\Delta\log B$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Leave-one-band-out as economic MIG]
\label{prop:lobo}
Let $V(I)$ be the OOS certainty-equivalent value of a fixed action rule given information set $I$. Define $\widehat{\mathrm{MIG}}_k=V(I)-V(I\setminus E_k)$. If $V$ is monotone in Blackwell informativeness and band $k$ is not redundant given $I\setminus E_k$, then $\widehat{\mathrm{MIG}}_k>0$. Under the maintained PIT construction, setting band $k$ to \texttt{missing} implements $I\setminus E_k$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Blackwell monotonicity gives $V(I)\ge V(I\setminus E_k)$. Strict inequality holds when $E_k$ has unique predictive content for the payoff-relevant state under the fixed rule (non-redundancy). The empirical LOBO exercise replaces band status $k$ by \texttt{missing} before recomputing $B^{\mathrm{hier}},U,H$ and ACWMI, which is exactly the information-set deletion $I\setminus E_k$ in the laboratory.
\end{proof}

\section{The EvoQuant laboratory as measurement instrument}
\label{sec:lab}

EvoQuant is a modular crypto market laboratory: data-layer collectors ingest exchange, macro, on-chain, options, tokenomics, news, and alternative series into split SQLite stores; a logic-layer DAG computes indicators, readiness, and AI market context; a FastAPI service exposes quality-tagged bundles. For this paper, the laboratory supplies:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item multi-band history tables with observation timestamps (and macro \texttt{available\_at} vintages);
\item band readiness / WMI / ACWMI implementations;
\item \texttt{BandPITService} / \texttt{time\_slice} for point-in-time reconstruction;
\item \texttt{load\_availability\_shocks} over \texttt{collection\_runs} as an $O_t$ query surface;
\item configurable thresholds \texttt{WMI\_ABSTAIN\_THRESHOLD}, \texttt{ACWMI\_ABSTAIN\_THRESHOLD}, \texttt{WORLD\_MODEL\_INDEX\_MODE}.
\end{itemize}
We emphasize the epistemological order: theory first, laboratory second.

\section{Data and real multi-band PIT archive}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Populated archive}
We bootstrap the project's split SQLite store (OKX-reachable exchange path in the build environment):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{Exchange:} $\sim$9.4k OKX klines/merged daily bars for 10 symbols, 2025-06-30 to 2026-08-03; $\sim$2.4k funding rows.
\item \textbf{Macro:} $\sim$81k vintaged points with \texttt{available\_at}, spanning decades through 2026-08-03.
\item \textbf{Alternative:} $\sim$89k points from 2017 onward.
\item \textbf{News / on-chain / options / tokenomics:} populated but mostly right-censored to collection day in the current archive.
\item \textbf{Analytics snapshots:} historically sparse; multi-band PIT is therefore built from raw history tables.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{PIT panel construction}
For each calendar date $t$ and asset $i$, band status $\in\{\mathrm{ready},\mathrm{limited},\mathrm{missing}\}$ is inferred from the latest observation time $\le t$ and band-specific freshness thresholds aligned to production \texttt{BandPITService}. We compute $B^{\mathrm{hier}}_t$, $U_t$, $H^{\mathrm{cont}}_t$, production WMI, and a world-only ACWMI placeholder; mechanism engines later refine $S_t$ and $C_t$ from pre-$t$ returns. Durable historical bands are $\{\mathrm{exchange},\mathrm{macro},\mathrm{alternative}\}$. Yahoo daily close-to-close returns provide payoffs. The resulting panel has 400 days $\times$ 10 assets $=4{,}000$ asset-days.

\subsection{Band readiness rates}
Empirical ready rates on the panel are approximately: exchange $0.998$, macro $1.000$, alternative $1.000$, news $0.008$, on-chain/options/tokenomics/event-calendar $\approx 0$. Identification on durable bands is therefore the credible core; right-censored bands enter only as agenda items for continuous collection.

\section{Empirical design}
\label{sec:design}

\paragraph{Sample split.}
Chronological IS/OOS split at 2026-01-16 (200/200 days).

\paragraph{Frozen thresholds.}
AC thresholds are frozen on IS by Sharpe maximization subject to abstain rate $\in[5\%,55\%]$, yielding $\mathrm{ACWMI}<0.35$ or $C<0.35$. The production WMI threshold $0.2$ is never tuned on OOS.

\paragraph{Policies.}
Always-long; momentum; thick-ungated mechanism signals; simple outage; cascade; WMI; ACWMI (IS-frozen).

\paragraph{Economic value.}
Annualized return and volatility, Sharpe ratio, CRRA certainty equivalent with $\gamma=2$, and maximum drawdown. Positions are equal-weight across tradeable names on active days; abstention earns cash (zero return).

\paragraph{Inference.}
OOS contrasts use circular block bootstrap on daily portfolio PnL ($n_{\mathrm{boot}}=999$, block length 5 days). We report two-sided $p$-values for $\Delta$Sharpe and $\Delta$CE under $H_0:\Delta=0$.

\paragraph{Identification designs.}
(i) Thin vs thick worlds using real PIT band statuses; (ii) leave-one-band-out on durable bands as economic MIG (Proposition~\ref{prop:lobo}); (iii) scarce-world event study using bottom $B^{\mathrm{hier}}$ quintile when natural hard outages are rare inside continuous OKX backfill.

\subsection{Mechanism signals (opened)}
\label{sec:mech}
Directional actions are \emph{not} a latent model. On pre-$t$ returns only, production calculators emit:
\begin{align*}
S_t&=\mathrm{clip}\!\big(\mathrm{hl\_factor}\cdot(1-0.7\,\mathrm{crowd}_t)\cdot(0.35+0.65\,\mathrm{surprise}_t)\big),\\
C_t&=\text{pairwise sign-agreement among }\{\mathrm{mom5},\mathrm{flow},-\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{casc}>0.55},-\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{sys}>55}\},
\end{align*}
with $\mathrm{cascade\_p}$ from \texttt{LiquidationCascadeCalculator}, systemic score from \texttt{ContagionRiskCalculator}, half-life/crowding/surprise from \texttt{AlphaDecayCalculator}, flow/VPIN from \texttt{FlowDecompositionCalculator}, and regime from \texttt{RegimeClassifier}. The action rule is deterministic:
\begin{align*}
\text{(R1)}&\quad \mathrm{crisis}\ \vee\ \mathrm{cascade\_p}\ge 0.60 \Rightarrow \mathrm{signal}=-1,\\
\text{(R2)}&\quad \mathrm{trend}\ \wedge\ \mathrm{mom5}>0\ \wedge\ \mathrm{cascade\_p}<0.45 \Rightarrow \mathrm{signal}=+1,\\
\text{(R3)}&\quad \text{else }\mathrm{sign}(\mathrm{mom5})\ \text{(or 0)}.
\end{align*}
Component definitions are exported in \texttt{table\_mechanism\_definition.csv}; regime-level averages in \texttt{table\_mechanism\_by\_regime.csv}.

\section{Main results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Thick real PIT worlds dominate thin worlds}
Table~\ref{tab:thin} reports OOS economic value. Exchange-only thin worlds deliver Sharpe $\approx 0$ and CE $-0.011$. \subsection{Mechanism audit}
Opening the signal shows a stark composition: $3546/4000$ asset-days are classified \texttt{crisis} with mean $\mathrm{cascade\_p}=0.87$ and $\mathrm{signal}=-1$ always; only $454$ days are \texttt{range}. Thick-ungated CE therefore largely reflects the R1 short branch under cascade pressure, not an opaque learner. This is a feature for auditability and a caveat for external validity: longer non-crisis spans are required before claiming regime-balanced skill.

\subsection{Interpretation}
Five implications follow. First, compilation quality has first-order economic content (Propositions~\ref{prop:compile}--\ref{prop:lobo}). Second, LOBO MIG is strongest for alternative ($p=0.04$) and marginal for macro ($p=0.08$). Third, selective prediction requires thresholds frozen to archive support. Fourth, mechanism actions are an auditable R1--R3 rule. Fifth, 200-day OOS bootstrap CIs remain wide---point estimates and significance must be separated separately.

\section{Robustness, threats, and the JF/RFS agenda}
\label{sec:robust}

\paragraph{Right-censored bands.}
News/on-chain/options/tokenomics lack durable history in the current archive. Continuous multi-year collection is required before claiming full thirteen-band identification.

\paragraph{Natural outages.}
Hard institutional outages are rare inside a continuous OKX backfill. Logged \texttt{collection\_runs} failures and planted availability shocks are the path to sharper $O_t$ designs.

\paragraph{Snapshot density.}
Analytics snapshots remain sparse historically; PIT currently relies on raw history. Daily readiness/AI-context snapshots will enable pure \texttt{time\_slice} replay.

\paragraph{External validity.}
The panel covers ten liquid names. Cross-section expansion and longer calendars are required for external validity.

\paragraph{Multiple testing.}
Policy comparisons are pre-registered by the frozen-threshold protocol, but a final submission should report additional loss functions and transaction-cost adjusted CE.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

Information-set compilation is a first-order object in cryptocurrency markets. This paper restores a complete RCA-WM / ACWMI theory---epistemic observations, lag bounds, compilation operators, calibration penalties, MIG, identification DAG, abstention, and explanation metrics---and instantiates it on a real multi-band PIT archive. Thick worlds dominate thin worlds; durable bands have large leave-one-out economic value; selective ACWMI gating is implementable under frozen thresholds. EvoQuant is the laboratory. The remaining path to a final top-finance submission is institutional: deepen vintaged histories, log outages, snapshot daily, and expand the cross-section---without abandoning the formal apparatus.
