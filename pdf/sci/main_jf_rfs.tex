\begin{abstract}
Conditional asset pricing treats the investor's information set $I_t$ as primitive. In cryptocurrency markets, asynchronous and intermittently missing evidence makes \emph{compilation of} $I_t$ first-order. We develop a theory of information-set compilation whose core objects are epistemic observations, a compilation operator between raw and usable filtrations, reconstruction bounds, and an SDF interface under which pricing statements are constrained to the compiled $\sigma$-field; leave-one-band-out (LOBO) value then decomposes into content and gating channels. Empirically, on a point-in-time panel with durable bands $\{\mathrm{exchange},\mathrm{macro},\mathrm{alternative}\}$, vintage-safe macro and stablecoin-flow \emph{content} enters a transparent action rule under a previous-close clock. The pre-specified contrast---mechanism versus same-input momentum---is positive out of sample ($\Delta$CE $0.334$, $p=0.034$) and remains significant after 10--25\,bps costs as a \emph{relative} value gap, even though absolute CE is cost-fragile. LOBO shows losses operate mainly through content; VIX/DXY conjunction tests, joint deletion, and a scrambled-content placebo discipline the design. A 2017--2026 audit finds no unconditional edge over momentum when band archives are absent, so gains load on compiled content rather than a hidden return predictor. World-quality indices and selective prediction remain part of the theory but are secondary in this sparse archive.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Modern asset pricing conditions on an information set $I_t$ \citep{fama1993,cochrane2005,gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021,harvey2016}. The literature's discipline has focused on how $I_t$ is \emph{used}---for discount factors, return prediction, and machine-learning pricing kernels---not on how $I_t$ is \emph{compiled} from asynchronous, quality-heterogeneous evidence. In liquid equities, the omission is often second-order: prices and accounting vintages are relatively synchronized. In cryptocurrency markets, compilation is first-order. Venue fragmentation, perpetual leverage, options walls, token unlocks, on-chain flows, and macro liquidity can map the same price path into incompatible states \citep{makarov2020,liu2022}.

This paper makes three contributions, ordered by what the present archive can identify.

\paragraph{Theory (core apparatus).}
We formalize information-set compilation for AI-consumable market worlds (RCA-WM). The primitive object is an epistemic observation $O_{j,t}=(x,\tau,q,g,r)$, not a bare feature. A compilation operator $\Pi_t$ maps $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t$ into a usable filtration $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t$. Asynchronous lags induce a reconstruction bound with delay, noise, and missingness. The SDF interface states that implementable conditional pricing is constrained to $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t$, so alpha measured against the raw filtration confounds skill with compilation loss. LOBO economic value decomposes into a \emph{content} channel and a \emph{gating} channel (Propositions~\ref{prop:compile}--\ref{prop:lobo}). World-model indices (WMI/ACWMI) and world-conditional abstention complete the apparatus; in the present sparse archive they are maintained as theory objects and secondary empirics, not as the headline test.

\paragraph{Measurement instrument.}
A reproducible multi-band laboratory supplies vintage-aware history, readiness scoring, point-in-time queries, and quality thresholds. The instrument implements the theory; it does not invent it.

\paragraph{Identification (headline empirics).}
On a real PIT panel under a previous-close clock, vintaged macro and stablecoin-flow \emph{content} enter a transparent action rule that shares all return-based inputs with momentum. The pre-specified OOS contrast is mechanism $-$ momentum. We discipline it with content/gating LOBO, VIX/DXY conjunction deletions, joint band deletion, a scrambled-content placebo, costs/funding, a reality-check correction, and a 2017--2026 audit in which band content is absent. What we claim is relative economic value of compiled band content in this design---not unconditional trading alpha, not full thirteen-band identification, and not a completed selective-prediction horse-race.

\section{Related literature}
\label{sec:lit}

\paragraph{Information sets in asset pricing.}
Classic and modern pricing frameworks treat $I_t$ as given \citep{cochrane2005,fama1993}. Machine-learning asset pricing expands the feature span inside $I_t$ \citep{gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021} but typically assumes synchronized, clean panels. We study the prior step: how asynchronous evidence is compiled into an AI-visible world.

\paragraph{Cryptocurrency market structure.}
Crypto markets exhibit fragmentation and microstructure frictions \citep{makarov2020} and distinctive risk factors \citep{liu2022}. These facts motivate multi-band worlds rather than price-only predictors.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and abstention.}
Selective classification studies when a learner should refuse to predict \citep{elyaniv2010,geifman2017}. When the world is thin or dishonest, the optimal action may be abstention. Our Bayesian rule links abstention costs to world-model quality, connecting selective prediction to information-set support rather than to model confidence alone.

\paragraph{Information choice and rational inattention.}
The information-choice literature endogenizes what investors learn \citep{sims2003,veldkamp2011}. We study the complementary engineering margin: given collected evidence, how the usable information set is assembled, and what is lost when assembly fails.

\paragraph{Measurement error and vintages.}
Macro data are vintage-dependent \citep{croushore2001}; investor attention proxies show that information arrival itself is priced \citep{da2011}. Crypto bands add freshness and missingness on top of vintages. Our reconstruction bound and PIT construction treat measurement timing as first-class.

\paragraph{Inference discipline.}
Data-snooping corrections \citep{white2000,hansen2005,harvey2016} and dependent-data bootstrap methods \citep{politis1994,ledoit2008} discipline our out-of-sample comparisons: all contrasts carry block-bootstrap $p$-values, block-length sensitivity, and a reality-check correction across the policy menu.

\section{Theory}
\label{sec:theory}

\noindent\textit{Reading guide.} The core theory is the compilation chain: epistemic observations $\rightarrow$ operator $\Pi_t$ $\rightarrow$ reconstruction bounds $\rightarrow$ SDF interface on $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t$ $\rightarrow$ LOBO content/gating. WMI/ACWMI and abstention belong to the same apparatus but are \emph{not} the margin identified by the headline empirics in Section~\ref{sec:results}; readers focused on the present archive may treat Sections~\ref{sec:wmi} and the abstention/ACWMI paragraphs as completing definitions for denser worlds.

\subsection{World-model quality: breadth, stability, honesty}
\label{sec:wmi}

\begin{definition}[Breadth]
Let $K$ be the number of critical evidence bands at date $t$, and let $a_{k,t}\in\{0,1\}$ indicate whether band $k$ meets an AI-usable threshold. Breadth is
\begin{equation}
B_t=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^{K}a_{k,t}.
\label{eq:breadth}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Stability]
Let $d_{j,t}$ be a freshness/TTL penalty for source $j$ with importance weight $\omega_j\ge 0$. Stability is
\begin{equation}
U_t=\exp\!\left(-\sum_{j=1}^{J}\omega_j d_{j,t}\right).
\label{eq:stability}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Honesty]
Let $m_{j,t}=1$ if source $j$ should not enter the AI main view but is incorrectly injected. Honesty is
\begin{equation}
H_t=1-\frac{1}{J}\sum_{j=1}^{J}m_{j,t}.
\label{eq:honesty}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

The production baseline World Model Index used by the laboratory is the product form
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t\times U_t\times H_t.
\label{eq:wmi}
\end{equation}

For hierarchical readiness we use a convex combination of domain-, band-, and asset-level readiness ratios,
\begin{equation}
B^{\mathrm{hier}}_t=0.25\,B^{\mathrm{dom}}_t+0.35\,B^{\mathrm{band}}_t+0.40\,B^{\mathrm{asset}}_t,
\label{eq:bhier}
\end{equation}
and a continuous honesty score that rewards exclusion of contaminated evidence and penalizes partial contamination,
\begin{equation}
H^{\mathrm{cont}}_t=\exp(-2c_t)\max\!\bigl(0,\,1-0.5(1-e_t)\bigr),
\label{eq:hcont}
\end{equation}
where $e_t$ is the ready-share and $c_t$ a contamination share inferred from limited/missing bands.

\subsection{Epistemic observations}
\begin{definition}[Epistemic observation]
An AI-consumable market observation is
\begin{equation}
O_{j,t}=\bigl(x_{j,t},\,\tau_{j,t},\,q_{j,t},\,g_{j,t},\,r_{j,t}\bigr),
\label{eq:obs}
\end{equation}
with value $x$, latest-available time $\tau$, quality state $q$, main-view gate $g$, and semantic role $r$.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
The AI consumes a set of epistemic objects, not a flat feature matrix. Two numerically identical $x$ values with different $(\tau,q,g,r)$ are different world-model objects.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Asynchronous state space and lag error}
Let latent states evolve as
\begin{equation}
S_{t+1}=F(S_t,\eta_{t+1}).
\label{eq:state}
\end{equation}
Source $j$ observes a lagged map
\begin{equation}
X^{\mathrm{obs}}_{j,t}=h_j(S_{t-\ell_{j,t}})+\nu_{j,t},\qquad \ell_{j,t}\ge 0.
\label{eq:lagobs}
\end{equation}
\begin{assumption}[Lipschitz observation maps]
Each $h_j$ is Lipschitz with constant $L_j<\infty$.
\end{assumption}
Then
\begin{equation}
\bigl\|h_j(S_t)-h_j(S_{t-\ell_{j,t}})\bigr\|
\le L_j\sum_{u=t-\ell_{j,t}+1}^{t}\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|.
\label{eq:lipschitz}
\end{equation}
A world-model reconstruction $\widetilde S_t$ therefore obeys a bound with delay, noise, and missingness terms:
\begin{equation}
\|\widetilde S_t-S_t\|
\le C_1\sum_j\omega_j\ell_{j,t}
+C_2\sum_j\omega_j\|\nu_{j,t}\|
+C_3\sum_j\omega_j(1-z_{j,t}),
\label{eq:recon}
\end{equation}
where $z_{j,t}$ indicates availability. Latest-snapshot mechanisms, freshness/TTL rules, and readiness gates target these three terms.

\subsection{Filters and the compilation operator}
Define the raw and AI-visible filtrations
\begin{align}
\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t&=\sigma\bigl(\{X^{\mathrm{obs}}_{j,\tau}: j\le J,\ \tau\le t\}\bigr),
\label{eq:fraw}\\
\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t&=\sigma\bigl(W^{\mathrm{AI}}_t,\,D_t\bigr),
\label{eq:fai}
\end{align}
where $W^{\mathrm{AI}}_t$ is the main view and $D_t$ the diagnostic view. Compilation is the composition
\begin{equation}
\Pi_t=\mathcal{B}_t\circ M_t\circ A_t,\qquad
W^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\Pi_t\bigl(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t\bigr),
\label{eq:pi}
\end{equation}
with alignment/snapshot sync $A_t$, quality gating $M_t$, and bundle aggregation $\mathcal{B}_t$.

\begin{proposition}[Compilation is not feature expansion]
\label{prop:compile}
Enlarging $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t$ without a well-defined $\Pi_t$ need not enlarge $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t$ in a decision-relevant way: ungated, stale, or role-incoherent evidence can expand raw span while shrinking usable world quality via $H_t$ and $U_t$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Fix filtrations $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t\subset\widetilde{\mathcal{F}}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t$ obtained by adding a source $j^\star$ with large lag $\ell_{j^\star,t}$ or $m_{j^\star,t}=1$. Under $M_t$ that fails to exclude $j^\star$, honesty falls: $H_t'=H_t-\Delta$ for some $\Delta>0$. Freshness penalties raise $d_{j^\star,t}$, so $U_t'\le U_t$. Breadth may rise by at most $1/K$, but $\mathrm{WMI}_t'=B_t'U_t'H_t'$ can fall whenever the multiplicative loss in $U_tH_t$ dominates. Since $W^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t)$ inherits the gated objects, the AI-visible $\sigma$-field need not expand in a payoff-relevant way even though the raw $\sigma$-field does. Hence raw span and usable world quality are not monotone in the same direction.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[SDF interface: compilation constrains pricing]
\label{prop:sdf}
Let $m_{t+1}$ be a candidate stochastic discount factor used by an agent whose usable conditioning information is $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\sigma(\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t))$. Any implementable conditional pricing statement is of the form
$\mathbb{E}[m_{t+1}R_{t+1}\mid\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t]=1$.
If a researcher evaluates the same agent against the finer set $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t$, the measured conditional pricing error contains a \emph{compilation wedge}
\[
w_t \;=\; \mathbb{E}\bigl[m_{t+1}R_{t+1}\mid\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t\bigr]-\mathbb{E}\bigl[m_{t+1}R_{t+1}\mid\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t\bigr],
\]
which is zero for all payoffs if and only if $\mathbb{E}[m_{t+1}R_{t+1}\mid\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t]$ is $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t$-measurable, i.e.\ compilation loses no pricing-relevant information. Alpha measured against the raw filtration therefore confounds skill with compilation loss.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
By the law of iterated expectations, $\mathbb{E}[w_t\mid\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t]=0$: the wedge is conditionally mean-zero but not pointwise zero unless the raw-conditional moment is measurable with respect to the compiled $\sigma$-field. When $\Pi_t$ discards a band $E_k$ with $\mathrm{MIG}_{k,t}>0$ for the pricing task, there exists a payoff whose raw-conditional expectation varies with $E_{k,t}$ while its compiled-conditional expectation does not, so $w_t\neq 0$ on a positive-probability set. Full details in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Lag reconstruction bound]
\label{prop:recon}
Under Assumption~1 and bounded increments $\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|\le \bar\delta$, the reconstruction error satisfies
\[
\|\widetilde S_t-S_t\|
\le C_1'\sum_j\omega_j\ell_{j,t}
+C_2\sum_j\omega_j\|\nu_{j,t}\|
+C_3\sum_j\omega_j(1-z_{j,t})
\]
for constants $C_1'=C_1\bar\delta$ independent of the AI model class.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By the Lipschitz property,
$\|h_j(S_t)-h_j(S_{t-\ell_{j,t}})\|\le L_j\sum_{u=t-\ell_{j,t}+1}^{t}\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|\le L_j\bar\delta\,\ell_{j,t}$.
Decompose $\widetilde S_t-S_t$ into the sum of (i) lagged-map bias, (ii) observation noise $\nu_{j,t}$, and (iii) missingness when $z_{j,t}=0$ forces imputation/omission. Triangle inequality and weighting by $\omega_j$ yield the stated bound. Latest-snapshot / TTL / readiness controls are exactly operators that shrink $\ell_{j,t}$, $\|\nu_{j,t}\|$, and $(1-z_{j,t})$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Calibration penalty, MIG, and causal DAG}
If the AI reports confidence $\mathrm{conf}_t$ while $\mathrm{WMI}_t$ is low, define the epistemic calibration penalty
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{ECP}_t=\mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{conf}_t>\bar c\}\,\mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{WMI}_t<w\}.
\label{eq:ecp}
\end{equation}
Thick versus thin information sets differ by conditional mutual information
\begin{equation}
H(R_t\mid I^{\mathrm{thin}}_t)-H(R_t\mid I^{\mathrm{thick}}_t)
=I\!\bigl(R_t;\,I^{\mathrm{thick}}_t\setminus I^{\mathrm{thin}}_t\,\big|\,I^{\mathrm{thin}}_t\bigr).
\label{eq:thickthin}
\end{equation}
Band $k$'s marginal information gain on task $m$ is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{MIG}^{(m)}_{k,t}=I\!\bigl(R^{(m)}_t;\,E_{k,t}\,\big|\,I^{(-k)}_t\bigr).
\label{eq:mig}
\end{equation}
For identification, let $W_t$ be world quality, $A_t$ AI/analysis action quality, $M_t$ market complexity, $O_t$ observable availability shocks, and $C_t$ latent configuration:
\begin{equation}
O_t\to W_t\to A_t,\quad
M_t\to W_t,\quad
M_t\to A_t,\quad
C_t\to (W_t,A_t).
\label{eq:dag}
\end{equation}
Without controlling $M_t$ and $C_t$, ``thicker worlds correlate with better AI outcomes'' is not causal. Availability shocks $O_t$ are the preferred quasi-exogenous lever.

\subsection{Bayesian abstention}
With action set $\mathcal{A}=\{\mathrm{bullish},\mathrm{bearish},\mathrm{neutral},\mathrm{abstain}\}$ and loss $\ell(a,R_t)$,
\begin{equation}
a^\star_t=\arg\min_{a\in\mathcal{A}}\,\mathbb{E}\bigl[\ell(a,R_t)\mid W_t\bigr].
\label{eq:bayes}
\end{equation}
Abstain whenever every non-abstain action has expected loss above a world-dependent cost $c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)$:
\begin{equation}
\min_{a\neq\mathrm{abstain}}\mathbb{E}\bigl[\ell(a,R_t)\mid W_t\bigr]>c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t).
\label{eq:abstain}
\end{equation}

\begin{assumption}[Single crossing]
\label{ass:singlecross}
Let $\underline L(W)=\min_{a\neq\mathrm{abstain}}\mathbb{E}[\ell(a,R_t)\mid W]$. The function $\underline L(W)-c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W)$, viewed along the $\mathrm{WMI}$ ordering of worlds, crosses zero at most once and from above.
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}[World-conditional abstention]
\label{prop:abstain}
Suppose $\ell(\mathrm{abstain},R_t)\equiv c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)$ and $\ell(a,R_t)\ge 0$ for $a\neq\mathrm{abstain}$. If for every $a\neq\mathrm{abstain}$ one has $\mathbb{E}[\ell(a,R_t)\mid W_t]>c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)$, then $a^\star_t=\mathrm{abstain}$. Moreover, under Assumption~\ref{ass:singlecross}, with expected non-abstain loss and $c_{\mathrm{abs}}$ weakly decreasing in $\mathrm{WMI}_t$, the abstention region is a lower set in $\mathrm{WMI}_t$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The first claim is immediate from \eqref{eq:bayes}--\eqref{eq:abstain}: abstain uniquely minimizes expected loss. For the second, Assumption~\ref{ass:singlecross} makes $\{W:\underline L(W)>c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W)\}$ a lower contour set in $\mathrm{WMI}$. Threshold rules $\mathrm{ACWMI}_t<\bar a$ implement this lower-set structure after IS freezing of $\bar a$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{ACWMI and explanation metrics}
Regime-conditional ACWMI extends \eqref{eq:wmi} to a weighted geometric mean of
$x_t=(B^{\mathrm{hier}}_t,U_t,H^{\mathrm{cont}}_t,S_t,C_t)$ with regime weights $\gamma(r_t)\in\mathbb{R}^5_{+}$:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{ACWMI}_t
=\exp\!\left(
\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{5}\gamma_i(r_t)\log x_{i,t}}{\sum_{i=1}^{5}\gamma_i(r_t)}
\right),
\label{eq:acwmi}
\end{equation}
where $S_t$ is signal integrity and $C_t$ cross-evidence consistency. Explanation quality uses
\begin{align}
\mathrm{EAR}_t&=\frac{\#\{\text{claims bound to evidence}\}}{\#\{\text{claims}\}},
\label{eq:ear}\\
\mathrm{UCR}_t&=1-\mathrm{EAR}_t,
\label{eq:ucr}\\
\mathrm{EV}_t&=\frac{d(\Phi_t,\Phi_{t-1})}{1+d(W_t,W_{t-1})},
\label{eq:ev}
\end{align}
with explanation object $\Phi_t$ and distance $d(\cdot,\cdot)$. Thin and thick worlds also differ in explanation sets:
\begin{equation}
\Phi^{\mathrm{thin}}_t\neq\Phi^{\mathrm{thick}}_t,\qquad
\Pr(\phi^\star_t\in\Phi^{\mathrm{thick}}_t)>\Pr(\phi^\star_t\in\Phi^{\mathrm{thin}}_t).
\label{eq:phi}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[ACWMI factor monotonicity]
\label{prop:acwmi}
Fix $\gamma(r_t)\gg 0$ and $x_{i,t}\in(0,1]$. Then $\partial\mathrm{ACWMI}_t/\partial x_{k,t}>0$ for each factor $k$, and $\log\mathrm{ACWMI}_t$ is concave in $\log x_t$. Consequently, a proportional deterioration in any single factor (e.g., honesty under contamination) cannot be offset one-for-one by raising another factor with the same weight.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let $w_i=\gamma_i/\sum_j\gamma_j$. Then $\log\mathrm{ACWMI}=\sum_i w_i\log x_i$, so $\partial\mathrm{ACWMI}/\partial x_k=\mathrm{ACWMI}\cdot w_k/x_k>0$. Concavity in $\log x$ is immediate. Equal-weight compensation would require $\Delta\log x_k=-\Delta\log x_\ell$, but under contamination shocks that hit $H$ while $B$ rises only by $O(1/K)$, the net $\sum w_i\Delta\log x_i$ is negative whenever $w_H|\Delta\log H|>w_B\Delta\log B$.
\end{proof}

\begin{assumption}[Non-redundancy]
\label{ass:nonredundant}
Band $k$'s evidence $E_{k,t}$ has predictive content for the payoff-relevant state that is not measurable with respect to $\sigma(I_t\setminus E_{k,t})$, i.e.\ $\mathrm{MIG}_{k,t}>0$ on a positive-probability set.
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}[Leave-one-band-out as economic MIG, with channel decomposition]
\label{prop:lobo}
Let $V(I)$ be the OOS certainty-equivalent value of a fixed action rule given information set $I$, and define $\widehat{\mathrm{MIG}}_k=V(I)-V(I\setminus E_k)$. Band $k$ enters decisions through two channels: (i) a \emph{content} channel, where $E_{k,t}$ changes the direction or activation of the action itself; and (ii) a \emph{gating} channel, where band availability changes $B,U,H$ and hence the abstention decision. Writing $V$ as a function of both channels,
\[
\widehat{\mathrm{MIG}}_k=\underbrace{\bigl[V(I)-V(I^{\,-k\,\mathrm{content}})\bigr]}_{\text{content}}
+\underbrace{\bigl[V(I^{\,-k\,\mathrm{content}})-V(I\setminus E_k)\bigr]}_{\text{gating, evaluated after content deletion}}.
\]
If $V$ is monotone in Blackwell informativeness and Assumption~\ref{ass:nonredundant} holds, then $\widehat{\mathrm{MIG}}_k>0$. Under the maintained PIT construction, setting band $k$'s status to \texttt{missing} implements the gating deletion, and forcing the band's content signal to its uninformative value implements the content deletion; applying both implements $I\setminus E_k$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Blackwell monotonicity gives $V(I)\ge V(I\setminus E_k)$, with strict inequality under Assumption~\ref{ass:nonredundant} because a fixed rule that uses $E_{k,t}$ in its action map loses conditional information when the band is deleted. The decomposition is an exact telescoping identity. The empirical LOBO exercise implements the two deletions separately (content: band tilt forced to zero; gating: band status \texttt{missing} before recomputing $B^{\mathrm{hier}},U,H$, ACWMI) and jointly, which identifies each channel's contribution.
\end{proof}

\section{The measurement instrument}
\label{sec:lab}

The empirical instrument is a modular multi-band collection laboratory: data-layer collectors ingest exchange, macro, on-chain, options, tokenomics, news, and alternative series into vintage-aware history stores; a logic layer computes indicators, band readiness, and world-quality indices; a service layer exposes quality-tagged bundles. For this paper, the instrument supplies (i) multi-band history tables with observation timestamps and macro \texttt{available\_at} vintages; (ii) band readiness and WMI/ACWMI implementations; (iii) point-in-time band-status reconstruction; (iv) an availability-shock query surface over collection logs; and (v) configurable abstention thresholds. We emphasize the epistemological order: theory first, instrument second.

\section{Data and real multi-band PIT archive}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Populated archive}
We bootstrap the project's split SQLite store (OKX-reachable exchange path in the build environment):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \textbf{Exchange:} $\sim$9.4k OKX klines/merged daily bars for 10 symbols, 2025-06-30 to 2026-08-03; $\sim$2.4k funding rows.
\item \textbf{Macro:} $\sim$81k vintaged points with \texttt{available\_at}, spanning decades through 2026-08-03.
\item \textbf{Alternative:} $\sim$89k points from 2017 onward.
\item \textbf{News / on-chain / options / tokenomics:} populated but mostly right-censored to collection day in the current archive.
\item \textbf{Analytics snapshots:} historically sparse; multi-band PIT is therefore built from raw history tables.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{PIT panel construction}
\paragraph{Timing protocol (decision at previous close).}
For payoff calendar day $t$ (Yahoo close-to-close return $r_t$), all band statuses and ages are evaluated at decision clock $(t-1)\,23{:}59$. No same-day close information enters the information set that selects the position earning $r_t$. Band-content tilts are likewise constructed from vintages with \texttt{available\_at}$\le t$ and then lagged one day. This protocol is versioned in \texttt{experiment\_config.json} and recorded on each panel row as \texttt{decision\_asof}.

\paragraph{Construction path and migration disclosure.}
When domain SQLite history is populated, statuses are rebuilt from raw tables via production \texttt{BandPITService}. When a replication environment has empty exchange/macro history tables, the checked-in archive panel is migrated algebraically to the same clock by assigning to payoff day $t$ the statuses recorded at calendar day $t-1$ EOD (\texttt{timing\_migration=shift\_status\_from\_prev\_calendar\_day}). Content tilts continue to come from the vintaged feature cache (already lag-1) and are never overwritten by empty database pulls. Both paths implement the same decision clock; the migration path is disclosed so readers do not confuse protocol compliance with a fresh raw-history rebuild.

For each payoff date $t$ and asset $i$, band status $\in\{\mathrm{ready},\mathrm{limited},\mathrm{missing}\}$ is taken at the decision clock with band-specific freshness thresholds aligned to production. We compute $B^{\mathrm{hier}}_t$, $U_t$, $H^{\mathrm{cont}}_t$, production WMI, and a world-only ACWMI placeholder; mechanism engines later refine paper $S_t$ and $C_t$ from pre-$t$ returns. Durable historical bands are $\{\mathrm{exchange},\mathrm{macro},\mathrm{alternative}\}$. Yahoo returns provide payoffs and are reconciled against exchange daily bars when archived. News/on-chain/options/tokenomics remain right-censored and are \emph{not} part of the identifying band set.

\subsection{Band readiness rates}
Empirical ready rates on the panel are approximately: exchange $0.998$, macro $1.000$, alternative $1.000$, news $0.008$, on-chain/options/tokenomics/event-calendar $\approx 0$. Identification on durable bands is therefore the credible core; right-censored bands enter only as agenda items for continuous collection.

\section{Empirical design}
\label{sec:design}

\paragraph{Pre-specified contrast.}
The single pre-specified OOS contrast is \textbf{mechanism (band content) $-$ momentum}: the two policies share all return-based inputs and differ only by vintaged macro/alternative content. Menu-wide comparisons versus always-long are reported with a reality-check correction and are \emph{not} the headline claim.

\paragraph{Sample split.}
Chronological IS/OOS split (half/half on the archive calendar; currently 200/200 days).

\paragraph{Frozen thresholds.}
AC thresholds are frozen on IS by Sharpe maximization subject to abstain rate $\in[5\%,55\%]$. On the current sparse archive the calibration selects minimal additional gating ($\mathrm{ACWMI}<0.25$ or $C<0.25$), so selective-prediction results are secondary to content identification. The production WMI threshold $0.2$ is never tuned on OOS. All grids and bounds are versioned in \texttt{experiment\_config.json}.

\paragraph{Policies.}
Always-long; momentum; mechanism with band content (ungated); simple outage; cascade; WMI; ACWMI (IS-frozen).

\paragraph{Economic value.}
Annualized return and volatility, Sharpe ratio, CRRA certainty equivalent with $\gamma=2$, and maximum drawdown. Positions are equal-weight across tradeable names on active days; abstention earns cash (zero return).

\paragraph{Inference.}
OOS contrasts use circular block bootstrap on daily portfolio PnL ($n_{\mathrm{boot}}=999$, block length 5 days) with two-sided $p$-values for $\Delta$Sharpe and $\Delta$CE under $H_0:\Delta=0$, block-length sensitivity over $\{5,10,21\}$, and a \citet{white2000} reality-check correction across the policy menu against the always-long benchmark.

\paragraph{Costs and funding.}
Positions are in $\{-1,0,+1\}$; we report one-way proportional costs of $10/25/50$ bps on $|\Delta\mathrm{position}|$ and a perpetual-funding adjustment (longs pay, shorts receive the archived 8-hour rates aggregated daily) where funding history is archived.

\paragraph{Identification designs.}
(i) Thin vs thick worlds using real PIT band statuses \emph{and} content; (ii) leave-one-band-out on durable bands as economic MIG with the content/gating decomposition of Proposition~\ref{prop:lobo}, plus joint macro+alternative deletion; (iii) date-scrambled content placebo; (iv) scarce-world event study using an \emph{expanding} $B^{\mathrm{hier}}$ quantile (no full-sample look-ahead) and a separate hard $O_t$ availability event study when outages exist; (v) a 2017--2026 long-span external-validity audit of the action rule on BTC/ETH.

\subsection{Mechanism signals: transparent rule with band content}
\label{sec:mech}
Directional actions are \emph{not} a latent model, and---critically for identification---band \emph{content} enters the action map directly. On pre-$t$ returns, production calculators emit
\begin{align*}
S_t&=\mathrm{clip}\!\big(\mathrm{hl\_factor}\cdot(1-0.7\,\mathrm{crowd}_t)\cdot(0.35+0.65\,\mathrm{surprise}_t)\big),\\
C_t&=\text{pairwise sign-agreement among }\{\mathrm{mom5},\mathrm{flow},-\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{casc}>0.55},-\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{sys}>55},\mathrm{macro\_tilt},\mathrm{alt\_tilt}\},
\end{align*}
and two PIT-safe band-content signals:
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{macro\_tilt}_t&=\begin{cases}+1 & \Delta_5\mathrm{VIX}<0\ \wedge\ \Delta_5\mathrm{DXY}<0\ \text{(risk-on)}\\ -1 & \Delta_5\mathrm{VIX}>0\ \wedge\ \Delta_5\mathrm{DXY}>0\ \text{(risk-off)}\\ 0 & \text{otherwise or band not ready,}\end{cases}\\
\mathrm{alt\_tilt}_t&=\mathrm{sign}\bigl(\text{stablecoin 7d net supply change, latest obs}<t\bigr)\ \text{(0 if band not ready)},
\end{align*}
where macro changes use vintaged \texttt{available\_at} timestamps. The action rule is deterministic:
\begin{align*}
\text{(R1)}&\quad \mathrm{crisis}\ \wedge\ \mathrm{cascade\_p}\ge 0.60 \Rightarrow \mathrm{signal}=-1\quad\text{(evidence conjunction)},\\
\text{(R2)}&\quad \mathrm{trend}\ \wedge\ \mathrm{mom5}>0\ \wedge\ \mathrm{cascade\_p}<0.45\ \wedge\ \mathrm{macro\_tilt}\ge 0 \Rightarrow \mathrm{signal}=+1,\\
\text{(R2b)}&\quad \mathrm{range}\ \wedge\ \mathrm{macro\_tilt}>0\ \wedge\ \mathrm{alt\_tilt}>0\ \wedge\ \mathrm{mom5}\ge 0 \Rightarrow \mathrm{signal}=+1,\\
\text{(R3)}&\quad \text{else }\mathrm{sign}(\mathrm{mom5}),\ \text{with a long vetoed to }0\ \text{when both tilts are risk-off};\\
&\quad \text{ties broken by }\mathrm{sign}(\mathrm{macro\_tilt}+\mathrm{alt\_tilt}).
\end{align*}
A zero signal is an intentional stand-aside (flat position). Because tilts are forced to zero whenever their band is not PIT-ready, leave-one-band-out deletes \emph{content}, not only gating. R1 requires the conjunction of regime and cascade evidence---single-source triggers are not actionable, the same logic as the consistency factor $C$. Component definitions and regime-level composition are exported with the replication tables.

\paragraph{Calibration audit and engine-input repair.}
A long-span audit initially exposed two harness flaws: full-history drawdowns made \texttt{crisis} an absorbing state, and the cascade input map saturated ($\mathrm{cascade\_p}\approx0.86$ always), collapsing the rule into a constant short that won a bear-market OOS by construction. We repaired the input maps (bounded 60-day windows, volatility-standardized tail intensity, real RSI/ADX proxies). Post-repair, $\mathrm{cascade\_p}$ is monotone in realized next-day left tails across deciles ($P(r_{t+1}<-5\%)$ rising from $0.039$ to $0.110$), and regimes are no longer degenerate. We disclose this repair explicitly: it was driven by the audit, not by OOS performance search.

\subsection{Leave-one-band-out: significant, and driven by content}
Table~\ref{tab:lobo} is the empirical counterpart of Proposition~\ref{prop:lobo}. Deleting the macro band costs $\Delta\mathrm{CE}=-0.418$ ($p=0.010$); deleting alternative costs $-0.402$ ($p=0.008$); deleting exchange status costs $-0.056$ ($p=0.394$; exchange content is the return data itself and cannot be separately deleted). The decomposition shows the loss operates mainly through the \emph{content} channel (content-only deletion: $-0.334$, $p=0.034$ for both bands) with a smaller but significant \emph{gating} contribution (macro $-0.066$, $p=0.026$; alternative $-0.040$, $p=0.044$). Band information changes \emph{what} the rule trades, not merely \emph{when} it abstains---the direct answer to the concern that LOBO measures gating sensitivity rather than information content. The content-only losses coincide across the two bands because the R2b long and the double-risk-off veto both require agreement of the two tilts, so deleting either band disables both branches on most affected days. Joint macro+alternative deletion costs $\Delta\mathrm{CE}=-0.393$ ($p=0.018$). Zeroing either the VIX or the DXY leg of $\mathrm{macro\_tilt}$ collapses the rule to the momentum baseline ($\Delta\mathrm{CE}=-0.334$, $p=0.034$)---the macro signal is a conjunction, not a single-series alpha. A date-scrambled content placebo loses $0.386$ CE relative to timed content ($p=0.082$). Planted 5\% availability shocks move CE by $-0.029$ ($p=0.762$): hard $O_t$ bites directionally but is not yet precise in this continuous backfill.

\subsection{Costs, funding, and inference robustness}
The pre-specified mechanism $-$ momentum contrast remains significant after costs: at 10 bps, $\Delta\mathrm{CE}=0.333$ ($p=0.034$, CI excludes zero) even though the mechanism's own CE turns slightly negative ($-0.019$ vs momentum $-0.352$); at 25 bps, $\Delta\mathrm{CE}=0.332$ ($p=0.038$). Absolute profitability is therefore fragile to costs; relative content value versus the same-input momentum benchmark is not. Funding adjustments change CE by less than $0.005$. Bootstrap conclusions are stable across block lengths $\{5,10,21\}$. The \citet{white2000} reality check across the full policy menu against always-long gives $p=0.144$---menu-wide claims remain weaker than the pre-specified pairwise contrast. The $B^{\mathrm{hier}}$ weight perturbations leave CE unchanged to three decimals.

A transparent IS-fit / OOS-evaluate linear bridge (compiled features vs $\mathrm{mom5}$ alone) is reported in the replication package as a predictive stand-in for the compilation wedge: compiled features improve the sign-projection Sharpe relative to thin momentum in this window, while $R^2$ remains near zero---consistent with economic value coming from state-contingent actions rather than from high linear return predictability.

\subsection{World-quality indices on a sparse archive (secondary)}
The core theory still defines WMI/ACWMI and world-conditional abstention, but the present archive does not identify them as the headline margin. IS calibration selects minimal additional index gating (frozen $\mathrm{ACWMI}<0.25$ barely binds beyond content stand-asides), so ACWMI tracks the ungated mechanism; production WMI$<0.2$ abstains essentially always because sparse-archive levels sit below a denser-world threshold. These facts are \emph{consistent} with Proposition~\ref{prop:abstain} (thresholds must be frozen to the support they police); they are \emph{not} a successful selective-prediction horse-race. ECP is high ($0.688$ at $\mathrm{conf}>0.7$, $\mathrm{WMI}<0.2$), and actions remain fully evidence-bound ($\mathrm{EAR}=1$).

\subsection{Interpretation}
The results speak to the core theory at the content margin of compilation. First, enlarging usable $I_t$ by vintaged macro/alternative content changes actions and raises OOS certainty-equivalent value relative to the same return-based rule without that content (Propositions~\ref{prop:compile} and~\ref{prop:lobo}). Second, the content channel dominates gating in LOBO, matching the theory's distinction between what enters the action map and what only affects readiness. Third, the long-span audit shows the return-based core has no hidden alpha, so the OOS gap loads on compiled band content. Fourth, absolute CE is cost-fragile while the pre-specified relative gap remains significant---economic importance should be read as information value, not as a frictionless trading mandate. Fifth, WMI/ACWMI abstention and AI-consumer protocols remain part of the apparatus for denser archives; they are not the claim established by Tables~\ref{tab:econ}--\ref{tab:lobo}.

\label{sec:ai_consumer}

Because the SDF interface concerns agents that condition on $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t$, the replication package pre-registers a within-model Compiled versus Raw protocol for AI consumers:
\[
\Delta_m=V_m(\mathrm{Compiled})-V_m(\mathrm{Raw}),
\]
with frozen prompts, temperature zero, and action schema $\{\mathrm{bullish},\mathrm{bearish},\mathrm{neutral},\mathrm{abstain}\}$. This is deliberately \emph{not} primary identification. \section{Robustness, threats, and the JF/RFS agenda}
\label{sec:robust}

\paragraph{Right-censored bands.}
News/on-chain/options/tokenomics lack durable history in the current archive. Continuous multi-year collection is required before claiming full thirteen-band identification.

\paragraph{Natural outages.}
Hard institutional outages are rare inside a continuous OKX backfill. Logged \texttt{collection\_runs} failures and planted availability shocks are the path to sharper $O_t$ designs; scarce-world labels use expanding quantiles only as descriptive complements.

\paragraph{Production vs paper ACWMI.}
Live API bundles may attach production proxies for $(S,C)$ when engines are absent; paper empirics use return-engine $(S,C)$. Bundles disclose \texttt{acwmi\_input\_source}$\in\{\texttt{paper\_engines},\texttt{production\_proxy}\}$. These objects must not be treated as interchangeable without disclosure.

\paragraph{Snapshot density.}
Analytics snapshots remain sparse historically; PIT currently relies on raw history. Daily readiness/AI-context snapshots will enable pure \texttt{time\_slice} replay.

\paragraph{External validity.}
The panel covers ten liquid names and durable content only for macro/alternative. The 2017--2026 long-span audit anchors the return-based core of the rule, but band-content identification currently rests on a single archive window. Cross-section expansion and longer content calendars remain first-order.

\paragraph{Multiple testing.}
The headline mechanism $-$ momentum contrast is a single pre-specified comparison; the menu-wide reality check ($p=0.144$) is reported alongside it. A final submission should add loss-function variants and stationary-bootstrap confirmation.

\paragraph{AI-consumer limits.}
Multi-model Compiled--Raw contrasts validate the AI interface of the theory. They are not substitutes for PIT/LOBO identification and must not be read as unconditional LLM alpha.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

The core claim of the paper is theoretical before it is empirical: in markets where evidence is asynchronous and quality-heterogeneous, the object that enters pricing and AI decisions is not the raw filtration but a compiled information set $\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\sigma(\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t))$. Epistemic observations, reconstruction bounds, the SDF interface, and the LOBO content/gating decomposition are the apparatus that make that claim precise.

What the present archive identifies is the content margin of that apparatus. Under a previous-close PIT clock, vintage-safe macro and stablecoin-flow content entering a transparent rule raises OOS certainty-equivalent value relative to same-input momentum; LOBO attributes the loss from band deletion mainly to content; and a long-span audit without band archives finds no hidden return-rule alpha. Absolute profitability is cost-fragile; menu-wide superiority versus always-long is not established. World-quality indexing and selective prediction remain defined for denser information-set supports, and an AI-interface protocol is recorded for later consumer tests.

The agenda is therefore not to replace the theory, but to give it thicker measurement: multi-year vintaged band histories, logged availability shocks, daily snapshots for pure time-slice replay, and broader cross-sections---so that gating, abstention, and the SDF wedge can be tested with the same discipline already applied to band content.

\section{Full proofs}
\label{app:proofs}

\paragraph{Proposition~\ref{prop:sdf} (SDF interface).}
Let $\mathcal{G}=\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t\subseteq\mathcal{H}=\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t$ and $Y=m_{t+1}R_{t+1}$ integrable. Define $w_t=\mathbb{E}[Y\mid\mathcal{H}]-\mathbb{E}[Y\mid\mathcal{G}]$. (i) $\mathbb{E}[w_t\mid\mathcal{G}]=\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{E}[Y\mid\mathcal{H}]\mid\mathcal{G}]-\mathbb{E}[Y\mid\mathcal{G}]=0$ by the tower property. (ii) $w_t=0$ a.s.\ for all payoffs iff $\mathbb{E}[Y\mid\mathcal{H}]$ is $\mathcal{G}$-measurable for all $Y$, which is the statement that no pricing-relevant information is lost by $\Pi_t$. (iii) If band $k$ has $\mathrm{MIG}_{k,t}>0$ for the pricing task, take $Y^\star$ measurable with respect to $\sigma(E_{k,t})\vee\mathcal{G}$ with $\mathbb{E}[Y^\star\mid\mathcal{H}]$ non-degenerate in $E_{k,t}$; then $\mathrm{Var}(w_t)\ge\mathrm{Var}\bigl(\mathbb{E}[Y^\star\mid\mathcal{H}]-\mathbb{E}[Y^\star\mid\mathcal{G}]\bigr)>0$. Hence alpha measured against $\mathcal{H}$ for an agent constrained to $\mathcal{G}$ mixes true mispricing with the compilation wedge. \hfill$\square$

\paragraph{Proposition~\ref{prop:lobo} (decomposition detail).}
Let $V(\cdot)$ be evaluated at fixed frozen thresholds. Write the empirical rule as $a_t=\rho(E^{\mathrm{ret}}_t,\kappa_{k,t},g_t)$ where $\kappa_{k,t}$ is band $k$'s content signal and $g_t=\mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{ACWMI}_t\ge\bar a,\,C_t\ge\bar c\}$ the gate. Content deletion maps $\kappa_{k,t}\mapsto 0$ holding the gate inputs at their observed values; gating deletion maps band status to \texttt{missing}, changing $(B,U,H,\mathrm{ACWMI},C)$ and hence $g_t$, holding $\kappa_{k,t}$. The telescoping identity in the proposition is exact by construction of the three experiment arms; no approximation is involved. Blackwell monotonicity of $V$ in the underlying information order follows from the value function being a maximum over decision rules measurable with respect to the conditioning set. \hfill$\square$
