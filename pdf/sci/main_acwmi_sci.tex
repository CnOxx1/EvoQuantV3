\title{Regime-Conditional Adaptive World Models for AI Cryptocurrency Market Analysis: Formalization and Project-Grounded Evaluation of EvoQuant}

\author[mymainaddress]{Guocong Li}
\ead{lmu151638@gmail.com}
\address[mymainaddress]{Independent Researcher; correspondence: lmu151638@gmail.com}

\begin{abstract}
AI market systems often fail because the market world they consume is incomplete, stale, or silently contaminated---not because the predictor lacks capacity. This paper studies that failure mode through \emph{EvoQuant}, an open cryptocurrency data world-model infrastructure comprising 43 data domains, 13 audit bands, 39 logic modules, and a production World Model Index (WMI) already implemented in code. We formalize EvoQuant's compilation pipeline as a regime-conditional adaptive world model (RCA-WM) and propose an Adaptive Conditional World Model Index (ACWMI) that augments production WMI with hierarchical breadth, continuous honesty, signal integrity, and cross-evidence consistency. Critically, all mechanism scores used in evaluation are computed by EvoQuant's own calculators (regime classification, liquidation cascade, contagion, alpha decay, flow decomposition, volatility, and degradation control). On a project-grounded panel of 1800 asset-day observations with planted structural events, cascade detection reaches F1$=0.895$, crisis detection F1$=0.793$, and coarse regime match accuracy is $71.6\%$. An ACWMI-aware abstention policy drives unsafe action rates in crisis to $0$, versus $81\%$ under the production WMI threshold. The contribution is a systems-native theory of AI market world-model quality that is inseparable from a concrete, runnable infrastructure.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Most discussions of AI trading emphasize model architecture, prompts, or feature lists. In production cryptocurrency markets, a more fundamental bottleneck appears first: whether the agent is shown a market world that is broad enough, fresh enough, and honest enough to support auditable judgment. Identical price paths can correspond to incompatible states once exchange fragmentation, leverage crowding, funding distortions, options positioning, unlock pressure, and macro liquidity are considered \citep{makarov2020,liu2022}.

EvoQuant is built precisely as that missing layer. It is not a trading bot. It is an AI-oriented market world-model infrastructure that continuously collects heterogeneous evidence, maintains \texttt{latest\_*} snapshots, gates AI-ready inputs, separates main and diagnostic views, and aggregates an \texttt{ai\_market\_context} bundle. In the current repository, this surface includes 43 data domains, 13 audit bands, 39 logic modules, roughly $1.34\times 10^5$ lines of Python, and a production World Model Index
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t\times U_t\times H_t
\label{eq:wmi}
\end{equation}
implemented in \texttt{logic\_layer.ai\_market\_context}, with abstention suggested when $\mathrm{WMI}_t<0.2$.

Equation~\eqref{eq:wmi} is operationally useful but theoretically incomplete for the system EvoQuant has already become. Breadth is hierarchical (domains $\rightarrow$ bands $\rightarrow$ assets), honesty is richer than a three-level flag, mechanism engines emit cascade/contagion/regime/decay signals that should enter compilation, and degradation modes change what an honest system is allowed to claim. This paper therefore asks:

\begin{quote}
How can the world-model quality of a concrete AI market infrastructure be formalized and evaluated using that system's own runnable analytics?
\end{quote}

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Systems formalization.} We cast EvoQuant's pipeline as a regime-conditional compilation operator $\Pi_t^{(r,m)}$.
\item \textbf{Metric.} We propose ACWMI, a weighted-geometric extension of production WMI with hierarchical breadth, continuous honesty, signal integrity, and consistency.
\item \textbf{Project-grounded evaluation.} Mechanism features and detection tasks are produced by EvoQuant calculators; planted events provide non-circular labels.
\item \textbf{Safety result.} ACWMI-aware abstention eliminates unsafe actions in crisis on our panel, while the production WMI threshold does not.
\item \textbf{Reproducibility.} All figures/tables are generated by repository scripts that import production code.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related work}
\label{sec:lit}

Conditional asset pricing treats expected returns as functions of an information set \citep{fama1993,cochrane2005}. Machine-learning pricing expands that set with high-dimensional characteristics \citep{gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021}, while multiple-testing critiques warn against undisciplined expansion \citep{harvey2016}. Measurement-error and robustness theories emphasize that decision quality depends on the reliability of the observed world \citep{fuller1987,carroll2006,hansen2008}. Selective prediction / abstention literatures study when a model should refuse to answer under uncertainty \citep{chow1957,geifman2017}. Data-centric AI argues that data governance can dominate model changes \citep{zha2023}. Cryptocurrency microstructure work documents fragmented venues and shared risk factors \citep{makarov2020,liu2022}.

This paper differs from feature-expansion studies: the object is not another alpha factor, but the \emph{compilation and governance} of an asynchronous multi-source market world inside a running system. It also differs from purely conceptual world-model essays by binding every claim to EvoQuant modules and calculators.

\subsection{Production WMI}
Production WMI uses asset-readiness coverage as breadth $B$, pipeline-latency freshness as stability $U$, and quality-flag honesty $H\in\{1.0,0.7,0.4\}$. \section{Regime-conditional adaptive world models}
\label{sec:theory}

\subsection{Hierarchical breadth}
\begin{definition}[Hierarchical breadth]
\begin{equation}
B_t^{\mathrm{hier}}=\alpha_1 B_t^{\mathrm{domain}}+\alpha_2 B_t^{\mathrm{band}}+\alpha_3 B_t^{\mathrm{asset}},
\end{equation}
with $\alpha=(0.25,0.35,0.40)$ in experiments and $B^{\mathrm{asset}}$ computed from EvoQuant band weights and ready/limited/missing ratios.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Continuous honesty}
\begin{definition}[Continuous honesty]
\begin{equation}
H_t^{\mathrm{cont}}=\exp(-\beta_1\rho_t^{\mathrm{cont}})\cdot\big(1-\beta_2(1-\rho_t^{\mathrm{ex}})\big)_+,
\end{equation}
where $\rho^{\mathrm{ex}}$ is the exclusion rate of non-AI-ready sources and $\rho^{\mathrm{cont}}$ is main-view contamination.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Signal integrity and consistency}
Signal integrity $S_t$ is built from EvoQuant \texttt{alpha\_decay} outputs (half-life, crowding, surprise). Consistency $C_t$ measures directional agreement among price momentum, smart-money flow, cascade pressure, contagion, and VPIN signs produced by project calculators.

\subsection{Conditional compilation and ACWMI}
\begin{equation}
\Pi_t^{(r,m)}=B_t^{(r,m)}\circ M_t^{(r,m)}\circ A_t\circ \Psi_t^{\mathrm{mech}},
\end{equation}
where $\Psi^{\mathrm{mech}}$ denotes mechanism engines (regime, cascade, contagion, flow, volatility, decay). ACWMI is the weighted geometric mean
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{ACWMI}_t^{(r)}
=
\exp\!\left(
\frac{\sum_{x\in\{B,U,H,S,C\}}\gamma_x(r)\log x_t}{\sum_x\gamma_x(r)}
\right),
\label{eq:acwmi}
\end{equation}
with crisis regimes emphasizing honesty/consistency and trend regimes emphasizing signal integrity.

\subsection{Degradation-aware abstention}
EvoQuant's \texttt{DegradationManager} exposes NORMAL/REDUCED/MINIMAL/EMERGENCY levels that gate modules. An AC policy abstains under low ACWMI, severe degradation, high cascade probability, or crisis detections with elevated cascade pressure.

\section{Experimental design}
\label{sec:design}

\paragraph{Principle.}
Synthetic paths are used only as inputs. Every reported mechanism metric is computed by importing EvoQuant classes:
\texttt{RegimeClassifier}, \texttt{LiquidationCascadeCalculator}, \texttt{ContagionRiskCalculator}, \texttt{AlphaDecayCalculator}, \texttt{FlowDecompositionCalculator}, \texttt{VolatilityCalculator}, \texttt{AIMarketContextService.\_compute\_world\_model\_index}, and \texttt{DegradationManager}.

\paragraph{Panel.}
10 assets $\times$ 180 days ($N=1800$) with Markov regimes $\{\mathrm{trend},\mathrm{range},\mathrm{crisis}\}$ and source outages (probability $0.15$ in crisis, $0.03$ otherwise). Planted labels are the true regime, true cascade-visible state, and outage indicator---not functions of ACWMI.

\paragraph{Tasks.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Crisis detection and cascade detection from project engines.
\item Coarse regime match between planted regime and classifier output.
\item Unsafe-action rate: acting (non-abstention) during crisis or outage.
\item Degradation matrix: which modules \texttt{should\_run} at each level.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:disc}

The results support three claims. First, EvoQuant already implements a nontrivial world-model stack; the scientific task is to formalize and stress-test it, not to invent a detached benchmark. Second, production WMI is too coarse for crisis refusal: a fixed $0.2$ threshold leaves most crisis observations actionable. Third, mechanism engines are first-class citizens of world-model quality: cascade and contagion outputs materially improve abstention.

Limitations remain. The panel uses controlled paths as inputs, so results are project-grounded but not yet a full live-market backtest. Regime exponents $\gamma(r)$ are pre-specified. External validity beyond crypto 24/7 microstructure is open. Future work should freeze PIT snapshots from live collectors and evaluate downstream LLM/analyst decision quality under ACWMI gates.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

This paper presented a standalone formalization and evaluation of AI market world-model quality grounded entirely in the EvoQuant system. By elevating production WMI to a regime-conditional ACWMI, and by scoring detection/safety tasks with the repository's own calculators, we show that world-model quality can be treated as a measurable systems property. In our project-grounded panel, mechanism detection is strong and AC-aware abstention removes crisis-time unsafe actions that production WMI permits. For AI market analysis, the scarce asset is a runnable, auditable world model---and EvoQuant provides the empirical substrate on which that claim can be tested.
