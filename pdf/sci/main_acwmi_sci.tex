\begin{abstract}
Conditional asset pricing treats the investor's information set as given. In cryptocurrency markets that premise fails: evidence is asynchronous, multi-source, and intermittently unavailable. This paper formalizes \emph{information-set compilation} and measures world-model quality with an Adaptive Conditional World Model Index (ACWMI). Empirically, we populate a real multi-band archive---OKX exchange bars, macro vintages, alternative series, plus thinner news/on-chain/options/tokenomics snapshots---and construct a 400-day point-in-time panel (10 assets, 4{,}000 asset-days) aligned to Yahoo returns. Mechanism engines use only pre-$t$ history. Abstention thresholds are frozen in-sample. Out of sample, thick real PIT worlds dominate exchange-only thin worlds (CE $0.47$ vs $-0.01$). Leave-one-band-out on durable bands shows large CE losses from dropping macro ($-0.53$), alternative ($-0.53$), or exchange ($-0.34$). An IS-frozen ACWMI gate remains implementable (Sharpe $0.90$, CE $0.20$) but does not dominate ungated thick signals on CE. The production WMI$<0.2$ rule is mis-scaled for sparse archives (100\% abstention), illustrating why quality thresholds must be frozen to the information set's support. The contribution is a finance-native compilation theory plus a reproducible PIT identification protocol on a live multi-band laboratory.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} information set, selective prediction, cryptocurrency, point-in-time, measurement error\\

\section{Introduction}

Modern asset pricing conditions on an information set $I_t$ \citep{fama1993,cochrane2005,gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021}. The literature's discipline has focused on how $I_t$ is \emph{used}, not how it is \emph{compiled} from asynchronous, quality-heterogeneous evidence. Cryptocurrency markets make compilation first-order: venue fragmentation, leverage, options walls, unlocks, on-chain flows, and macro liquidity can map the same price path into incompatible states \citep{makarov2020,liu2022}.

This paper (i) formalizes Regime-Conditional Adaptive World Models (RCA-WM) and ACWMI, (ii) populates a real multi-band archive using the project's collectors, (iii) builds a point-in-time panel from history tables, and (iv) evaluates OOS economic value with frozen thresholds and leave-one-band-out identification.

\section{Theory}
\label{sec:theory}

This section restores the formal objects of the World-Model-First / RCA-WM framework
(source: \texttt{pdf/original/}). Empirics in later sections instantiate these objects on a real PIT archive; they do not replace the theory.

\subsection{World-model quality: breadth, stability, honesty}
\label{sec:wmi}

\begin{definition}[Breadth]
Let $K$ be the number of critical evidence bands at $t$, and $a_{k,t}\in\{0,1\}$ indicate whether band $k$ meets the AI-usable threshold. Breadth is
\begin{equation}
B_t=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^{K}a_{k,t}.
\label{eq:breadth}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Stability]
Let $d_{j,t}$ be a freshness/TTL penalty for source $j$ with importance weight $\omega_j$. Stability is
\begin{equation}
U_t=\exp\!\left(-\sum_{j=1}^{J}\omega_j d_{j,t}\right).
\label{eq:stability}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Honesty]
Let $m_{j,t}=1$ if source $j$ should not enter the AI main view but is incorrectly injected. Honesty is
\begin{equation}
H_t=1-\frac{1}{J}\sum_{j=1}^{J}m_{j,t}.
\label{eq:honesty}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

The production baseline World Model Index is the product form used by EvoQuant:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t\times U_t\times H_t.
\label{eq:wmi}
\end{equation}

For hierarchical readiness we also use a convex combination of domain-, band-, and asset-level readiness ratios,
\begin{equation}
B^{\mathrm{hier}}_t=0.25\,B^{\mathrm{dom}}_t+0.35\,B^{\mathrm{band}}_t+0.40\,B^{\mathrm{asset}}_t,
\label{eq:bhier}
\end{equation}
and a continuous honesty score that rewards exclusion of contaminated evidence and penalizes partial contamination,
\begin{equation}
H^{\mathrm{cont}}_t=\exp(-2c_t)\max\!\bigl(0,\,1-0.5(1-e_t)\bigr),
\label{eq:hcont}
\end{equation}
where $e_t$ is the ready-share and $c_t$ a contamination share.

\subsection{Epistemic observations}
An AI-consumable market observation is not a bare scalar $x_{j,t}$ but an epistemic object
\begin{equation}
O_{j,t}=\bigl(x_{j,t},\,\tau_{j,t},\,q_{j,t},\,g_{j,t},\,r_{j,t}\bigr),
\label{eq:obs}
\end{equation}
with latest-available time $\tau$, quality state $q$, main-view gate $g$, and semantic role $r$. The AI consumes a set of such objects, not a flat feature matrix.

\subsection{Asynchronous state space and lag error}
Let latent states evolve as $S_{t+1}=F(S_t,\eta_{t+1})$. Source $j$ observes a lagged map
\begin{equation}
X^{\mathrm{obs}}_{j,t}=h_j(S_{t-\ell_{j,t}})+\nu_{j,t},\qquad \ell_{j,t}\ge 0.
\label{eq:lagobs}
\end{equation}
If $h_j$ is Lipschitz with constant $L_j$, then
\begin{equation}
\bigl\|h_j(S_t)-h_j(S_{t-\ell_{j,t}})\bigr\|
\le L_j\sum_{u=t-\ell_{j,t}+1}^{t}\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|.
\label{eq:lipschitz}
\end{equation}
A world-model reconstruction $\widetilde S_t$ therefore obeys a bound with delay, noise, and missingness terms:
\begin{equation}
\|\widetilde S_t-S_t\|
\le C_1\sum_j\omega_j\ell_{j,t}
+C_2\sum_j\omega_j\|\nu_{j,t}\|
+C_3\sum_j\omega_j(1-z_{j,t}).
\label{eq:recon}
\end{equation}
Latest-snapshot, freshness/TTL, and \texttt{is\_ready\_for\_ai} gates target these three terms.

\subsection{Filters and the compilation operator}
Define the raw and AI-visible filtrations
\begin{align}
\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t&=\sigma\bigl(\{X^{\mathrm{obs}}_{j,\tau}: j\le J,\ \tau\le t\}\bigr),
\label{eq:fraw}\\
\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t&=\sigma\bigl(W^{\mathrm{AI}}_t,\,D_t\bigr),
\label{eq:fai}
\end{align}
where $W^{\mathrm{AI}}_t$ is the main view and $D_t$ the diagnostic view. Compilation is
\begin{equation}
\Pi_t=B_t\circ M_t\circ A_t,\qquad
W^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\Pi_t\bigl(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t\bigr),
\label{eq:pi}
\end{equation}
with alignment/snapshot sync $A_t$, quality gating $M_t$, and bundle aggregation $B_t$.\footnote{Here $B_t$ denotes the aggregation map in the operator composition; it is distinct from breadth in \eqref{eq:breadth}. Context disambiguates.}

\subsection{Calibration penalty, MIG, and causal DAG}
If the AI reports confidence $\mathrm{conf}_t$ while $\mathrm{WMI}_t$ is low, define the epistemic calibration penalty
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{ECP}_t=\mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{conf}_t>\bar c\}\,\mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{WMI}_t<w\}.
\label{eq:ecp}
\end{equation}
Thick versus thin information sets differ by conditional mutual information
\begin{equation}
H(R_t\mid I^{\mathrm{thin}}_t)-H(R_t\mid I^{\mathrm{thick}}_t)
=I\!\bigl(R_t;\,I^{\mathrm{thick}}_t\setminus I^{\mathrm{thin}}_t\,\big|\,I^{\mathrm{thin}}_t\bigr).
\label{eq:thickthin}
\end{equation}
Band $k$'s marginal information gain on task $m$ is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{MIG}^{(m)}_{k,t}=I\!\bigl(R^{(m)}_t;\,E_{k,t}\,\big|\,I^{(-k)}_t\bigr).
\label{eq:mig}
\end{equation}
For identification, let $W_t$ be world quality, $A_t$ AI analysis quality, $M_t$ market complexity, $O_t$ observable availability shocks, and $C_t$ latent configuration:
\begin{equation}
O_t\to W_t\to A_t,\quad
M_t\to W_t,\quad
M_t\to A_t,\quad
C_t\to (W_t,A_t).
\label{eq:dag}
\end{equation}
Without controlling $M_t,C_t$, ``thicker worlds correlate with better AI'' is not causal. Availability shocks $O_t$ are the preferred quasi-exogenous lever.

\subsection{Bayesian abstention}
With action set $\mathcal{A}=\{\mathrm{bullish},\mathrm{bearish},\mathrm{neutral},\mathrm{abstain}\}$ and loss $\ell(a,R_t)$,
\begin{equation}
a^\star_t=\arg\min_{a\in\mathcal{A}}\,\mathbb{E}\bigl[\ell(a,R_t)\mid W_t\bigr].
\label{eq:bayes}
\end{equation}
Abstain whenever every non-abstain action has expected loss above a world-dependent cost $c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)$:
\begin{equation}
\min_{a\neq\mathrm{abstain}}\mathbb{E}\bigl[\ell(a,R_t)\mid W_t\bigr]>c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t).
\label{eq:abstain}
\end{equation}

\subsection{ACWMI and explanation metrics}
Regime-conditional ACWMI extends \eqref{eq:wmi} to a weighted geometric mean of
$x_t=(B^{\mathrm{hier}}_t,U_t,H^{\mathrm{cont}}_t,S_t,C_t)$ with regime weights $\gamma(r_t)\in\mathbb{R}^5_{+}$:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{ACWMI}_t
=\exp\!\left(
\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{5}\gamma_i(r_t)\log x_{i,t}}{\sum_{i=1}^{5}\gamma_i(r_t)}
\right),
\label{eq:acwmi}
\end{equation}
where $S_t$ is signal integrity and $C_t$ cross-evidence consistency. Explanation quality uses evidence attribution, unsupported-claim, and explanation-volatility rates:
\begin{align}
\mathrm{EAR}_t&=\frac{\#\{\text{claims bound to evidence}\}}{\#\{\text{claims}\}},
\label{eq:ear}\\
\mathrm{UCR}_t&=1-\mathrm{EAR}_t,
\label{eq:ucr}\\
\mathrm{EV}_t&=\frac{d(\Phi_t,\Phi_{t-1})}{1+d(W_t,W_{t-1})},
\label{eq:ev}
\end{align}
with explanation object $\Phi_t$ and distance $d(\cdot,\cdot)$.

\section{Data and PIT archive}

\subsection{Populated laboratory}
We bootstrap the project's split SQLite store (OKX-only exchange path; Binance/Bybit blocked in this environment):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Exchange:} $\sim$9.4k OKX klines / merged bars for 10 symbols, daily range 2025-06-30 to 2026-08-03; 2.4k funding rows.
\item \textbf{Macro:} 81k vintaged points (\texttt{available\_at} present), spanning decades to 2026-08-03.
\item \textbf{Alternative:} 89k points from 2017 onward (stablecoin/related series; Google Trends largely rate-limited).
\item \textbf{News / on-chain / options / tokenomics:} populated but mostly right-censored to collection day.
\item \textbf{\texttt{time\_slice}:} historically resolves klines; analytics snapshots remain sparse, so multi-band PIT is built from raw history tables via \texttt{build\_pit\_archive.py}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{PIT panel}
For each date $t$ and asset, band status $\in\{\mathrm{ready},\mathrm{limited},\mathrm{missing}\}$ is inferred from the latest observation time $\le t$ and band-specific freshness thresholds. Breadth/stability/honesty and production WMI are computed with project code. Durable historical bands are $\{\mathrm{exchange},\mathrm{macro},\mathrm{alternative}\}$. Yahoo daily returns provide the payoff series; engines use only pre-$t$ returns.

\section{Empirical design}

Chronological IS/OOS split (200/200 days) at 2026-01-16. AC thresholds frozen on IS by Sharpe maximization with abstain rate $\in[5\%,55\%]$: $\mathrm{ACWMI}<0.35$ or $C<0.35$. Production WMI threshold $0.2$ is never tuned. Baselines: always-long, momentum, thick-ungated, simple outage, cascade, WMI, ACWMI. Economic value: annualized return/vol, Sharpe, CRRA CE ($\gamma=2$), max DD.

\section{Results}

\subsection{Thick real PIT worlds dominate thin worlds}

\subsection{\texttt{time\_slice} status}
Monthly \texttt{time\_slice} probes show historical kline readiness with sparse analytics snapshots. This motivates the hybrid design: raw multi-band history for $W_t$, \texttt{time\_slice} for logic-domain replay as snapshot density grows.

\section{What remains for JF/RFS}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Continuous multi-year collection so news/on-chain/options/tokenomics cease to be right-censored.
\item Logged institutional outages as $O_t$ (natural hard outages are rare inside a continuous OKX backfill).
\item Persist daily readiness/AI-context snapshots for pure \texttt{time\_slice} replay.
\item Recalibrate/freeze WMI thresholds on the archive's support before comparing to ACWMI.
\item External validity beyond ten liquid names.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

Information-set compilation is measurable. On a real multi-band PIT archive, thick worlds dominate thin worlds, and durable bands have large leave-one-out economic value. Selective ACWMI gating is implementable under frozen thresholds but is not CE-dominant versus ungated thick signals in this sample. The laboratory path to a top-finance paper is now concrete: keep collecting, snapshot daily, and replace right-censored bands with vintaged histories.
