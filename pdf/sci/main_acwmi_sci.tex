\begin{abstract}
Conditional asset pricing and machine-learning return prediction treat the investor's information set as given. In cryptocurrency markets, that premise is first-order false: the economically relevant world is asynchronous, multi-source, and frequently unavailable. This paper formalizes \emph{information-set compilation} as an object of study. We define epistemic observation objects, lag/reconstruction bounds, a regime-conditional compilation operator, and an Adaptive Conditional World Model Index (ACWMI). Refusal to act is modeled as Bayes-optimal selective prediction when compiled world quality is low. Empirically, we construct a point-in-time panel on real daily returns for ten major cryptocurrencies (2024-08 to 2026-08), freeze abstention thresholds in-sample, and evaluate out-of-sample economic value against strong baselines. Availability shocks that are orthogonal to returns identify large declines in world-model indices. Thin (exchange-only) worlds underperform thick worlds; leaving out the exchange band produces the largest certainty-equivalent loss. Mechanism-based signals dominate always-long and momentum baselines out of sample. An IS-frozen ACWMI gate is more conservative than ungated thick signals and does not dominate on certainty equivalent in this sample---a result we report rather than conceal. The paper's contribution is a finance-native theory and identification protocol for world-model quality; a full JF/RFS test requires vintaged multi-band archives, which the accompanying infrastructure is designed to support.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The central state variable in modern asset pricing is an information set. Cochrane's stochastic discount factor framework, Fama--French factor models, and machine-learning pricing all condition expected returns on what agents are assumed to know \citep{fama1993,cochrane2005,gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021}. The literature's discipline has focused on how that information is \emph{used}---linear betas, nonlinear learners, multiple-testing controls \citep{harvey2016}---not on how it is \emph{compiled} from asynchronous, quality-heterogeneous market evidence.

Cryptocurrency markets make the compilation problem unavoidable. Fragmented venues, leverage and liquidations, options walls, unlock schedules, on-chain flows, and macro liquidity can map the same price path into incompatible economic states \citep{makarov2020,liu2022}. An AI or quantitative analyst that ``sees'' only a thin slice of this world is not estimating a different model; it is solving a different inference problem.

This paper asks four finance questions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item How should an investor-facing market world be formalized as a compiled information set rather than a raw feature matrix?
\item How can world-model quality be measured in a way that is honesty-compatible (penalizing contamination, not honest exclusion)?
\item When is refusal to trade Bayes-optimal given world quality?
\item Does compiled world quality have measurable consequences for out-of-sample economic value?
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(i) A theory of Regime-Conditional Adaptive World Models (RCA-WM) that absorbs a World-Model-First epistemology: epistemic observations, lag bounds, information filters, explanation-confidence penalties, and selective prediction. (ii) ACWMI, a regime-conditional geometric quality index over breadth, stability, honesty, signal integrity, and consistency. (iii) An identification strategy based on availability shocks $O_t$ that shift world quality without, by construction, shifting contemporaneous returns. (iv) An empirical protocol with \emph{real} crypto returns, chronological IS/OOS freezing, strong baselines, leave-one-band-out, and thin-versus-thick comparisons. (v) An honest mapping to a runnable measurement system (EvoQuant) used only as laboratory infrastructure.

\paragraph{Positioning for top finance journals.}
Relative to ML pricing, we do not propose another return predictor. Relative to crypto factor papers, we do not claim a new risk factor. The object is the \emph{governance and compilation of the conditional information set}---closer in spirit to measurement-error and robustness theories \citep{fuller1987,carroll2006,hansen2008} and to selective prediction \citep{chow1957,geifman2017} than to alpha discovery.

\section{Related literature}

\textbf{Conditional information sets.} Asset pricing takes $I_t$ as primitive \citep{cochrane2005}. We study the map from raw asynchronous evidence to an AI-/investor-usable $I_t$.

\textbf{High-dimensional predictors and mining risk.} \citet{gu2020}, \citet{kelly2019}, and \citet{nagel2021} expand characteristics; \citet{harvey2016} warn against undisciplined discovery. Our claim is prior: expansion without governance can worsen the compiled world.

\textbf{Measurement error and robustness.} Classical EIV and robust control \citep{fuller1987,carroll2006,hansen2008} motivate treating world quality as a first-order constraint on decision rules.

\textbf{Selective prediction.} Abstention has a long decision-theoretic history \citep{chow1957,geifman2017}. We make abstention a function of compiled world quality, not only of predictive entropy.

\textbf{Crypto microstructure.} Venue fragmentation and common risk factors \citep{makarov2020,liu2022} justify multi-band worlds.

\section{Theory}

\subsection{Epistemic observations and compilation}
Let $S_t$ be the latent market state and $X_{j,t}=h_j(S_t)+\nu_{j,t}$ source observations. An epistemic object is
\begin{equation}
O_{j,t}=(x_{j,t},\tau_{j,t},q_{j,t},g_{j,t},r_{j,t}),
\end{equation}
with timestamp, quality, gate, and semantic role. The compiled world is $W_t=G(\{O_{j,\tau}\},Q_t,R_t,A_t)$. Investors/AI consume $W_t$, not ungated raw feeds.

\subsection{Lag bound}
With lag $\ell_{j,t}$ and Lipschitz $h_j$, reconstruction error admits the bound
\begin{equation}
\|\tilde S_t-S_t\|
\le C_1\sum_j\omega_j\ell_{j,t}+C_2\sum_j\omega_j\|\nu_{j,t}\|+C_3\sum_j\omega_j(1-z_{j,t}).
\end{equation}

\subsection{ACWMI and selective prediction}
Let factors $\{B^{\mathrm{hier}},U,H^{\mathrm{cont}},S,C\}$ denote hierarchical breadth, stability, continuous honesty, signal integrity, and consistency. Define
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{ACWMI}_t^{(r)}
=
\exp\!\left(\frac{\sum_x\gamma_x(r)\log x_t}{\sum_x\gamma_x(r)}\right).
\end{equation}
With action set including abstain and loss $\ell(a,R_t)$, abstain is optimal when the best non-abstain risk exceeds a world-dependent cost $c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)$. Operationally we use an IS-frozen threshold rule on $(\mathrm{ACWMI}_t,C_t)$.

\subsection{Identification}
Let $W_t$ be world quality, $A_t$ analysis/decision quality, $M_t$ market complexity, $O_t$ an availability shock, and $C_t$ confounding configuration:
\begin{equation}
O_t\to W_t\to A_t,\quad M_t\to W_t,\ A_t,\quad C_t\to W_t,A_t.
\end{equation}
We construct $O_t$ as return-orthogonal Bernoulli availability shocks, so event-time moves in $W_t$ are not mechanical return artifacts. Full institutional identification will replace constructed $O_t$ with logged collector/API outages.

\section{Empirical design}

\subsection{Data}
Daily returns for BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, ADA, AVAX, LINK, DOT, and NEAR from Yahoo Finance, 2024-08-04 to 2026-08-03 ($N=7{,}300$ asset-days before warm-up; 20-day warm-up for engines). All signals use only pre-$t$ history (point-in-time). Equal-weight portfolios are formed daily.

\subsection{Information-set layer}
Because a multi-year multi-band archive is not yet populated in-repository, band readiness is constructed with production band weights and the project's WMI implementation, then subjected to return-orthogonal availability shocks ($p=0.08$). Mechanism scores (regime, cascade, contagion, flow, alpha-decay) are computed by importing production calculators on real return windows. This is a stepping-stone design toward vintaged PIT using the project's \texttt{time\_slice} layer.

\subsection{IS/OOS freeze}
Chronological split at 2025-08-14 (355 IS days / 355 OOS days). Abstention thresholds for ACWMI are chosen on IS only by maximizing Sharpe subject to abstain rate $\in[5\%,55\%]$. Production WMI threshold $0.2$ is never tuned. Frozen parameters: $\mathrm{ACWMI}<0.55$ or $C<0.25$.

\subsection{Strategies}
Always-long; momentum-always; thick-ungated mechanism signal; simple outage rule; simple cascade rule (IS-calibrated); WMI$<0.2$; ACWMI (IS-frozen). Economic value: annualized return/vol, Sharpe, CRRA certainty equivalent ($\gamma=2$), max drawdown, abstain rate.

\section{Results}

\subsection{Availability shocks identify world-quality variation}
Around return-orthogonal outages, mean WMI falls from about $0.88$ to $0.23$ and ACWMI from about $0.58$ to $0.44$ at event day $0$, while contemporaneous equal-weight returns remain near zero (Table~\ref{tab:event}, Figure~\ref{fig:event}). This supports $O_t\to W_t$ without a mechanical return confound.

\subsection{OOS economic value}
Table~\ref{tab:econ} reports OOS results. Always-long and momentum lose money in this window. Mechanism signals (thick ungated) deliver Sharpe $1.26$ and CE $0.40$. The production WMI threshold never binds (abstain rate $0$). The IS-frozen ACWMI gate abstains $21.7\%$ of the time, cutting exposure and delivering Sharpe $0.58$ and CE $\approx 0$. \textbf{We do not claim ACWMI dominance on CE in this sample.} The finance content is that (i) compiled mechanism information beats naive baselines, (ii) a frozen quality gate is implementable without OOS tuning, and (iii) gating is economically consequential (more cash, different risk profile).

\subsection{Conditional signal value}
Sorting on ACWMI alone does \emph{not} produce a clean monotonic decline in signal IC in this OOS window (Appendix table). Low-ACWMI states partly coincide with periods where short mechanism signals performed well. This qualifies the abstention motive and motivates double-sorts by regime $\times$ quality and, ultimately, vintaged multi-band shocks that are cleaner than constructed readiness.

\section{What remains for a JF/RFS submission}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Populate a multi-year vintaged archive (klines, funding, options, on-chain, macro with \texttt{available\_at}) and replay via \texttt{time\_slice}.
\item Replace constructed $O_t$ with logged collector/API outages and exchange incidents.
\item Estimate band-level MIG with real leave-one-source dropouts.
\item Evaluate analyst/LLM judgment quality (EAR/ECP) on frozen bundles, not only portfolio CE.
\item Cross-market external validity beyond the ten-asset crypto complex.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

Information-set compilation is a first-order asset-pricing object in markets where evidence is asynchronous and intermittently unavailable. RCA-WM/ACWMI provide a theory of that object; real-return OOS experiments with frozen thresholds show that thick compiled worlds and mechanism signals matter, availability shocks identify world-quality variation, and band ablations are economically heterogeneous. Selective ACWMI gating is implementable but not CE-dominant in the present sample. The path to a top-finance publication is therefore clear: keep the theory and identification protocol, and replace the constructed information-set layer with a full vintaged multi-source PIT archive.
