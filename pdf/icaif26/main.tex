\title{A Market World Model for Public LLMs:\\
Complete, Honest, Auditable Crypto Cognition before Decision}

\begin{abstract}
Most quant stacks start from \emph{strategy}. Public large language models (GPT-, DeepSeek-, and GLM-class agents) that act in cryptocurrency markets instead fail earlier: they lack a complete, honest, auditable \emph{market cognition base}. Raw feeds are asynchronous, intermittently missing, and quality-heterogeneous; ungated tool dumps invite overconfident action. We build a \emph{financial world-model runtime} that compiles multi-band evidence into a point-in-time (PIT) world bundle for public LLMs. The primitive is an epistemic observation \(O_{j,t}=(x,\tau,q,g,r)\); a compilation operator \(\Pi_t\) yields \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t\); completeness, honesty \((B,U,H)\), and WMI/ACWMI gate abstention; evidence IDs make actions auditable. On a real crypto PIT panel, Compiled-versus-Raw consumers that honor the world model abstain on thin worlds (thin-world abstain rate \(1.0\), EAR proxy \(1.0\)), while momentum-only and noisy consumers do not. A secondary content probe shows vintaged macro/stablecoin fields change a transparent rule versus same-input momentum (\(\Delta\)CE \(0.334\), \(p{=}0.034\))---evidence the compiled world is nonempty, not a strategy Holy Grail. Live OpenAI-compatible adapters (GPT / DeepSeek / GLM) share the same protocol when API keys are present.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Public LLMs are increasingly asked to analyze or trade crypto markets. The bottleneck is not another alpha signal. It is \emph{understanding}: before decision, an agent needs a market world that is \textbf{complete} (missingness disclosed), \textbf{honest} (stale or role-incoherent evidence gated), and \textbf{auditable} (actions bound to evidence). Venue fragmentation, derivatives, on-chain flows, news, and macro vintages arrive on incompatible clocks~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022}. Generative world models~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path} and tool-using agents~\cite{yao2023react,brown2020gpt3} typically assume a usable observation stream. Crypto agents often do not have one.

\paragraph{Thesis.}
We build a market \emph{world-model runtime} for public LLMs: compile raw multi-band evidence into a PIT-safe cognition bundle that GLM / DeepSeek / GPT-class models can call. Strategy metrics are secondary probes that the world has content---not the product objective.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Cognition semantics.} Epistemic observations, compilation \(\Pi_t\), reconstruction bound, completeness/honesty fields, WMI/ACWMI abstention, evidence-bound actions.
\item \textbf{Runtime for public LLMs.} Vintage-aware collectors, BandPIT previous-close clock, quality-tagged bundles, availability shocks \(O_t\), frozen Compiled-versus-Raw protocol with OpenAI-compatible live adapters.
\item \textbf{Understanding-first validation.} On a real PIT archive, world-honoring consumers abstain when the world is thin; content probes show compiled band fields are economically nonempty versus same-input momentum.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models.}
World models learn compact states and often dynamics~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path}. We address the complementary \emph{observation-side} problem: a PIT-safe, quality-tagged crypto world \emph{before} dynamics or LLM policy.

\paragraph{LLM agents in finance.}
Agentic workflows call tools over streams~\cite{yao2023react}. Without a compiled cognition base, models face stale or incomplete evidence. We evaluate within-model Compiled vs.\ Raw under a frozen schema.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and trustworthy AI.}
Abstention can be optimal under noise~\cite{elyaniv2010,geifman2017}. We tie refusal to world quality, not classifier confidence alone---aligned with trustworthy AI in finance.

\paragraph{PIT measurement.}
Macro vintages~\cite{croushore2001} and crypto microstructure~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022} motivate multi-band compilation. Bootstrap discipline follows~\cite{white2000,politis1994,harvey2016}.

\section{Market World-Model Semantics}
\label{sec:method}

\begin{definition}[Epistemic observation]
\(O_{j,t}=(x_{j,t},\tau_{j,t},q_{j,t},g_{j,t},r_{j,t})\): value, latest-available time, quality, main-view gate, semantic role.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Compilation]
\(W_t=\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t)\) with \(\Pi_t=B_t\circ M_t\circ A_t\) (align, honesty/missingness gates, band assembly); \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\sigma(W_t)\).
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Completeness, honesty, quality.}
Ready/limited/missing counts disclose coverage. Breadth/stability/honesty give \(\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t U_t H_t\); ACWMI supports regime-conditional gating. Reconstruction error admits a delay/noise/missingness bound (Eq.\ omitted for space).

\begin{proposition}[Compilation \(\neq\) feature dump]
\label{prop:compile}
Enlarging raw span without \(\Pi_t\) need not enlarge usable \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}\): ungated stale evidence can raise raw volume while lowering honesty/stability.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[World-conditional abstention]
\label{prop:abstain}
If non-abstain actions exceed a world-dependent cost \(c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)\), Bayes action is abstain; with costs decreasing in WMI, abstention is a lower set in world quality~\cite{elyaniv2010}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[Not a generative WM]
We do not learn \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\). We deliver a \emph{state compiler + abstention runtime} for public LLM consumers.
\end{remark}

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup}
PIT panel \(\sim\)399 days, 10 liquid names, previous-close clock, IS/OOS \(200/200\) days (\(\sim\)2000 asset-days OOS). Consumers: \texttt{public-llm-compiled-follower} (stylized public LLM), diagnostic mocks (compiled-aware / momentum / noisy), optional live vendors. Prompts frozen; temperature \(0\).

\subsection{RQ1: Does the world model change LLM behavior?}
Table~\ref{tab:llm} and Figure~\ref{fig:und}: world-honoring consumers abstain on the entire OOS window under the sparse-archive WMI/ACWMI gate (abstain \(1.0\); thin-world abstain \(1.0\); EAR proxy \(1.0\)). Raw arms never receive abstention guidance (abstain \(0\)). Momentum-only and noisy consumers \emph{ignore} thin-world flags (thin-world abstain \(0\))---exactly the failure mode of ungated public LLMs. Mean \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}\) across consumers is positive but secondary: the cognition claim is correct refusal under thin support (Prop.~\ref{prop:abstain}).

\subsection{RQ2: Is the compiled world nonempty? (content probe)}
To show the bundle is not an empty label, a transparent rule shares return inputs with momentum and adds vintaged \(\mathrm{macro\_tilt}\) / \(\mathrm{alt\_tilt}\). Pre-specified OOS contrast mechanism $-$ momentum: \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}0.334\) (\(p{=}0.034\); stationary \(p{=}0.022\)). LOBO: deleting macro or alternative collapses to momentum (content share \(1.0\) under the ungated rule). Relative gaps survive \(10\)--\(25\)bps costs; absolute CE is cost-fragile. A \(2017\)--\(2026\) audit without vintaged archives finds no hidden return-rule alpha. These results \emph{probe content}, they do not define the product.

\subsection{Discussion}
Quant stacks usually ask ``what trades?'' We ask ``what does the LLM know, and should it act?'' Table~\ref{tab:llm} answers the second question under sparse support: a cognition base that marks the world thin induces abstention; raw feeds and strategy-like mocks do not. Table~\ref{tab:econ} answers a weaker but necessary question: compiled band fields are not decorative---they change actions and CE versus same-input momentum. Together they match the product claim: \emph{understanding first, strategy second}.

Live vendors share the OpenAI-compatible adapter without changing the estimand. In this environment, API keys were absent, so we report the offline public-LLM follower and diagnostic mocks; the same prompts and bundles apply when GPT / DeepSeek / GLM endpoints are configured.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limit}

Offline stylized public-LLM followers are protocol-complete without keys; live GPT/DeepSeek/GLM runs require credentials and remain the next measurement step. Sparse archives make production WMI thresholds bind almost always---consistent with support-matched abstention (Prop.~\ref{prop:abstain}), not a finished selective-prediction horse-race on dense worlds. News/on-chain/options history remains right-censored and is disclosed as missing. We do not claim generative dynamics \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\) or unconditional trading alpha.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

Public LLMs need a market to \emph{understand} before they decide. We compile asynchronous crypto evidence into a complete, honest, auditable world-model bundle and show that world-honoring consumers abstain when the world is thin, while content probes confirm the compiled state is nonempty. Strategy is downstream of understanding---not the other way around.
