Compiling Asynchronous Market Evidence for LLM Agents}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Large language models and autonomous agents increasingly consume market evidence, yet raw financial feeds are asynchronous, intermittently missing, and quality-heterogeneous. Generative world models assume a usable observation stream; financial agents often lack that stream. We propose a \emph{financial world-model runtime}: a point-in-time compiler that maps raw multi-band evidence into an AI-consumable world state with explicit quality scores and abstention. The primitive object is an epistemic observation \(O_{j,t}=(x,\tau,q,g,r)\); a compilation operator \(\Pi_t\) yields \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t\); world-quality indices (WMI/ACWMI) gate actions. Empirically, on a cryptocurrency PIT panel, vintaged macro and stablecoin-flow \emph{content} changes a transparent consumer rule relative to same-input momentum (\(\Delta\)CE \(0.334\), \(p{=}0.034\)), with leave-one-band-out losses dominated by content rather than gating. A Compiled-versus-Raw consumer protocol tests the agent interface. Trading metrics validate that the compiled world has content; they do not claim a strategy Holy Grail.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} world models, LLM agents, information-set compilation, selective prediction, point-in-time, cryptocurrency

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

AI agents that analyze or trade markets do not observe a clean panel. Venue fragmentation, derivatives surfaces, on-chain flows, news, and macro vintages arrive on incompatible clocks~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022}. Parallel literatures on machine-learning asset pricing~\cite{gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021} and on generative world models~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path} largely assume that observations are already aligned for learning or control. For LLM and tool-using agents~\cite{brown2020gpt3,yao2023react}, the prior systems problem is different: \emph{compile} asynchronous evidence into a world state that can be read, refused when thin, and acted on when ready.

\paragraph{Thesis.}
We build a financial \emph{world-model runtime}: an observation-side world model that compiles raw market evidence into a quality-tagged state for AI consumers. Trading statistics are instruments that show this world has nonempty content---not a claim of unconditional strategy alpha.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(1)~\textbf{Semantics:} epistemic observations, compilation operator \(\Pi_t\), reconstruction bound (delay/noise/missingness), WMI/ACWMI, and world-conditional abstention.
(2)~\textbf{Runtime:} a reproducible PIT multi-band system exposing quality-tagged bundles, availability shocks, and a Compiled-versus-Raw consumer protocol.
(3)~\textbf{Validation:} on a real PIT archive, compiled macro/alternative content yields a significant OOS certainty-equivalent gap versus same-input momentum, with content-dominated LOBO ablations.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models in AI.}
World models learn compact states and (often) dynamics for imagination and control~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path}. We address the complementary \emph{observation-side} problem in markets: constructing a PIT-safe state with explicit quality before any dynamics model or LLM policy is applied.

\paragraph{LLM agents in finance.}
LLM workflows increasingly call tools and act on streams~\cite{yao2023react}. Without a compiled world interface, agents face stale, ungated, or role-incoherent evidence. Our runtime supplies that interface.

\paragraph{Selective prediction.}
Refusing to predict can be optimal under noise~\cite{elyaniv2010,geifman2017}. We tie abstention to world quality rather than classifier confidence alone.

\paragraph{PIT measurement and crypto structure.}
Macro vintages~\cite{croushore2001} and crypto fragmentation~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022} motivate multi-band compilation. Inference uses bootstrap and snooping discipline~\cite{white2000,politis1994,harvey2016}.

\section{Financial World-Model Runtime}
\label{sec:method}

\begin{definition}[Epistemic observation]
An AI-consumable observation is \(O_{j,t}=(x_{j,t},\tau_{j,t},q_{j,t},g_{j,t},r_{j,t})\): value, latest-available time, quality, main-view gate, and semantic role. Identical \(x\) with different \((\tau,q,g,r)\) are different world objects.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Compilation]
Let \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t=\sigma(\{X^{\mathrm{obs}}_{j,\tau}\})\) and \(W_t=\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t)\) with \(\Pi_t=B_t\circ M_t\circ A_t\) (align, missingness/honesty gates, band assembly). The AI filtration is \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\sigma(W_t)\).
\end{definition}

\paragraph{World quality.}
Breadth \(B_t\), stability \(U_t\), and honesty \(H_t\) yield \(\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t U_t H_t\). A geometric ACWMI variant is used for regime-conditional gating. Reconstruction error admits a delay/noise/missingness bound under Lipschitz observation maps (omitted for space).

\begin{proposition}[Compilation \(\neq\) feature expansion]
Enlarging \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}\) without a well-defined \(\Pi_t\) need not enlarge decision-relevant \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}\): stale or role-incoherent evidence can raise raw span while lowering usable world quality via \(H_t\) and \(U_t\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[World-conditional abstention]
If every non-abstain action has expected loss above a world-dependent cost \(c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)\), the Bayes action is abstain. With costs weakly decreasing in WMI, abstention is a lower set in world quality~\cite{elyaniv2010}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{definition}[LOBO content vs.\ gating]
Band \(k\)'s economic value decomposes into a \emph{content} channel (changes the action map) and a \emph{gating} channel (changes readiness/abstention only).
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Scope vs.\ generative WMs]
We do not claim a learned \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\). This paper delivers a \emph{state compiler + abstention runtime}; generative dynamics are orthogonal future work.
\end{remark}

\section{System}
\label{sec:system}

The prototype runtime (anonymized) implements: (i)~multi-band collectors into vintage-aware stores; (ii)~BandPIT reconstruction under a previous-close decision clock---positions earning return \(r_t\) use information known at \((t{-}1)\,23{:}59\); (iii)~readiness, WMI/ACWMI, and paper engine snapshots; (iv)~availability-shock queries \(O_t\); (v)~quality-tagged AI bundles; (vi)~a Compiled-versus-Raw consumer protocol with frozen prompts, temperature \(0\), and action schema \(\{\mathrm{bullish},\mathrm{bearish},\mathrm{neutral},\mathrm{abstain}\}\).

Durable historical bands in the present archive are \(\{\mathrm{exchange},\mathrm{macro},\mathrm{alternative}\}\); news/on-chain/options/tokenomics remain right-censored and are not identifying.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\paragraph{Setup.}
PIT panel: \(\sim\)399 days, 10 liquid crypto names, previous-close clock, IS/OOS split \(200/200\). Pre-specified contrast: \textbf{mechanism (band content) $-$ momentum}, sharing all return-based inputs. Metrics: annualized Sharpe and CRRA CE (\(\gamma{=}2\)). Inference: circular block bootstrap (\(n{=}999\), block \(5\)) with stationary-bootstrap confirmation.

\paragraph{RQ1: Does compiled content matter?}
Ungated mechanism Sharpe/CE \(0.767/0.132\) vs.\ momentum \(0.101/-0.202\). Headline \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}0.334\) (\(p{=}0.034\); stationary \(p{=}0.022\)). Relative gap remains significant at \(10\)--\(25\)bps costs even though absolute mechanism CE turns negative---content value, not a frictionless mandate.

\paragraph{RQ2: Content vs.\ gating.}
Under the ungated mechanism, deleting macro or alternative collapses the rule onto momentum (\(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}-0.334\), \(p{=}0.034\)); content share of LOBO loss is \(1.0\) and gating residual is \(0\). This identifies the content channel of the world state; denser archives are needed for nontrivial gating identification.

\paragraph{RQ3: Agent interface.}
Compiled-versus-Raw offline consumers keep the protocol executable without API keys. We report protocol smoke results and evidence-binding (EAR\({\approx}1\)); we do \emph{not} claim live LLM trading alpha. The estimand is unchanged when live vendors replace mocks.

\paragraph{RQ4: External audit.}
On \(2017\)--\(2026\) without vintaged band archives, the return-based core shows no hidden edge over momentum; joint unsigned content proxies can fire signals without recreating the PIT CE gap. Gains load on compiled PIT content, not a secret return rule.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limit}

The archive is sparse outside exchange/macro/alternative bands; WMI thresholds must be support-matched. Absolute CE is cost-fragile. Consumer tests are protocol-first (mocks). We provide an observation-side world-model runtime, not a full generative dynamics model. Menu-wide superiority versus always-long is not claimed.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

Financial AI agents need a world to read. We formalize and implement a world-model runtime that compiles asynchronous evidence into an analyzable, abstention-aware, tradeable state, and we show that compiled band content is economically nonempty under a disciplined PIT design. Generative dynamics and live multi-vendor LLM consumers are natural next layers on the same interface.
