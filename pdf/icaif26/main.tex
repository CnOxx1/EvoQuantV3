\title{A Market World Model for Public LLMs:\\
Complete, Honest, Auditable Crypto Cognition before Decision}

\begin{abstract}
Most quant stacks start from \emph{strategy}. Public large language models (GPT-, DeepSeek-, GLM-, and Gemini-class agents) that act in cryptocurrency markets instead fail earlier: they lack a complete, honest, auditable \emph{market cognition base}. Raw feeds are asynchronous, intermittently missing, and quality-heterogeneous; ungated tool dumps invite overconfident action. We build a \emph{financial world-model runtime} that compiles multi-band evidence into a point-in-time (PIT) world bundle for public LLMs. The primitive is an epistemic observation \(O_{j,t}=(x,\tau,q,g,r)\); a compilation operator \(\Pi_t\) yields \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t\); completeness, honesty \((B,U,H)\), and WMI/ACWMI gate abstention; evidence IDs make actions auditable. On a real crypto PIT panel, four live public LLMs via an OpenAI-compatible gateway (temperature \(0\), frozen prompts) receive Compiled vs.\ Raw bundles on \(100\) OOS asset-days each: under Compiled, every model abstains on thin worlds (thin-world abstain \(1.0\), EAR proxy \(1.0\)); under Raw, abstain rates fall to \(0.04\)--\(0.75\) and models trade into sparse support. Mean \(\Delta\)CE (Compiled$-$Raw) is \(+1.07\), reported as a secondary content probe---not a strategy Holy Grail. Offline rule consumers reproduce the same refusal pattern when they honor the world model and fail when they do not.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Public LLMs are increasingly asked to analyze or trade crypto markets. The bottleneck is not another alpha signal. It is \emph{understanding}: before decision, an agent needs a market world that is \textbf{complete} (missingness disclosed), \textbf{honest} (stale or role-incoherent evidence gated), and \textbf{auditable} (actions bound to evidence). Venue fragmentation, derivatives, on-chain flows, news, and macro vintages arrive on incompatible clocks~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022}. Generative world models~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path} and tool-using agents~\cite{yao2023react,brown2020gpt3} typically assume a usable observation stream. Crypto agents often do not have one.

\paragraph{Thesis.}
We build a market \emph{world-model runtime} for public LLMs: compile raw multi-band evidence into a PIT-safe cognition bundle that GPT / DeepSeek / GLM / Gemini-class models can call. Strategy metrics are secondary probes that the world has content---not the product objective. In slogan form: \emph{quant from strategy; this runtime from understanding}.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Cognition semantics.} Epistemic observations, compilation \(\Pi_t\), a lag reconstruction bound, completeness/honesty fields, WMI/ACWMI abstention, evidence-bound actions.
\item \textbf{Runtime for public LLMs.} Vintage-aware collectors, BandPIT previous-close clock, quality-tagged bundles, availability shocks \(O_t\), frozen Compiled-versus-Raw protocol with an OpenAI-compatible multi-vendor adapter.
\item \textbf{Live understanding-first validation.} Four live public LLMs abstain when the compiled world is thin and often trade under Raw; a secondary content probe shows vintaged band fields are economically nonempty versus same-input momentum.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models in AI.}
World models learn compact states and often dynamics for control or imagination~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path}. We address the complementary \emph{observation-side} problem: a PIT-safe, quality-tagged crypto world \emph{before} dynamics or LLM policy. We do not claim a Dreamer/JEPA-style generative simulator; we claim the missing systems layer those models usually assume.

\paragraph{LLM agents in finance.}
Agentic workflows call tools over streams~\cite{yao2023react}. Without a compiled cognition base, models face stale or incomplete evidence and overconfident action schemas. We evaluate within-model Compiled vs.\ Raw under a frozen schema so the estimand is the world interface, not a new chat prompt.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and trustworthy AI.}
Abstention can be optimal under noise~\cite{elyaniv2010,geifman2017}. We tie refusal to world quality (WMI/ACWMI), not classifier confidence alone---aligned with trustworthy AI in finance.

\paragraph{PIT measurement and crypto microstructure.}
Macro vintages~\cite{croushore2001} and crypto microstructure~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022} motivate multi-band compilation. Bootstrap and multiple-testing discipline for secondary economic probes follow~\cite{white2000,politis1994,harvey2016}. Asset-pricing ML~\cite{gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021} is adjacent but not our estimand: we validate that the compiled world is nonempty for agents, not that a return factor is priced.

\section{Market World-Model Semantics}
\label{sec:method}

\begin{definition}[Epistemic observation]
An epistemic observation is
\(O_{j,t}=(x_{j,t},\tau_{j,t},q_{j,t},g_{j,t},r_{j,t})\):
value, latest-available time, quality, main-view gate, and semantic role.
A bare feature dump that omits \((\tau,q,g,r)\) is not a world observation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Financial world state]
A financial world state at \(t\) is a quality-tagged, PIT-safe compilation
\(W_t=\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t)\) together with a scalar world quality \(q(W_t)\)
(e.g.\ WMI/ACWMI) that gates abstention; \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\sigma(W_t)\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Compilation]
\(\Pi_t=B_t\circ M_t\circ A_t\): align clocks, apply honesty/missingness gates, assemble band views.
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Completeness, honesty, quality.}
Ready/limited/missing counts disclose coverage. Breadth/stability/honesty give
\(\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t U_t H_t\); ACWMI supports regime-conditional gating. The runtime exposes
\texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} and \texttt{thin\_world} as first-class fields.

\begin{assumption}[Bounded lag and increments]
Each band \(j\) has a maximum observation lag \(\Delta_j\) under the decision clock, and the latent state \(S_t\) has bounded increments \(\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|\le\bar\delta\).
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}[Lag reconstruction bound]
\label{prop:recon}
Under Assumption~1, a reconstruction \(\widetilde S_t\) built from lagged, noisy, and partially missing observations satisfies
\[
\|\widetilde S_t-S_t\|
\;\le\;
\underbrace{C_{\mathrm{del}}\bar\delta}_{\mathrm{delay}}
+\underbrace{C_{\mathrm{noi}}\varepsilon_t}_{\mathrm{noise}}
+\underbrace{C_{\mathrm{miss}}m_t}_{\mathrm{missingness}},
\]
where \(\varepsilon_t\) aggregates observation noise and \(m_t\) aggregates disclosed missing mass. Compilation cannot erase delay; it can only refuse to pretend the bound is zero.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Compilation \(\neq\) feature dump]
\label{prop:compile}
Enlarging raw span without \(\Pi_t\) need not enlarge usable \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}\): ungated stale evidence can raise raw volume while lowering honesty/stability.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[World-conditional abstention]
\label{prop:abstain}
If non-abstain actions exceed a world-dependent cost \(c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)\), Bayes action is abstain; with costs decreasing in WMI, abstention is a lower set in world quality~\cite{elyaniv2010}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[LOBO content vs.\ gating]
\label{prop:lobo}
Deleting band \(j\) changes economic value through a \emph{content} channel (actions change given the same gate) and a \emph{gating} channel (the abstention set changes). Empirically we report content share under an ungated rule so the probe speaks to nonempty fields, not only refusal.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[Not a generative WM]
We do not learn \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\). We deliver a \emph{state compiler + abstention runtime} for public LLM consumers---the observation interface generative world models usually assume.
\end{remark}

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup}
PIT panel \(\sim\)399 days, 10 liquid names, previous-close clock, IS/OOS \(200/200\) days. Live RQ1 uses a stratified OOS sample of \(100\) asset-days per model \(\times\) arm (Compiled/Raw), temperature \(0\), frozen prompts, OpenAI-compatible multi-vendor gateway. Models: \texttt{gpt-5.4-mini}, \texttt{deepseek-v4-flash}, \texttt{glm-5.2}, \texttt{gemini-3.5-flash-lite}. Offline diagnostic consumers (stylized public-LLM follower; compiled-aware / momentum / noisy mocks) reproduce the protocol on the full OOS panel without keys.

\subsection{RQ1 (primary): Do live public LLMs change behavior under the world model?}
Table~\ref{tab:llm} and Figure~\ref{fig:und}: under Compiled, all four live models abstain on every sampled thin-world day (abstain \(1.0\); thin-world abstain \(1.0\); EAR proxy \(1.0\)). Under Raw---same dates/assets, no WMI/ACWMI guidance---abstain rates drop to \(0.04\)--\(0.75\) and models take directional positions into sparse support. Mean \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}\) (Compiled$-$Raw) is \(+1.07\); CE is secondary. The cognition claim is correct refusal when the world is thin (Prop.~\ref{prop:abstain}), not outperformance as a trading Holy Grail.

\subsection{RQ2: Is the compiled world nonempty? (content probe)}
To show the bundle is not an empty label, a transparent rule shares return inputs with momentum and adds vintaged \(\mathrm{macro\_tilt}\) / \(\mathrm{alt\_tilt}\). Pre-specified OOS contrast mechanism $-$ momentum: \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}0.334\) (\(p{=}0.034\); stationary \(p{=}0.022\)). LOBO (Prop.~\ref{prop:lobo}): deleting macro or alternative collapses to momentum (content share \(1.0\) under the ungated rule). Relative gaps survive \(10\)--\(25\)bps costs; absolute CE is cost-fragile. A \(2017\)--\(2026\) audit without vintaged archives finds no hidden return-rule alpha. These results \emph{probe content}, they do not define the product.

\subsection{Discussion}
Quant stacks usually ask ``what trades?'' We ask ``what does the LLM know, and should it act?'' Table~\ref{tab:llm} answers under sparse support with \emph{live} public LLMs: a cognition base that marks the world thin induces abstention across four vendors; raw feeds do not. Table~\ref{tab:econ} answers a weaker but necessary question: compiled band fields are not decorative---they change actions and CE versus same-input momentum. Together they match the product claim: \emph{understanding first, strategy second}.

Sparse archives make production WMI thresholds bind on the sampled OOS window---consistent with support-matched abstention (Prop.~\ref{prop:abstain}). The live result is therefore a strong honesty test (do models refuse when told the world is thin?), not a finished selective-prediction horse-race on dense worlds. Expanding historical band coverage is the natural next measurement step; denser worlds would let ACWMI separate ``act'' from ``abstain'' on a nontrivial fraction of days while keeping the same protocol.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limit}

Live eval uses \(100\) stratified OOS asset-days per model (cost/latency budget); full-panel live replication remains future work. Sparse archives make WMI thresholds bind almost always on the sample---honest under thin support, not a dense-world calibration study. News/on-chain/options history remains right-censored and is disclosed as missing. We do not claim generative dynamics \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\) or unconditional trading alpha. Gateway routing and model IDs are vendor-dependent; prompts and schema are frozen for within-model Compiled vs.\ Raw contrasts.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

Public LLMs need a market to \emph{understand} before they decide. We compile asynchronous crypto evidence into a complete, honest, auditable world-model bundle and show that four live public LLMs abstain when the world is thin, while Raw arms often trade and content probes confirm the compiled state is nonempty. Strategy is downstream of understanding---not the other way around.
