\title{A Market World Model for Public LLMs:\\
Complete, Honest, Auditable Crypto Cognition before Decision}

\begin{abstract}
Most quant stacks start from \emph{strategy}. Public LLMs (GPT-, DeepSeek-, GLM-, and Gemini-class agents) that act in cryptocurrency markets fail earlier: they lack a complete, honest, auditable \emph{market cognition base}. We present an anonymized \emph{financial world-model runtime} that compiles asynchronous multi-band evidence into a point-in-time (PIT) world bundle. Epistemic observations \(O_{j,t}=(x,\tau,q,g,r)\) and compilation \(\Pi_t\) yield \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t\); completeness, honesty \((B,U,H)\), and WMI/ACWMI support a typed abstention contract; evidence IDs make actions auditable. Four live public LLMs (temperature \(0\)) on the \emph{same} \(100\) OOS asset-days per arm show a three-arm behavior ladder: Compiled thin-world abstain \(1.0\); Ungated (same content, no hard flag) mean \(0.68\) (range \(0.43\)--\(0.86\)); Raw \(0.04\)--\(0.75\). Disclosure helps but is vendor-dependent; the typed boolean enforces full cross-vendor refusal, and models that act under Ungated lose money on thin support. A non-trading grounding workflow shows the same asymmetry for analysis: with the compiled three-band bundle, models recover ready/missing sets and tilt signs near-perfectly (mean F1/acc.\ \(0.97\)); from a raw feed the same scores collapse to \(0.04\)--\(0.23\). A within-archive dense-world split shows the content probe is strongest on fully ready days. Economic CE remains a secondary nonempty-content probe---not a strategy Holy Grail. We detail the runtime architecture (collectors, vintage store, BandPIT clock, bundle schema, LLM adapter) as a systems contribution for ICAIF.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Public LLMs are increasingly asked to analyze or trade crypto markets. The bottleneck is not another alpha signal. It is \emph{understanding}: before decision, an agent needs a market world that is \textbf{complete} (missingness disclosed), \textbf{honest} (stale or role-incoherent evidence gated), and \textbf{auditable} (actions bound to evidence). Venue fragmentation, derivatives, on-chain flows, news, and macro vintages arrive on incompatible clocks~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022}. Generative world models~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path} and tool-using finance agents~\cite{yao2023react,yu2023finmem,zhang2024finagent} typically assume a usable observation stream. Crypto agents often do not have one.

\paragraph{Thesis.}
We build a market \emph{world-model runtime} for public LLMs: compile raw multi-band evidence into a PIT-safe cognition bundle that GPT / DeepSeek / GLM / Gemini-class models can call. Strategy metrics are secondary probes that the world has content---not the product objective. In slogan form: \emph{quant from strategy; this runtime from understanding}.

\paragraph{What we claim (and do not).}
RQ1 tests whether a typed world interface can \emph{enforce} world-conditional abstention across live public LLMs---a runtime contract, not a claim that models spontaneously discover thinness from raw ticks. RQ2 tests whether compiled band fields are economically nonempty versus same-input momentum. We do not claim unconditional trading alpha or a Dreamer-style generative simulator.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Cognition semantics.} Epistemic observations, compilation \(\Pi_t\), a lag reconstruction bound, completeness/honesty fields, WMI/ACWMI abstention, evidence-bound actions.
\item \textbf{Anonymized runtime architecture.} Vintage-aware collectors, BandPIT previous-close clock, quality-tagged bundles, availability shocks \(O_t\), OpenAI-compatible multi-vendor adapter, and a frozen Compiled / Ungated / Raw consumer protocol.
\item \textbf{Live three-arm validation.} Four live public LLMs on identical \(100\)-day samples: Compiled enforces thin-world abstain \(1.0\); Ungated soft judgment averages \(0.68\); Raw often trades. A grounding workflow shows compiled worlds make ready/missing/tilt answers verifiable (mean F1/acc.\ \(0.97\) vs.\ \(0.04\)--\(0.23\) raw); a secondary content probe---including a dense-world split---shows vintaged band fields are economically nonempty.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models in AI.}
World models learn compact states and often dynamics~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path}. We address the complementary \emph{observation-side} problem: a PIT-safe, quality-tagged crypto world \emph{before} dynamics or LLM policy. We claim the missing systems layer generative WMs usually assume---not a Dreamer/JEPA simulator.

\paragraph{LLM agents in finance.}
ChatGPT return prediction~\cite{lopez2023chatgpt}, open financial LLMs~\cite{yang2023fingpt}, memory-augmented trading agents~\cite{yu2023finmem,li2023tradinggpt}, and tool-augmented multimodal agents~\cite{zhang2024finagent} advance agent skill. Agentic tool use~\cite{yao2023react} still assumes aligned inputs. We evaluate within-model Compiled vs.\ Ungated vs.\ Raw under a frozen schema so the estimand is the \emph{world interface}, not a new memory module or prompt trick.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and trustworthy AI.}
Abstention can be optimal under noise~\cite{elyaniv2010,geifman2017}. We tie refusal to world quality via an explicit runtime contract, not classifier confidence alone.

\paragraph{PIT measurement and crypto microstructure.}
Macro vintages~\cite{croushore2001} and crypto microstructure~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022} motivate multi-band compilation. Bootstrap discipline for secondary probes follows~\cite{white2000,politis1994,harvey2016}. Asset-pricing ML~\cite{gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021} is adjacent: we validate nonempty compiled content for agents, not priced factors.

\section{Market World-Model Semantics}
\label{sec:method}

\begin{definition}[Epistemic observation]
An epistemic observation is
\[
O_{j,t}=(x_{j,t},\ \tau_{j,t},\ q_{j,t},\ g_{j,t},\ r_{j,t}):
\]
value, latest-available time, quality, main-view gate, semantic role.
A bare feature dump that omits \((\tau,q,g,r)\) is not a world observation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Financial world state]
\(W_t=\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t)\) with scalar quality \(q(W_t)\) (e.g.\ WMI/ACWMI) gating abstention; \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\sigma(W_t)\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Compilation]
\(\Pi_t=B_t\circ M_t\circ A_t\): align clocks, apply honesty/missingness gates, assemble band views.
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Completeness, honesty, quality.}
Ready/limited/missing counts disclose coverage. \(\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t U_t H_t\); ACWMI supports regime-conditional gating. The Compiled contract exposes the abstain flag (\texttt{should\_ai\_abstain}) and \texttt{thin\_world} as first-class fields.

\begin{assumption}[Bounded lag and increments]
Band \(j\) has max observation lag \(\Delta_j\); latent state increments satisfy \(\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|\le\bar\delta\).
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}[Lag reconstruction bound]
\label{prop:recon}
Under Assumption~1,
\[
\|\widetilde S_t-S_t\|
\;\le\;
C_{\mathrm{del}}\bar\delta
+C_{\mathrm{noi}}\varepsilon_t
+C_{\mathrm{miss}}m_t.
\]
Compilation cannot erase delay; it can refuse to pretend the bound is zero.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Intuition.}
Crypto agents that concatenate tool dumps silently absorb delay and missingness into the feature vector. Prop.~\ref{prop:recon} makes those terms explicit: a thicker raw payload can worsen the reconstruction if honesty collapses.

\begin{proposition}[Compilation \(\neq\) feature dump]
\label{prop:compile}
Enlarging raw span without \(\Pi_t\) need not enlarge usable \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}\): ungated stale evidence can raise volume while lowering honesty/stability.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[World-conditional abstention]
\label{prop:abstain}
If non-abstain actions exceed a world-dependent cost \(c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)\), the Bayes action is abstain; with costs decreasing in WMI, abstention forms a lower set in world quality~\cite{elyaniv2010}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[LOBO content vs.\ gating]
\label{prop:lobo}
Deleting band \(j\) changes value through a content channel and a gating channel. Empirically we report content share under an ungated rule so the probe speaks to nonempty fields.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Reading for systems reviewers.}
Props.~\ref{prop:compile}--\ref{prop:abstain} justify why the runtime exports gates and abstain flags rather than only tilts. Prop.~\ref{prop:lobo} justifies the secondary economic probe: if deleting a durable band does not change actions, the bundle is decorative.

\begin{remark}[Not a generative WM]
We do not learn \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\). We deliver a \emph{state compiler + abstention runtime}.
\end{remark}

\section{Runtime Architecture}
\label{sec:system}

We describe the anonymized prototype as a layered systems object (Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline}). Source paths and product names are omitted for double-blind review; an anonymized artifact will be released upon acceptance.

\subsection{Layered design}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Data layer (collectors).} Exchange, macro, and alternative collectors write vintage-aware history: observation timestamps plus macro \texttt{available\_at} vintages.
\item \textbf{Store layer.} Embedded analytical stores hold raw series, merged market panels, and pipeline outputs (readiness, WMI/ACWMI, paper world snapshots).
\item \textbf{Logic layer (compilation).} BandPIT reconstructs band readiness on a previous-close clock; honesty/missingness gates and band assembly implement \(\Pi_t\); world-quality indices and availability shocks \(O_t\) are first-class.
\item \textbf{Service layer.} A read API serves quality-tagged AI bundles and health/readiness objects to consumers without requiring collector restarts for newly arrived rows.
\item \textbf{Consumer layer.} Frozen-prompt LLM / rule agents call an OpenAI-compatible adapter (temperature \(0\)) under Compiled, Ungated, or Raw treatments.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Data flow: inputs, processing, outputs}
Table~\ref{tab:dataflow} traces the runtime end-to-end. \emph{Inputs}: the evaluation archive populates three public bands---exchange klines/funding/open-interest via REST, daily macro risk indices (equity volatility, dollar index) with \texttt{available\_at} vintages, and aggregate stablecoin circulation---each row stored with its observation timestamp. \emph{Processing}: for every asset-day, BandPIT selects the latest observation with timestamp \(\le(t{-}1)\,23{:}59\), grades it fresh/stale/missing against per-band age thresholds, aggregates breadth/stability/honesty into WMI and regime-conditional ACWMI, derives content tilts (5-day macro-index changes; 7-day stablecoin-flow slope; return momentum), and logs availability shocks \(O_t\). \emph{Outputs}: a \(\sim\)3{,}990-row PIT panel (10 assets \(\times\) 399 days), per-day world bundles scoped to these three archive bands (Listing~\ref{lst:bundle}), and the consumer transcripts/tables behind Sections~\ref{sec:exp}.

\subsection{PIT previous-close clock}
For calendar day \(t\), the decision information set is reconstructed at \((t{-}1)\,23{:}59\). The payoff is the same-calendar-day close-to-close return \(r_t\). This removes same-day look-ahead in band statuses and vintaged macro/alternative content. The bundle records the clock in \texttt{decision\_asof}, and every live run pins \texttt{timing\_protocol} to the previous-close protocol.

\subsection{Three information arms and LLM adapter}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Compiled (product contract):} full bundle; frozen prompt \emph{requires} abstain when \texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} is true.
\item \textbf{Ungated (ablation):} same content/completeness/numeric WMI; \emph{no} hard flag; soft prompt asks the model to judge thinness.
\item \textbf{Raw (without world model):} \texttt{mom5} only; no quality index or abstention guidance.
\end{itemize}
Actions \(\in\{\mathrm{bullish},\mathrm{bearish},\mathrm{neutral},\mathrm{abstain}\}\). Understanding metrics prioritize thin-world abstain rate and EAR proxy over CE. The evaluation archive is the three-band set \(\{\mathrm{exchange},\mathrm{macro},\mathrm{alternative}\}\) (Figure~\ref{fig:ready}): macro and alternative stay ready throughout; exchange readiness drops in the later OOS window and defines the within-archive thickness contrast below. The adapter speaks OpenAI-compatible chat completions; model IDs are passed through; credentials stay in environment variables (never in artifacts).

\subsection{Frozen prompt contracts}
Listings~\ref{lst:prompt-c}--\ref{lst:prompt-u} excerpt the frozen Compiled and Ungated contracts (temperature \(0\); action schema identical). The only intentional difference is hard vs.\ soft abstention. Raw prompts omit world-quality language entirely.

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Compiled contract (excerpt).},label={lst:prompt-c}]
Decision rules (excerpt):
1. If world_model_index.should_ai_abstain is true
   OR the world is thin, you MUST "abstain".
2. Prefer band content over raw momentum
   (macro_tilt, alt_tilt are PIT-safe).
3. Rationale must be consistent with evidence_ids.
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Ungated soft contract (excerpt).},label={lst:prompt-u}]
Decision guidance (soft):
1. Judge whether missingness / low WMI/ACWMI
   makes action unsafe; you MAY "abstain".
2. Prefer band content when you act.
NO hard abstain boolean; NO forced policy.
\end{lstlisting}

\paragraph{Not ``just instruction following.''}
Compiled abstention is partly prompt obedience---and that is the \emph{product requirement}: production agents need an enforceable refuse interface when \(q(W_t)\) is low (Prop.~\ref{prop:abstain}). Ungated measures soft judgment from disclosure alone; Raw measures the common ``price signal only'' integration. Reliable Compiled refusal is the SLA; spontaneous Ungated refusal is a bonus.

\subsection{Service surface and reproducibility}
The service layer exposes read-only objects used by the consumer harness: (i)~quality-tagged world bundles; (ii)~band readiness / WMI--ACWMI snapshots; (iii)~availability-shock queries \(O_t\); (iv)~health metadata disclosing whether paper engines or production proxies hydrate ACWMI inputs. Collectors and the logic pipeline can run as supervised jobs; the API reads stores live, so newly compiled rows appear without process bounce. For review we freeze prompts, temperature, action schema, IS/OOS cut, and sampling seed; model IDs and an OpenAI-compatible base URL are configuration, not estimands. Credentials never enter committed artifacts. Upon acceptance we will release an anonymized repository with the consumer harness, frozen prompts, and table-reproduction scripts.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup}
PIT panel \(\sim\)399 days (\(\approx\)13 months), 10 liquid names, previous-close clock, chronological IS/OOS \(200/200\) days. Live RQ1: all three arms run on the \emph{same} stratified \(100\) OOS asset-days (same sampling seed), temperature \(0\), frozen prompts, OpenAI-compatible gateway. Models: \texttt{gpt-5.4-mini}, \texttt{deepseek-v4-flash}, \texttt{glm-5.2}, \texttt{gemini-3.5-flash-lite}. Offline mocks reproduce the protocol on full OOS without keys. Scarce/thin labels bind on OOS by design: primary test is honesty under sparse support, not a dense-world trading horse-race.

\subsection{RQ1 (primary): Three-arm live behavior}
Table~\ref{tab:llm} and Figure~\ref{fig:und} are the headline result; all arms share the same \(100\) OOS asset-days. Under Compiled, all four models abstain on every thin-world day (thin-abs.\ \(1.0\)); rationales cite world fields on \(93\)--\(99\%\) of days. Under Ungated, mean thin-abs.\ falls to \(0.68\) (range \(0.43\)--\(0.86\)): DeepSeek and GLM stay above \(0.80\), GPT drops to \(0.63\), and \texttt{gemini-3.5-flash-lite} falls to \(0.43\) and trades. When Ungated models do act, they lose (CE \(-1.04\) to \(+0.02\) across models)---soft judgment fails exactly where enforcement matters. Under Raw, abstain rates are \(0.04\)--\(0.75\) with substantial directional mass (Table~\ref{tab:mix}). Mean \(\Delta\)CE (Compiled$-$Raw) \(=+1.07\) is reported last: Compiled CE is zero under full abstention, so the gap mainly reflects Raw overtrading---not a Holy Grail.

\paragraph{Inside the Ungated arm.}
Ungated rationales cite disclosed world fields (missingness, readiness, WMI) on \(53\)--\(93\%\) of days, so the models demonstrably \emph{read} the world; they differ in acting on it. Non-abstain Ungated actions are overwhelmingly directional (e.g.\ Gemini: \(55\) bearish, \(2\) bullish of \(57\) trades) and destroy value on thin support---evidence that vendor-specific caution, not lack of disclosure, drives the gap the typed contract closes.

\paragraph{Offline diagnostics.}
World-honoring offline followers abstain at \(1.0\) on full OOS; momentum-only and noisy mocks ignore thin-world flags (thin-abs.\ \(0\))---the Raw failure mode in miniature. Offline results are protocol checks; live vendors are the product claim.

\subsection{RQ3: Non-trading cognition---grounding workflow}
\label{sec:grounding}
The cognition base should serve analysis, not only trade/abstain decisions. We run a live multi-step \emph{grounding workflow} on \(50\) OOS asset-days per arm. Given Compiled or Raw inputs, the model must (i)~list which of \(\{\mathrm{exchange},\mathrm{macro},\mathrm{alternative}\}\) are ready, (ii)~list which are missing, (iii)~report \(\mathrm{sign}(\mathrm{macro\_tilt})\) and \(\mathrm{sign}(\mathrm{alt\_tilt})\), and (iv)~judge sufficiency. Steps (i)--(iii) are scored against PIT ground truth. Table~\ref{tab:grounding}: with the Compiled bundle, mean ready-band F1 \(0.97\), missing-band F1 \(0.97\), and tilt-sign accuracy \(0.97\); with Raw, the same scores collapse to \(0.04\), \(0.23\), and \(0.19\). Sufficiency alone is \emph{not} the discriminator once completeness is scoped to real archive bands---models may call a \(3/3\)-ready day ``sufficient'' while WMI still marks the world thin---so the product claim is \emph{verifiable epistemics}: only the compiled world lets the model say what is ready, what is missing, and which tilts are present, and be checked. This is the analyst-facing counterpart of Prop.~\ref{prop:recon}'s missingness term.

\subsection{Discussion and threats}
The live ladder
\(\mathrm{Abs}_C=1.0 > \mathrm{Abs}_U\approx 0.68 > \mathrm{Abs}_R\in[0.04,0.75]\)
is the product evidence: a cognition base that marks the world thin induces vendor-stable refusal; disclosure alone is incomplete; raw feeds over-act. Cross-vendor heterogeneity under Ungated (GLM \(0.86\) vs.\ Gemini \(0.43\), non-overlapping Wilson CIs) is exactly why a typed contract is valuable in production: soft judgment is model-dependent, while Compiled refusal is stable. RQ2 shows band fields are not decorative. Claim: \emph{understanding first, strategy second}.

Relative to FinMem / FinAgent-style systems~\cite{yu2023finmem,zhang2024finagent}, we do not propose a new memory or tool router. We propose the observation compiler those agents implicitly need when the market feed is asynchronous and quality-heterogeneous. Relative to return-prediction LLM studies~\cite{lopez2023chatgpt}, we score refusal and auditability before PnL.

\paragraph{Threats to validity.}
(1)~\emph{Prompt contract}---Compiled refusal is partly instructed; Ungated is the soft contrast. (2)~\emph{Three-band archive}---evaluation uses exchange/macro/alternative only; within-archive thickness is not a multi-domain dense world. (3)~\emph{Baselines}---Raw matches common thin integrations; Ungated is the same-content control. (4)~\emph{Sample size}---\(100\) live days per arm (Wilson CIs reported); harness supports full \(\sim\)2000-row OOS sweeps. (5)~\emph{Vendor routing}---gateway and model IDs vary; prompts/schema are frozen for within-model contrasts. (6)~\emph{Task scope}---the trading action space is a coarse directional schema; the grounding workflow (Sec.~\ref{sec:grounding}) covers ready/missing/tilts, not full analyst research.

\section{Design Rationale for ICAIF Systems Readers}
\label{sec:design}

Three implementation choices are deliberate.

\paragraph{Export gates, not only features.}
Silent feature stores hide staleness. By placing completeness, honesty, and \texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} in the bundle, the runtime makes refusal a first-class machine-readable event rather than a free-text apology.

\paragraph{Previous-close PIT over wall-clock streaming.}
Intraday streaming demos look impressive but confound look-ahead. The previous-close clock is conservative and auditable: every live decision cites a \texttt{decision\_asof} stamp that a reviewer can recompute from the panel.

\paragraph{Three arms instead of one leaderboard.}
A single Compiled-vs-holdout Sharpe invites Holy-Grail readings. Compiled / Ungated / Raw separates (i)~typed enforcement, (ii)~soft judgment from disclosure, and (iii)~no world model---matching how teams actually integrate LLMs.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limit}

We do not claim generative dynamics or unconditional trading alpha. The evaluation archive covers three public bands (exchange, macro, alternative); richer domains (news, on-chain, options) are out of scope for this study and are not claimed as censored feeds. Dense-world selective-prediction horse-races beyond within-archive thickness remain future work. Live samples are budget-constrained (\(100\) asset-days per arm); the harness already supports full OOS replication. Camera-ready will restore author identity and point to an anonymized repository deferred during review.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

Public LLMs need a market to \emph{understand} before they decide. We contribute cognition semantics and an anonymized layered runtime---collectors, vintage store, BandPIT compilation, bundle contract, and LLM adapter---that turns asynchronous crypto evidence into a complete, honest, auditable world state. Live public LLMs exhibit a three-arm ladder: Compiled enforces thin-world refusal, Ungated partially recovers it from disclosure alone, and Raw often trades into sparse support. The same bundle makes analyst-facing answers verifiable (grounding ready/missing/tilt mean \(0.97\) vs.\ \(0.04\)--\(0.23\) raw), and content probes---including a dense-world split---confirm the compiled state is nonempty. Strategy remains downstream of understanding.
