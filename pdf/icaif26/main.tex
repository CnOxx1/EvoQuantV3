\title{Financial World-Model Runtime: Compiling Asynchronous Market Evidence for LLM Agents}

\begin{abstract}
Large language models and autonomous agents increasingly \emph{consume} market evidence, yet raw financial feeds are asynchronous, intermittently missing, and quality-heterogeneous. Generative world models assume a usable observation stream; financial agents often lack that stream. We propose a \emph{financial world-model runtime}: a point-in-time (PIT) compiler that maps raw multi-band evidence into an AI-consumable world state with explicit quality scores and abstention. The primitive is an epistemic observation \(O_{j,t}=(x,\tau,q,g,r)\); a compilation operator \(\Pi_t\) yields a usable filtration \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t\); world-quality indices (WMI/ACWMI) gate actions. On a cryptocurrency PIT panel, vintaged macro and stablecoin-flow \emph{content} changes a transparent consumer rule relative to same-input momentum (\(\Delta\)CE \(0.334\), \(p{=}0.034\)), with leave-one-band-out (LOBO) losses dominated by content. A Compiled-versus-Raw protocol tests the agent interface. Trading metrics validate that the compiled world has content; they do not claim a strategy Holy Grail.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

AI agents that analyze or trade markets do not observe a clean panel. Venue fragmentation, derivatives surfaces, on-chain flows, news, and macro vintages arrive on incompatible clocks~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022}. Literatures on machine-learning asset pricing~\cite{gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021} and on generative world models~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path} largely assume that observations are already aligned for learning or control. For LLM and tool-using agents~\cite{brown2020gpt3,yao2023react}, the prior systems problem is different: \emph{compile} asynchronous evidence into a world state that can be analyzed, refused when thin, and acted on when ready.

\paragraph{Thesis.}
We build a financial \emph{world-model runtime}: an \emph{observation-side} world model that compiles raw market evidence into a quality-tagged state for AI consumers. Trading statistics are instruments that show this world has nonempty content---not a claim of unconditional strategy alpha, and not a Dreamer-style learned dynamics model~\cite{hafner2020dreamer}.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Semantics.} Epistemic observations, compilation operator \(\Pi_t\), a delay/noise/missingness reconstruction bound, WMI/ACWMI, world-conditional abstention, and a LOBO content/gating decomposition.
\item \textbf{Runtime.} A reproducible PIT multi-band system exposing quality-tagged bundles, availability shocks \(O_t\), and a Compiled-versus-Raw consumer protocol (frozen prompts, temperature 0, abstain allowed).
\item \textbf{Validation.} On a real PIT archive, compiled macro/alternative content yields a significant OOS certainty-equivalent (CE) gap versus same-input momentum, with content-dominated LOBO ablations and a long-span audit ruling out hidden return-rule alpha.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models in AI.}
World models learn compact states and often dynamics for imagination and control~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path}. We address the complementary problem in markets: constructing a PIT-safe, quality-tagged state \emph{before} any dynamics model or LLM policy is applied.

\paragraph{LLM agents in finance.}
LLM workflows call tools and act on streams~\cite{yao2023react,brown2020gpt3}. Without a compiled world interface, agents face stale, ungated, or role-incoherent evidence. Our runtime supplies that interface; we evaluate a protocol, not live vendor alpha.

\paragraph{Selective prediction.}
Refusing to predict can be optimal under noise~\cite{elyaniv2010,geifman2017}. We tie abstention to world quality rather than classifier confidence alone.

\paragraph{PIT measurement and crypto structure.}
Macro vintages~\cite{croushore2001} and crypto fragmentation~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022} motivate multi-band compilation. Inference uses bootstrap and snooping discipline~\cite{white2000,politis1994,harvey2016}.

\section{Financial World-Model Runtime}
\label{sec:method}

\begin{definition}[Epistemic observation]
An AI-consumable observation is
\[
O_{j,t}=(x_{j,t},\tau_{j,t},q_{j,t},g_{j,t},r_{j,t}),
\]
with value, latest-available time, quality, main-view gate, and semantic role. Identical \(x\) with different \((\tau,q,g,r)\) are different world objects.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Compilation operator]
Let \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t=\sigma(\{X^{\mathrm{obs}}_{j,\tau}\})\). The compiler is \(\Pi_t=B_t\circ M_t\circ A_t\) (align, missingness/honesty gates, band assembly), the world state is \(W_t=\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t)\), and \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\sigma(W_t)\).
\end{definition}

\paragraph{World quality.}
Let \(a_{k,t}\in\{0,1\}\) indicate whether band \(k\) meets an AI-usable threshold among \(K\) critical bands, \(d_{j,t}\) a freshness penalty, and \(m_{j,t}\) an incorrect main-view injection. Define
\begin{align}
B_t&=\tfrac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^{K}a_{k,t},\qquad
U_t=\exp\!\Bigl(-\sum_{j}\omega_j d_{j,t}\Bigr),\nonumber\\
H_t&=1-\tfrac{1}{J}\sum_{j}m_{j,t},\qquad
\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t\,U_t\,H_t.
\label{eq:wmi}
\end{align}
A geometric ACWMI aggregates readiness factors with cascade/regime context for selective gating. Production thresholds are frozen on an in-sample grid and never retuned out of sample.

\paragraph{Reconstruction bound.}
Latent states evolve as \(S_{t+1}=F(S_t,\eta_{t+1})\); source \(j\) observes a lagged map \(X^{\mathrm{obs}}_{j,t}=h_j(S_{t-\ell_{j,t}})+\nu_{j,t}\). Under Lipschitz \(h_j\) and bounded increments, reconstruction error admits
\begin{equation}
\|\tilde S_t-S_t\|
\le C_1\sum_j\omega_j\ell_{j,t}
+C_2\sum_j\omega_j\|\nu_{j,t}\|
+C_3\sum_j\omega_j(1-z_{j,t}),
\label{eq:bound}
\end{equation}
with availability \(z_{j,t}\). Latest-snapshot, TTL, and readiness gates shrink the three terms.

\begin{proposition}[Compilation \(\neq\) feature expansion]
\label{prop:compile}
Enlarging \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}\) without a well-defined \(\Pi_t\) need not enlarge decision-relevant \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}\): stale or role-incoherent evidence can raise raw span while lowering usable world quality via \(H_t\) and \(U_t\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[World-conditional abstention]
\label{prop:abstain}
With action set including \(\mathrm{abstain}\), if every non-abstain action has expected loss above a world-dependent cost \(c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)\), the Bayes action is abstain. With costs weakly decreasing in \(\mathrm{WMI}\), abstention is a lower set in world quality~\cite{elyaniv2010}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{definition}[LOBO content vs.\ gating]
\label{def:lobo}
Let \(V(I)\) be OOS CE of a fixed action rule given information \(I\). Band \(k\)'s value \(\widehat{\mathrm{MIG}}_k=V(I)-V(I\setminus E_k)\) decomposes into a \emph{content} channel (changes the action map) and a \emph{gating} channel (changes readiness/abstention only). Empirically we report a telescoping identity under the headline ungated rule.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Scope vs.\ generative WMs]
We do \emph{not} claim a learned \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\). This paper delivers a \emph{state compiler + abstention runtime}, complementary to generative world models that assume a clean observation stream~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer}.
\end{remark}

\section{System}
\label{sec:system}

Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline} summarizes the anonymized prototype. Collectors ingest exchange, macro, alternative, and (when available) news/on-chain/options/tokenomics series into vintage-aware stores. A BandPIT service reconstructs band readiness under a \emph{previous-close} decision clock: positions earning calendar-day return \(r_t\) use only information known at \((t{-}1)\,23{:}59\). Content features are likewise vintaged with \(\texttt{available\_at}\le t\) and then lagged one day. The runtime computes readiness, WMI/ACWMI, availability shocks \(O_t\), and quality-tagged AI bundles consumed by rule-based or LLM agents.

\paragraph{Runtime loop (conceptual).}
At each decision clock: pull latest PIT-safe observations \(\{O_{j,t}\}\) \(\to\) update band readiness and ages \(\to\) compute \((B,U,H,\mathrm{WMI},\mathrm{ACWMI})\) and content tilts \(\to\) emit world bundle \(W_t\) \(\to\) consumer returns \(a_t\in\{\pm 1,0,\mathrm{abstain}\}\) \(\to\) log evidence bindings. This loop is the system contribution: a silent feature store without quality, abstention, and Compiled/Raw contrast is not a world-model runtime.

\paragraph{Compiled-versus-Raw protocol.}
For consumer \(m\),
\[
\Delta_m = V_m(\mathrm{Compiled}) - V_m(\mathrm{Raw}),
\]
with frozen prompts, temperature \(0\), and action schema \(\{\mathrm{bullish},\mathrm{bearish},\mathrm{neutral},\mathrm{abstain}\}\). Offline mocks keep the protocol executable without API keys; live vendors may replace mocks without changing the estimand. No live LLM trading alpha is claimed.

Durable historical bands in the present archive are \(\{\mathrm{exchange},\mathrm{macro},\mathrm{alternative}\}\) with empirical ready rates approximately \(0.80/1.00/1.00\); news/on-chain/options/tokenomics remain right-censored and are not identifying.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup}
PIT panel: \(\sim\)399 days, 10 liquid crypto names, previous-close clock, IS/OOS \(200/200\). Metrics: annualized Sharpe and CRRA CE (\(\gamma{=}2\)). Inference: circular block bootstrap (\(n{=}999\), block length 5) with stationary-bootstrap confirmation~\cite{politis1994}.

\paragraph{Transparent content rule.}
Band content enters the action map directly (not only as a gate). PIT-safe tilts use vintaged \texttt{available\_at}:
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{macro\_tilt}_t &=
\begin{cases}
+1 & \Delta_5\mathrm{VIX}<0 \wedge \Delta_5\mathrm{DXY}<0\ \text{(risk-on)},\\
-1 & \Delta_5\mathrm{VIX}>0 \wedge \Delta_5\mathrm{DXY}>0\ \text{(risk-off)},\\
0 & \text{otherwise or band not ready,}
\end{cases}\\
\mathrm{alt\_tilt}_t &= \mathrm{sign}(\text{stablecoin 7d net supply change}),\ 0\text{ if not ready.}
\end{align*}
The deterministic rule shares all return-based inputs with momentum and adds content:
\begin{align*}
\text{(R1)}&\ \mathrm{crisis}\wedge\mathrm{cascade\_p}\ge 0.60\Rightarrow -1,\\
\text{(R2)}&\ \mathrm{trend}\wedge\mathrm{mom5}>0\wedge\mathrm{cascade\_p}<0.45\wedge\mathrm{macro\_tilt}\ge 0\Rightarrow +1,\\
\text{(R2b)}&\ \mathrm{range}\wedge\mathrm{macro\_tilt}>0\wedge\mathrm{alt\_tilt}>0\wedge\mathrm{mom5}\ge 0\Rightarrow +1,\\
\text{(R3)}&\ \text{else }\mathrm{sign}(\mathrm{mom5}),\ \text{long vetoed when both tilts risk-off.}
\end{align*}
A zero signal is an intentional stand-aside. Because tilts are forced to zero when their band is not PIT-ready, LOBO deletes \emph{content}, not only gating. The single pre-specified OOS contrast is \textbf{mechanism $-$ momentum}.

\subsection{RQ1: Does compiled content matter?}
Table~\ref{tab:econ} reports the OOS horse-race. Always-long fails in this window (CE \(-1.157\)); momentum is nearly flat (Sharpe \(0.101\), CE \(-0.202\)); the content-aware rule earns Sharpe \(0.767\) / CE \(0.132\), standing aside on \(7.5\%\) of asset-days via content-driven vetoes. IS-frozen ACWMI is secondary (Sharpe \(0.654\), CE \(0.076\)). Headline \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}0.334\) (\(p{=}0.034\); stationary \(p{=}0.022\); 95\% CI \([0.03,0.68]\) excludes zero). At \(10\)bps, \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}0.333\) (\(p{=}0.034\)) even though mechanism CE turns slightly negative (\(-0.019\)); at \(25\)bps, \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}0.332\) (\(p{=}0.038\)). Absolute profitability is therefore fragile; relative content value is not (Figure~\ref{fig:cost}). The gap is stable across \(\gamma\in\{1,2,4,6\}\) and a leave-one-asset jackknife.

\subsection{RQ2: Content vs.\ gating (LOBO)}
Under the ungated mechanism, deleting macro or alternative collapses the rule onto momentum (\(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}-0.334\), \(p{=}0.034\)); content share of LOBO loss is \(1.0\) and gating residual is \(0\) (Table~\ref{tab:lobo}, Figure~\ref{fig:lobo}). Exchange status deletion costs \(0\) under the ungated rule. Band information changes \emph{what} is traded, not only \emph{when}. Nontrivial gating identification requires denser archives where abstention binds.

\subsection{RQ4: External audit}
On \(2017\)--\(2026\) without vintaged band archives, the return-based core shows no hidden edge over momentum. Injecting joint \emph{unsigned} content proxies (Yahoo VIX/DXY + DefiLlama stablecoin supply change) can change \(157\) of \(6630\) signals, but does \emph{not} recreate the PIT CE gap versus no-content (\(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{\approx}-0.022\), \(p{=}0.40\)). Macro-only proxies change zero signals, confirming two-band conjunction. Gains in Tables~\ref{tab:econ}--\ref{tab:lobo} load on compiled PIT content, not a secret return predictor.

\subsection{Discussion}
Read the tables as \emph{content validation for the world-model runtime}, not as a mandate to trade frictionlessly. Enlarging usable \(I_t\) by vintaged macro/alternative content changes actions and raises OOS CE relative to same-input momentum (Props.~\ref{prop:compile}, Def.~\ref{def:lobo}). LOBO's content channel dominates gating under the ungated rule---partly by construction when content deletion collapses to momentum; that is exactly the content channel the runtime must expose. The agent protocol (Table~\ref{tab:llm}) shows that Compiled versus Raw can change abstention behavior even for offline consumers. Menu-wide superiority versus always-long is \emph{not} established (reality-check \(p{=}0.144\)); we do not claim it.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limit}

News/on-chain/options/tokenomics lack durable history; full multi-band identification is future work. Absolute CE is cost-fragile. Consumer tests are protocol-first (mocks); live multi-vendor LLM evaluations remain open. WMI thresholds must be support-matched on sparse archives---production \(\mathrm{WMI}{<}0.2\) abstains essentially always here, consistent with Prop.~\ref{prop:abstain} but not a completed selective-prediction horse-race. We provide an observation-side world-model runtime, not a full generative dynamics model \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\).

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

Financial AI agents need a world to read. We formalize and implement a world-model runtime that compiles asynchronous evidence into an analyzable, abstention-aware, tradeable state \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\sigma(\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t))\), and show that compiled band content is economically nonempty under a disciplined PIT design. Trading numbers prove content; they do not proclaim a strategy Holy Grail. Generative dynamics and live multi-vendor LLM consumers are natural next layers on the same interface.
