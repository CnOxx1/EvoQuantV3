\title{A Market World Model for Public LLMs:\\
Complete, Honest, Auditable Crypto Cognition before Decision}

\begin{abstract}
Most quant stacks start from \emph{strategy}. Public large language models (GPT-, DeepSeek-, GLM-, and Gemini-class agents) that act in cryptocurrency markets instead fail earlier: they lack a complete, honest, auditable \emph{market cognition base}. Raw feeds are asynchronous, intermittently missing, and quality-heterogeneous; ungated tool dumps invite overconfident action. We build a \emph{financial world-model runtime} that compiles multi-band evidence into a point-in-time (PIT) world bundle for public LLMs. The primitive is an epistemic observation \(O_{j,t}=(x,\tau,q,g,r)\); a compilation operator \(\Pi_t\) yields \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t\); completeness, honesty \((B,U,H)\), and WMI/ACWMI gate abstention; evidence IDs make actions auditable. On a real crypto PIT panel, four live public LLMs via an OpenAI-compatible gateway (temperature \(0\), frozen prompts) receive Compiled vs.\ Raw bundles on \(100\) OOS asset-days each. Under the typed Compiled contract, every model abstains on thin worlds (thin-world abstain \(1.0\)); under Raw, abstain rates fall to \(0.04\)--\(0.75\) and models take directional bets into sparse support. An Ungated ablation (same content fields, numeric WMI, \emph{no} hard abstain flag; \(50\) days) yields mean thin-world abstain \(0.74\) (range \(0.38\)--\(0.94\))---disclosure helps, but the typed boolean is what enforces full refusal. Mean \(\Delta\)CE (Compiled$-$Raw) is secondary---not a strategy Holy Grail.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Public LLMs are increasingly asked to analyze or trade crypto markets. The bottleneck is not another alpha signal. It is \emph{understanding}: before decision, an agent needs a market world that is \textbf{complete} (missingness disclosed), \textbf{honest} (stale or role-incoherent evidence gated), and \textbf{auditable} (actions bound to evidence). Venue fragmentation, derivatives, on-chain flows, news, and macro vintages arrive on incompatible clocks~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022}. Generative world models~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path} and tool-using agents~\cite{yao2023react,brown2020gpt3} typically assume a usable observation stream. Crypto agents often do not have one.

\paragraph{Thesis.}
We build a market \emph{world-model runtime} for public LLMs: compile raw multi-band evidence into a PIT-safe cognition bundle that GPT / DeepSeek / GLM / Gemini-class models can call. Strategy metrics are secondary probes that the world has content---not the product objective. In slogan form: \emph{quant from strategy; this runtime from understanding}.

\paragraph{What we claim (and do not).}
RQ1 tests whether a typed world interface can \emph{enforce} world-conditional abstention across live public LLMs---a runtime contract, not a claim that models spontaneously discover thinness from raw ticks. RQ2 tests whether compiled band fields are economically nonempty versus same-input momentum. We do not claim unconditional trading alpha or a Dreamer-style generative simulator.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Cognition semantics.} Epistemic observations, compilation \(\Pi_t\), a lag reconstruction bound, completeness/honesty fields, WMI/ACWMI abstention, evidence-bound actions.
\item \textbf{Runtime for public LLMs.} Vintage-aware collectors, BandPIT previous-close clock, quality-tagged bundles, availability shocks \(O_t\), frozen Compiled / Ungated / Raw protocol with an OpenAI-compatible multi-vendor adapter.
\item \textbf{Live understanding-first validation.} Four live public LLMs abstain under the Compiled contract when the world is thin and often trade under Raw; an Ungated ablation removes the hard abstain flag; a secondary content probe shows vintaged band fields are economically nonempty.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models in AI.}
World models learn compact states and often dynamics for control or imagination~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path}. We address the complementary \emph{observation-side} problem: a PIT-safe, quality-tagged crypto world \emph{before} dynamics or LLM policy. We do not claim a Dreamer/JEPA-style generative simulator; we claim the missing systems layer those models usually assume.

\paragraph{LLM agents in finance.}
Recent work builds LLM trading agents and finance-tuned models---return prediction with ChatGPT~\cite{lopez2023chatgpt}, open financial LLMs~\cite{yang2023fingpt}, memory-augmented trading agents~\cite{yu2023finmem,li2023tradinggpt}, and tool-augmented multimodal trading agents~\cite{zhang2024finagent}. Agentic workflows call tools over streams~\cite{yao2023react}. These systems typically assume the observation stream is already aligned and trustworthy. We evaluate within-model Compiled vs.\ Raw (and Ungated) under a frozen schema so the estimand is the \emph{world interface}, not a new chat prompt or memory module.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and trustworthy AI.}
Abstention can be optimal under noise~\cite{elyaniv2010,geifman2017}. We tie refusal to world quality (WMI/ACWMI) via an explicit runtime contract, not classifier confidence alone.

\paragraph{PIT measurement and crypto microstructure.}
Macro vintages~\cite{croushore2001} and crypto microstructure~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022} motivate multi-band compilation. Bootstrap discipline for secondary economic probes follows~\cite{white2000,politis1994,harvey2016}. Asset-pricing ML~\cite{gu2020,kelly2019,nagel2021} is adjacent but not our estimand: we validate that the compiled world is nonempty for agents, not that a return factor is priced.

\section{Market World-Model Semantics}
\label{sec:method}

\begin{definition}[Epistemic observation]
An epistemic observation is
\(O_{j,t}=(x_{j,t},\tau_{j,t},q_{j,t},g_{j,t},r_{j,t})\):
value, latest-available time, quality, main-view gate, and semantic role.
A bare feature dump that omits \((\tau,q,g,r)\) is not a world observation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Financial world state]
A financial world state at \(t\) is a quality-tagged, PIT-safe compilation
\(W_t=\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t)\) together with a scalar world quality \(q(W_t)\)
(e.g.\ WMI/ACWMI) that gates abstention; \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\sigma(W_t)\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Compilation]
\(\Pi_t=B_t\circ M_t\circ A_t\): align clocks, apply honesty/missingness gates, assemble band views.
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Completeness, honesty, quality.}
Ready/limited/missing counts disclose coverage. Breadth/stability/honesty give
\(\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t U_t H_t\); ACWMI supports regime-conditional gating. The runtime exposes
\texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} and \texttt{thin\_world} as first-class fields in the Compiled contract.

\begin{assumption}[Bounded lag and increments]
Each band \(j\) has a maximum observation lag \(\Delta_j\) under the decision clock, and the latent state \(S_t\) has bounded increments \(\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|\le\bar\delta\).
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}[Lag reconstruction bound]
\label{prop:recon}
Under Assumption~1, a reconstruction \(\widetilde S_t\) built from lagged, noisy, and partially missing observations satisfies
\[
\|\widetilde S_t-S_t\|
\;\le\;
\underbrace{C_{\mathrm{del}}\bar\delta}_{\mathrm{delay}}
+\underbrace{C_{\mathrm{noi}}\varepsilon_t}_{\mathrm{noise}}
+\underbrace{C_{\mathrm{miss}}m_t}_{\mathrm{missingness}},
\]
where \(\varepsilon_t\) aggregates observation noise and \(m_t\) aggregates disclosed missing mass. Compilation cannot erase delay; it can only refuse to pretend the bound is zero.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Compilation \(\neq\) feature dump]
\label{prop:compile}
Enlarging raw span without \(\Pi_t\) need not enlarge usable \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}\): ungated stale evidence can raise raw volume while lowering honesty/stability.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[World-conditional abstention]
\label{prop:abstain}
If non-abstain actions exceed a world-dependent cost \(c_{\mathrm{abs}}(W_t)\), Bayes action is abstain; with costs decreasing in WMI, abstention is a lower set in world quality~\cite{elyaniv2010}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[LOBO content vs.\ gating]
\label{prop:lobo}
Deleting band \(j\) changes economic value through a \emph{content} channel (actions change given the same gate) and a \emph{gating} channel (the abstention set changes). Empirically we report content share under an ungated rule so the probe speaks to nonempty fields, not only refusal.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[Not a generative WM]
We do not learn \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\). We deliver a \emph{state compiler + abstention runtime} for public LLM consumers---the observation interface generative world models usually assume.
\end{remark}

\section{Runtime for Public LLMs}
\label{sec:system}

Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline} shows the anonymized prototype. Collectors fill vintage-aware stores; BandPIT reconstructs readiness at \((t{-}1)\,23{:}59\) for payoff \(r_t\) (previous-close decision clock; no same-day look-ahead). The runtime emits a world bundle consumed by rule or LLM agents via frozen prompts (temperature \(0\)). An OpenAI-compatible adapter routes the same schema to GPT / DeepSeek / GLM / Gemini-class endpoints; offline stylized followers keep the protocol reproducible without keys. Availability shocks \(O_t\) are queryable from collection logs as a quasi-exogenous lever on world thickness.

\paragraph{Three information arms.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Compiled (product contract):} full bundle including hard \texttt{should\_ai\_abstain}; frozen prompt requires abstain when the flag is true.
\item \textbf{Ungated (ablation):} same content, completeness, honesty, and numeric WMI/ACWMI, but \emph{no} hard abstain boolean and no forced policy---the model must judge thinness itself.
\item \textbf{Raw (without world model):} thin feed (\texttt{mom5} only); no quality index, no band roles, no abstention guidance.
\end{itemize}
Understanding metrics prioritize thin-world abstain rate and evidence-audit (EAR) proxy over CE. Durable bands in the archive: \(\{\mathrm{exchange},\mathrm{macro},\mathrm{alternative}\}\) (ready rates \(\approx 0.80/1/1\)); news/on-chain/options are right-censored and disclosed as missing. Actions are restricted to
\(\{\mathrm{bullish},\mathrm{bearish},\mathrm{neutral},\mathrm{abstain}\}\).

\paragraph{Why this is not ``just instruction following.''}
A fair objection is that Compiled abstention is prompt obedience. We treat that as a \emph{feature of the runtime contract}: production agents need an enforceable refuse interface when \(q(W_t)\) is low (Prop.~\ref{prop:abstain}). The scientific contrast is then (i) Raw agents that lack any world quality signal and over-act, and (ii) Ungated agents that see the same disclosed world but must refuse without a boolean. Spontaneous refusal under Ungated is a bonus; reliable refusal under Compiled is the product requirement.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup}
PIT panel \(\sim\)399 days (\(\approx\)13 months), 10 liquid names, previous-close clock, IS/OOS \(200/200\) days. Live RQ1 uses a stratified OOS sample of \(100\) asset-days per model for Compiled/Raw (temperature \(0\), frozen prompts, OpenAI-compatible multi-vendor gateway). Models: \texttt{gpt-5.4-mini}, \texttt{deepseek-v4-flash}, \texttt{glm-5.2}, \texttt{gemini-3.5-flash-lite}. Ungated ablation uses the same protocol on a stratified \(50\)-day subsample (same seed). Offline diagnostics (stylized public-LLM follower; compiled-aware / momentum / noisy mocks) reproduce the protocol on the full OOS panel without keys. On this archive, scarce/thin labels bind on the OOS window by design---the primary test is honesty under sparse support, not a dense-world trading horse-race.

\subsection{RQ1 (primary): Does the world-model contract change live LLM behavior?}
Table~\ref{tab:llm} and Figure~\ref{fig:und}: under Compiled, all four live models abstain on every sampled thin-world day (thin-world abstain \(1.0\); EAR proxy \(1.0\)). Rationales cite world fields (\texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} / missingness / readiness) on \(93\)--\(99\%\) of Compiled days. Under Raw---same dates/assets, no WMI guidance---abstain rates drop to \(0.04\)--\(0.75\) and models take directional positions (Table~\ref{tab:mix}). Mean \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}\) (Compiled$-$Raw) is \(+1.07\); we report it last because Compiled CE is zero by construction under full abstention---the number mainly reflects Raw overtrading losses, not a Holy Grail.

\subsection{RQ2: Is the compiled world nonempty? (content probe)}
To show the bundle is not an empty label, a transparent rule shares return inputs with momentum and adds vintaged \(\mathrm{macro\_tilt}\) / \(\mathrm{alt\_tilt}\). Pre-specified OOS contrast mechanism $-$ momentum: \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}0.334\) (\(p{=}0.034\); stationary \(p{=}0.022\)). LOBO (Prop.~\ref{prop:lobo}): deleting macro or alternative collapses to momentum (content share \(1.0\) under the ungated rule). Relative gaps survive \(10\)--\(25\)bps costs; absolute CE is cost-fragile. A \(2017\)--\(2026\) audit without vintaged archives finds no hidden return-rule alpha. These results \emph{probe content}, they do not define the product.

\subsection{Discussion}
Quant stacks usually ask ``what trades?'' We ask ``what does the LLM know, and should it act?'' The live three-arm ladder on this sparse archive is:
\[
\mathrm{Abs}_{\mathrm{Compiled}}=1.0
\;>\;
\mathrm{Abs}_{\mathrm{Ungated}}\approx 0.74
\;>\;
\mathrm{Abs}_{\mathrm{Raw}}\in[0.04,0.75].
\]
Compiled delivers a vendor-stable refuse interface; Ungated shows that completeness/WMI disclosure alone recovers most---but not all---refusal (Gemini drops to \(0.38\)); Raw shows what happens without a world model. Table~\ref{tab:econ} then shows compiled band fields are not decorative labels. Together: \emph{understanding first, strategy second}.

\paragraph{Threats to validity.}
(1)~\emph{Prompt contract.} Compiled abstention is partly instructed; we disclose this and use Ungated as the soft-judgment contrast rather than hiding it. (2)~\emph{Thin archive.} Scarce rate \(\approx 1\) on OOS means we stress honesty, not dense-world calibration. (3)~\emph{Raw baseline strength.} Raw is intentionally thin (momentum-only), matching a common ``just give the model a price signal'' integration; Ungated is the stronger same-content control. (4)~\emph{Sample size.} Live Compiled/Raw uses \(100\) asset-days; Ungated \(50\); a full \(\sim\)2000-row OOS sweep is supported by the harness when budget allows and would mainly tighten intervals under current scarcity.

Expanding historical band coverage is the natural next measurement step: denser worlds would let ACWMI separate ``act'' from ``abstain'' on a nontrivial fraction of days while keeping the same three-arm protocol.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limit}

Live Compiled/Raw uses \(100\) stratified OOS asset-days; Ungated uses \(50\). Full-panel live replication remains future work. Sparse archives make WMI thresholds bind almost always on the sample---honest under thin support, not a dense-world calibration study. Compiled prompts include an explicit abstain rule when \texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} is true; we report this contract openly and use Ungated as the soft-judgment contrast. News/on-chain/options history remains right-censored and is disclosed as missing. We do not claim generative dynamics \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\) or unconditional trading alpha. Gateway routing and model IDs are vendor-dependent; prompts and schema are frozen for within-model contrasts.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

Public LLMs need a market to \emph{understand} before they decide. We compile asynchronous crypto evidence into a complete, honest, auditable world-model bundle and show that four live public LLMs abstain under the Compiled contract when the world is thin, while Raw arms often trade; Ungated ablation separates typed enforcement from soft judgment; content probes confirm the compiled state is nonempty. Strategy is downstream of understanding---not the other way around.
