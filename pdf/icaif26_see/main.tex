\title[See First, Refuse When Thin]{%
  See First, Refuse When Thin:\\
  Compiling Point-in-Time World Bundles\\
  for LLM Agents in Crypto Markets}

\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Purpose.} In AI-based finance, the performance frontier is often set less by model complexity than by whether the market world fed to the model is broad, stable, and honest.
We treat the missing piece as a \emph{data end}: an anonymized observation runtime that compiles multi-band crypto evidence into a point-in-time (PIT) \emph{world bundle} (BandPIT/WMI) with hard refusal when support is thin---infrastructure for LLM/agent market analysis and for evaluating whether models \emph{understand} that world, not a trading engine.
\textbf{Evidence.} Primary---seeing: grounding recovers ready/missing/tilt at \({\approx}0.97\) under Compiled vs.\ \({\lesssim}0.23\) under Raw, while sufficiency chat alone is unreliable (Compiled mean \(0.60\)).
Secondary---valve: under a \emph{frozen full-schema} stress gate (\(\max\mathrm{WMI}\approx 0.093{<}0.2\)), Compiled thin-abstain is \(1.0\) (100-day and full OOS \(n{=}2000\)) while Ungated soft judgment is only \({\approx}0.68\) / \({\approx}0.56\); Raw over-trades.
Secondary---handoff: the \emph{scoped-archive production valve} opens on \({\approx}61\%\) OOS days; live LLMs abstain \(1.0\) on closed days and act on \({\approx}89\%\) of open days, while a no-LLM tilt rule remains nonempty (Sharpe \(0.77\)).
\textbf{Claim.} First, give models a world; then ask them to decide. We move from model-first to data-end-first: compile so models can see; refuse when they must not act.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\paragraph{Purpose (read this first)}
At the methodology--application interface of AI and finance, many LLM/agent failures are not ``the model is not smart enough,'' but ``the market worldview is too thin.''
The stack is \textbf{data/observation} \(\to\) \textbf{decision} \(\to\) \textbf{execution} (Table~\ref{tab:stack}); agents and generative world models~\cite{yao2023react,yu2023finmem,zhang2024finagent,ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path} typically assume the first stage.
Crypto violates that assumption: exchange, macro, and alternative evidence arrive on incompatible clocks~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022}.
Stress episodes with multi-hour exchange outages make the gap concrete: an agent that sees only lagged momentum can act while the observation set is incomplete.
We build the missing substrate---compile a PIT world bundle so models can \emph{see} the market, evaluate that seeing with checkable grounding, and hard-refuse when the view is too thin.
The product is reduced world-model error and auditable AI judgments, not trading answers.

\paragraph{Failure modes}
No feed \(\to\) refuse (safe, useless as a data end).
Thin price fragment \(\to\) trade and lose (unsafe for any downstream agent).
Disclosed but ungated world \(\to\) read fields, disagree on action.
These are not model failures; they are interface failures.
So the data end must (i)~make the market legible to LLMs/agents and (ii)~enforce a quality valve before decision/execution.

\paragraph{What we build}
An anonymized \emph{data-provision runtime for public LLMs and finance agents} (Section~\ref{sec:system}): multi-band evidence \(\to\) BandPIT/WMI \(\to\) JSON world bundle for GPT~/DeepSeek~/GLM~/Gemini-class consumers, served through a read API.
It is also an evaluation substrate: the same bundle supports grounding and gated-action protocols that test whether a model understands disclosed market state.
``World model'' means state compiler + quality gate (Section~\ref{sec:method}), not dynamics and not a decision engine.
The bundle is complete, honest, and auditable; the hard abstain flag is the machine-readable valve.

\paragraph{How we evaluate (dual protocol)}
\textbf{RQ1 (primary---seeing):} Are ready~/missing~/tilt answers verifiable against PIT ground truth under Compiled vs.\ Raw?
\textbf{RQ2 (valve):} Under a \emph{frozen full-schema stress gate} (never clears \(0.2\)), does hard policy enforce thin abstention where soft MAY does not?
Arms: Compiled, HPO (hard prompt on numeric WMI, no boolean), Ungated, Raw, Blind.
\textbf{RQ3a (handoff):} Under the \emph{scoped-archive production valve}, do live LLMs obey closed days and act when open? Does a transparent no-LLM rule remain callable?
\textbf{RQ3b (content):} On the same vintaged tilts, does an ungated content probe beat momentum (\(\Delta\mathrm{CE}\); LOBO)?

\paragraph{Contributions}
(1)~AI--finance data-end semantics: epistemic observations, compilation \(\Pi_t\), world-quality index with band scope, and seeing vs.\ gating---interfaces that let LLM/agent stacks consume crypto markets safely.
(2)~An anonymized data-provision path for agents: collectors to BandPIT to service surface (bundle / health / handoff) to world bundles, plus a frozen four-arm harness to \emph{evaluate} market understanding.
(3)~Live LLM evidence that the substrate works: grounding \({\approx}0.97\); stress-gate abstain \(1.0\); scoped production valve obedience (closed \(1.0\); open act \({\approx}0.89\)); nonempty tilt handoff (Sharpe \(0.77\); LOBO \(1.0\)).
Estimand: whether public models can see a typed market world, refuse when thin, and hand off when ready---not portfolio alpha.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models}
World models learn compact states and often dynamics~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path}.
We address the complementary observation-side problem: a PIT-safe, quality-tagged crypto world \emph{before} dynamics or LLM policy---not a Dreamer/JEPA simulator.

\paragraph{LLM agents in finance (ICAIF intersection)}
Return prediction~\cite{lopez2023chatgpt}, open financial LLMs~\cite{yang2023fingpt}, memory- and tool-augmented agents~\cite{yu2023finmem,li2023tradinggpt,zhang2024finagent,yao2023react}, and ICAIF retrieval/debate/judge systems~\cite{choi2025finagentbench,shen2025finsearch,sun2025finresearchbench,duan2025factormad} improve how agents fetch, debate, and are scored.
Methodologically they still assume a usable observation stream; application stacks then conflate bad market context with bad policy.
We target that gap in a data-centric sense: compile a typed PIT world for LLM/agent consumers, and score market understanding (grounding, evidence-bound refusal) before debating alpha.
The estimand is orthogonal to memory routers and debate judges: without a typed world, those systems evaluate agents on tool fragments, not on whether the market view was broad, stable, and honest.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and PIT}
Abstention can be optimal under noise~\cite{elyaniv2010,geifman2017}; we attach refusal to \emph{world} quality \(q(W_t)\) via a runtime field, not classifier confidence alone (Prop.~\ref{prop:abstain})---blocking the overconfident-when-thin failure mode.
Macro vintages~\cite{croushore2001} and crypto microstructure~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022} motivate multi-band compilation with disclosed missingness (Prop.~\ref{prop:recon}).
Bootstrap discipline for the content probe follows~\cite{white2000,politis1994,harvey2016}.

\section{Observation-Layer Semantics}
\label{sec:method}

This section states the data-end theory as \emph{interface semantics}: what counts as an observation, how compilation yields a world, and when quality forces refusal.
It is not a pricing theorem and not a generative dynamics model.
Systems realization is Section~\ref{sec:system}; empirical tests are Section~\ref{sec:exp}.

\begin{definition}[Epistemic observation]
An observation is the tuple
\(O_{j,t}=(x_{j,t},\tau_{j,t},q_{j,t},g_{j,t},r_{j,t})\)
(value, latest-available time, quality, main-view gate, semantic role).
Omitting \((\tau,q,g,r)\) yields a feature dump, not a world observation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Compilation]
\(\Pi_t=B_t\circ M_t\circ A_t\): align clocks, apply honesty/missingness gates, assemble band views into a consumer object.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[World bundle]
\(W_t=\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t)\) with scalar quality \(q(W_t)\);
\(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\sigma(W_t)\).
\(W_t\) is the handoff object to decision.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[World-quality index]
\label{def:wmi}
Ready, limited, and missing counts disclose coverage.
We write \(q(W_t)\equiv\mathrm{WMI}_t\) for brevity in theoretical statements.
\(\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t U_t H_t\) aggregates breadth, stability, and honesty in \([0,1]\); ACWMI multiplies a regime weight.
Band scope selects the support of \((B,U,H)\): full-schema stress audit vs.\ the declared consumer archive (production valve).
Scoped WMI scores only declared bands; it is a contract-completeness index, not a lowered \(q^\star\).
The Compiled interface exposes a hard abstain boolean
\(\mathbf{1}\{q(W_t)<q^\star\}\) (and, under archive scope, incompleteness) with a thin-world label (production \(q^\star=0.2\) for scoped WMI).
We further define the Evidence Alignment Ratio (EAR) as the fraction of rationale evidence cues that bind to disclosed bundle observations (IDs/bands/tilts), measuring how tightly decisions attach to the world.
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Seeing vs.\ gating}
The bundle is what the model \emph{sees}; the hard flag \emph{blocks} action when \(q(W_t)<q^\star\).
Tilts without a gate are a dump; a gate without a readable bundle is only a switch.
The two interfaces are separable: Ungated keeps \(W_t\) and numeric \(q(W_t)\) but removes the hard boolean.
This is not feature engineering: a feature vector asks the model to infer reliability; a world bundle \emph{states} clocks, missingness, and a machine-readable valve.

\begin{assumption}[Bounded lag and increments]
\label{asm:lag}
Band \(j\) has max observation lag \(\Delta_j\); latent increments satisfy \(\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|\le\bar\delta\).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Multiplicative quality independence]
\label{asm:mult}
World availability requires breadth, stability, and honesty \emph{jointly}: if any factor is \(0\), then \(\mathrm{WMI}_t=0\).
The product \(\mathrm{WMI}=BUH\) is the simplest continuous \([0,1]\) score obeying this axiom; averages can stay large when one factor vanishes.
\end{assumption}
\begin{remark}
Practically, we treat \(\mathrm{WMI}<\varepsilon\) (e.g., \(\varepsilon{=}10^{-3}\)) as effective zero, consistent with the binary abstain contract.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Lag--missingness reconstruction bound]
\label{prop:recon}
Under Assumption~\ref{asm:lag}, any compiler that uses only in-clock observations satisfies
\[
\|\widetilde S_t-S_t\|
\;\le\;
C_{\mathrm{del}}\bar\delta\sum_j\Delta_j
+C_{\mathrm{noi}}\varepsilon_t
+C_{\mathrm{miss}}m_t,
\]
where \(m_t\) is disclosed missing mass and \(\varepsilon_t\) collects measurement noise.
Operationally, BandPIT maps age to ready/limited/missing so \(m_t\) and lag terms are readable in \(W_t\); compilation cannot erase delay, but it can refuse to pretend the bound is zero.
For typical crypto parameters (\(\Delta_{\mathrm{ex}}{\approx}2\mathrm{d}\), \(\Delta_{\mathrm{macro}}{\approx}5\mathrm{d}\), \(\bar\delta{\approx}\) daily volatility \({\approx}3\%\)), the lag term alone is already large relative to daily moves---illustratively \(\mathcal{O}(30\%)\) of daily-return variance scale---justifying refusal when \(B,U,H\) are incomplete rather than treating \(q^\star{=}0.2\) as a pure empirical knob.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Compilation \(\neq\) dump]
\label{prop:compile}
Enlarging raw span without \(\Pi_t\) need not enlarge usable \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}\): ungated stale evidence can raise volume while lowering honesty, tightening rather than loosening Prop.~\ref{prop:recon}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[World-conditional abstain]
\label{prop:abstain}
Let \(c_{\mathrm{act}}(W_t)\) be the expected cost of acting and \(c_{\mathrm{abs}}\) the abstain cost.
If \(c_{\mathrm{act}}(W_t)>c_{\mathrm{abs}}\) and \(c_{\mathrm{act}}\) is nonincreasing in \(q(W_t)\),
the Bayes action is abstain on \(\{W:q(W)<q^\star\}\)~\cite{elyaniv2010}.
A hard runtime contract implements that set; soft ``you may abstain'' text does not.
In finance, this aligns with the classic ``know when not to trade'' principle; our contribution is binding it to observable world quality rather than model confidence.
\end{proposition}
\begin{remark}[Threshold \(q^\star=0.2\)]
\label{rem:qstar}
Production valve: archive complete and \(\mathrm{WMI}\ge q^\star\).
For \(q^\star\in[0.10,0.30]\) open share stays \({\approx}0.61\) (thick days \(\approx 1\); gap days fail completeness; gap median \({\approx}0.14\)).
A WMI-only counterfactual moves when \(q^\star\) crosses that gap mass.
We keep \(0.2\) above gap-day WMI---also where Ungated soft thin-abstain fails (\({\approx}0.68\); Sec.~\ref{sec:gate}).
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[LOBO content vs.\ gating]
\label{prop:lobo}
Deleting band \(j\) changes a downstream value functional through a content channel and a gating channel.
Under an ungated rule the gating channel is shut, so the value gap identifies nonempty fields.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Theory \(\rightarrow\) protocol}
Prop.~\ref{prop:recon}--\ref{prop:compile} \(\Rightarrow\) RQ1.
Prop.~\ref{prop:abstain} \(\Rightarrow\) RQ2 (Compiled/HPO hard policy vs.\ Ungated soft MAY).
Def.~\ref{def:wmi}+Rem.~\ref{rem:qstar} \(\Rightarrow\) RQ3a (scoped valve).
Prop.~\ref{prop:lobo} \(\Rightarrow\) RQ3b.

\begin{remark}[Not a generative WM]
We do not learn \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\).
We deliver a state compiler with a quality-gated refuse interface: the data end of the stack.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Layered design}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Data layer.} Exchange, macro, and alternative collectors write vintage-aware history (observation timestamps; macro \texttt{available\_at}).
\item \textbf{Store layer.} Embedded stores hold raw series, merged market panels, and compiled analytics (readiness, WMI/ACWMI, world snapshots).
\item \textbf{Logic layer.} BandPIT reconstructs readiness on a previous-close clock; honesty and missingness gates implement \(\Pi_t\); WMI, ACWMI, and availability shocks \(O_t\) are first-class.
\item \textbf{Service layer.} A read API serves quality-tagged bundles and health objects without restarting collectors for newly arrived rows. Anonymized surface includes world-bundle reads, a valve-gated handoff endpoint (tilt action, no LLM), health exposing the abstain flag with band scope, a paper world-model time slice, and availability-shock queries.
\item \textbf{Consumer~/eval layer.} Frozen-prompt public LLMs (and rule agents) call an OpenAI-compatible adapter at temperature \(0\) under four arms---the harness that scores market understanding against the supplied world.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{PIT clock and freshness}
For calendar day \(t\), BandPIT reconstructs the decision information set at \((t{-}1)\,23{:}59\) (payoff is same-day close-to-close return); the bundle records \texttt{decision\_asof}.
Each band is graded ready~/limited~/missing against daily-scale age thresholds (Table~\ref{tab:fresh}).
Missingness is disclosed rather than imputed (Prop.~\ref{prop:recon}).
Breadth, stability, and honesty aggregate into \(\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t U_t H_t\) (ACWMI adds regime weight).
PIT-safe tilts use only in-clock observations: five-day macro changes, a seven-day stablecoin-flow slope, and \(\mathrm{mom5}\).
The \emph{scoped production valve} sets \texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} when the archive is incomplete or scoped \(\mathrm{WMI}<0.2\), and exposes \texttt{thin\_world}; bundles also disclose \texttt{full\_schema\_wmi} as a ceiling audit.
Figure~\ref{fig:ready}: macro and alternative stay ready; exchange readiness varies and defines thick/thin days.
Figure~\ref{fig:wmi}: same panel, two scopes---full-schema stress WMI stays under \(0.2\); scoped production WMI clears \(0.2\) on archive-complete days (shaded).
Table~\ref{tab:wmi}: scoped production valve opens on \({\approx}61\%\) OOS days (\(\equiv 3/3\) ready); full-schema stress gate stays closed.

\subsection{Arms and frozen prompts}
Actions \(\in\{\mathrm{bullish},\mathrm{bearish},\mathrm{neutral},\mathrm{abstain}\}\).
\textbf{Compiled:} full bundle; prompt \emph{requires} abstain when the hard boolean is true.
\textbf{HPO} (hard-prompt-only): same numeric fields as Ungated (\emph{no} boolean); prompt \emph{requires} abstain if \(\mathrm{WMI}<0.2\).
\textbf{Ungated:} same content and numeric WMI; soft prompt (\emph{may} abstain).
\textbf{Raw:} \(\mathrm{mom5}\) only.
\textbf{Blind:} date+asset, no feed.
\textbf{Dual protocol.} RQ1--RQ2 freeze full-schema bundles (\(\max\mathrm{WMI}\approx 0.093\)); RQ3a switches to scoped production bundles.
Prompts, IS/OOS cut, and seeds are frozen (Listings~\ref{lst:prompt-c}--\ref{lst:prompt-u}).

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Compiled (excerpt): hard boolean.},label={lst:prompt-c}]
1. If should_ai_abstain is true OR the world is thin,
   you MUST "abstain".
2. Prefer band content over raw momentum.
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Ungated (excerpt): soft judgment.},label={lst:prompt-u}]
1. Judge whether low WMI makes action unsafe;
   you MAY "abstain".
NO hard boolean; NO forced policy.
\end{lstlisting}

\paragraph{Why the hard valve is not a confound}
The data end must \emph{block} thin-day action (Prop.~\ref{prop:abstain}).
HPO separates hard \emph{prompt} obedience on numeric WMI from the typed boolean; Ungated removes both; Raw/Blind bound the ladder.

\subsection{Systems validation (engines)}
Paper-lab smoke wires BandPIT/WMI/ACWMI and checks the dual gate: scoped archive opens on \(3/3\)-ready statuses while full-schema stays closed; a handoff rule acts only when the scoped valve is open.
Availability shocks \(O_t\) are queryable on the service surface.

\subsection{RQ2 (safety valve): Hard refuse when the view is thin}
\label{sec:gate}
Legibility does not imply safe action.
All live LLM arms here freeze \emph{full-schema} bundles, so every OOS day is thin under the stress gate \(0.2\).
Table~\ref{tab:llm} and Figure~\ref{fig:und}: on the same \(100\) days, Compiled and HPO abstain \(1.0\); Ungated soft-judges at mean \(0.68\) (Gemini \(0.43\)); Raw over-trades (Table~\ref{tab:mix}); Blind abstains \(1.0\) with no feed---safe but useless as a data end.

Offline soft evidence-alignment (rationale cues to disclosed bands/tilts/quality) averages \({\approx}0.998\) Compiled / \(0.993\) HPO vs.\ \(0.80\) Ungated and \(0.38\) Raw on matched \(100\)-day slices.

\subsection{RQ3a (handoff): Scoped production valve with live LLMs}
\label{sec:econ}
Having established seeing (RQ1) and stress-gate refusal (RQ2), we ask whether the \emph{production} valve is callable.
The full-schema stress gate never clears \(0.2\), so it cannot open---by design in RQ2.
Scoped production WMI opens on \({\approx}61\%\) OOS days (\(\equiv 3/3\) ready).
We re-run the same four vendors on a stratified sample of \(70\) open + \(30\) closed OOS asset-days with scoped bundles (seed fixed; temp.\ \(0\)).

Table~\ref{tab:scopedllm}: on closed days Compiled abstains \(1.0\) for every vendor (Ungated soft rates \({\approx}0.87\)--\(0.97\); Raw often keeps trading).
On open days Compiled acts on a mean \({\approx}89\%\) of days---the valve opens and models consume the bundle.
The same sample with HPO (numeric WMI + hard prompt; no boolean) yields closed abstain \({\approx}0.96\) and open act \({\approx}0.86\): prompt obedience softens once WMI is no longer unambiguously far below \(0.2\), while the typed boolean remains \(1.0\).
Open-day CE does \emph{not} beat Raw (mean \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{\approx}{-}4.2\)): this is a usability check that models act when the valve opens, not a trading metric; the gap reflects LLM policy errors, not a world-bundle flaw (no-LLM tilt Sharpe \(0.772\), Table~\ref{tab:handoff}).
Closed-day refusal avoids Raw losses (mean \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{\approx}{+}1.6\) vs.\ Raw on the closed slice).

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:disc}

\paragraph{What carries the paper}
(i)~The compiled world is legible (grounding \({\approx}0.97\); sufficiency chat is not a substitute).
(ii)~Hard policy binds on thin days (Compiled/HPO \(1.0\)); soft MAY does not (\({\approx}0.68\)).
(iii)~Scoped production opens \({\approx}61\%\) OOS days; LLMs obey the valve; open-day CE \(\neq\) alpha; no-LLM tilts stay nonempty.
Relative to FinMem~/FinAgent and ICAIF agent stacks~\cite{yu2023finmem,zhang2024finagent,choi2025finagentbench,shen2025finsearch,sun2025finresearchbench,duan2025factormad}, we supply the observation stage those systems assume.

\paragraph{Deployment path}
Level~1: observation API (PIT world bundles; this paper).
Level~1 is already implementable today: any LLM agent can replace its raw OHLCV feed with a BandPIT read-API call and gain automatic thin-day refusal without changing decision logic.
Level~2: community benchmark for market understanding (grounding, gated action, soft EAR).
Level~3: compliance logging of abstain decisions for algorithmic accountability.
We do not claim to solve crypto prediction or open-day LLM alpha; seeing a typed world reduces decision risk before models get ``smarter.''

\paragraph{Design and scope}
World bundle \(\neq\) feature dump; previous-close PIT; dual WMI scope; HPO separates hard prompt from hard boolean.
Feature engineering outputs vectors; we output typed worlds with clocks, missingness declarations, and a machine-readable valve---downstream models read reliability rather than infer it.
Prompts, cut, and seeds are frozen; an anonymized harness ships on acceptance.

\balance
\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

AI-in-finance LLM/agent stacks need a reliable data end before decision or execution.
We formalize that observation layer and show: grounding \({\approx}0.97\); hard policy (Compiled/HPO) thin-abstains \(1.0\) while soft Ungated does not; scoped production opens and is obeyed; tilts remain nonempty without claiming open-day LLM alpha.
We do not teach models to be smarter. We teach them what the market looks like---and when not to guess.
In AI finance, seeing the world correctly is a prerequisite, not a luxury.
Move from model-first to data-end-first: compile so models can see; refuse when they must not act.
