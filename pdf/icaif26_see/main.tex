\title[Data End of Crypto Quant for Public LLMs]{%
  The Data End of Crypto Quant for Public LLMs:\texorpdfstring{\\}{ }%
  An Observation Layer that Compiles World Bundles}

\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Purpose.} End-to-end crypto quant for public LLMs needs a \emph{data end} before decision or execution.
We build that layer as an anonymized observation runtime: multi-band evidence \(\to\) BandPIT/WMI \(\to\) a point-in-time (PIT) \emph{world bundle}, with hard refusal when support is thin.
\textbf{Evidence.} Primary---seeing: grounding recovers ready/missing/tilt at \({\approx}0.97\) under Compiled vs.\ \({\lesssim}0.23\) under Raw, while sufficiency chat alone is unreliable (Compiled mean \(0.60\)).
Secondary---valve: Compiled thin-abstain is \(1.0\) (100-day and full OOS \(n{=}2000\)) while Ungated soft judgment is only \({\approx}0.68\) / \({\approx}0.56\); Raw over-trades.
Secondary---content: vintaged tilts beat momentum (\(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}0.334\), \(p{=}0.034\); LOBO content share \(1.0\)); open-slice Ungated CE is point-positive as a field lower bound.
On this panel \(\max\mathrm{WMI}\approx 0.093{<}0.2\), so production Compiled never opens---a valve stress test.
\textbf{Claim.} Build the quant data end first: compile so models can see; refuse when they must not act.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\paragraph{Purpose (read this first).}
Build the \emph{data end} of end-to-end crypto quant for public LLMs.
The stack is \textbf{data/observation} \(\to\) \textbf{decision} \(\to\) \textbf{execution} (Table~\ref{tab:stack}); agents and generative world models~\cite{yao2023react,yu2023finmem,zhang2024finagent,ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path} typically assume the first stage.
Crypto violates that assumption: exchange, macro, and alternative evidence arrive on incompatible clocks~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022}.
We own only the data end---compile a PIT world bundle so models can \emph{see}, and hard-refuse when the view is too thin.
Refusal is a safety valve, not the product and not a trading claim.

\paragraph{Failure modes.}
No feed \(\to\) refuse (safe, useless as a data end).
Thin price fragment \(\to\) trade and lose (unsafe for any downstream stack).
Disclosed but ungated world \(\to\) read fields, disagree on action.
So the data end must (i)~make the market legible and (ii)~enforce a quality valve before decision/execution.

\paragraph{What we build.}
An anonymized runtime (Section~\ref{sec:system}): multi-band evidence \(\to\) BandPIT/WMI \(\to\) JSON world bundle for GPT~/DeepSeek~/GLM~/Gemini-class models, served through a read API a decision layer can call.
``World model'' means state compiler + quality gate (Section~\ref{sec:method}), not dynamics and not a decision engine.
The bundle is complete, honest, and auditable; \texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} is the machine-readable valve.

\paragraph{How we evaluate.}
\textbf{RQ1 (primary---seeing):} Are ready~/missing~/tilt answers verifiable against PIT ground truth under Compiled vs.\ Raw?
\textbf{RQ2 (valve):} Does a hard flag enforce thin abstention where soft disclosure does not?
Four arms: Compiled, Ungated (same content, no flag), Raw (\texttt{mom5}), Blind.
\textbf{RQ3 (secondary):} Can a transparent no-LLM rule hand off from the bundle (production refuse vs.\ nonempty tilts)?

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(1)~Data-end semantics: epistemic observations, lag reconstruction, \(\Pi_t\), and seeing vs.\ gating.
(2)~Anonymized PIT compile path: layered collectors\(\to\)BandPIT\(\to\)service surface\(\to\)bundles; schema honesty (eight bands, three live); frozen four-arm harness.
(3)~Live + handoff validation: grounding \({\approx}0.97\) with sufficiency dissociation; thin-abstain \(1.0\) at \(100\)-day and full-OOS scale; no-LLM handoff (production refuse vs.\ nonempty tilts, \(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}0.334\); LOBO content share \(1.0\)) and an open-slice lower bound.
Estimand: legibility and valve reliability---not decision SOTA, execution, or Compiled-open alpha.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models.}
World models learn compact states and often dynamics~\cite{ha2018world,hafner2020dreamer,lecun2022path}.
We address the complementary observation-side problem: a PIT-safe, quality-tagged crypto world \emph{before} dynamics or LLM policy---not a Dreamer/JEPA simulator.

\paragraph{LLM agents in finance.}
Return prediction~\cite{lopez2023chatgpt}, open financial LLMs~\cite{yang2023fingpt}, memory- and tool-augmented agents~\cite{yu2023finmem,li2023tradinggpt,zhang2024finagent,yao2023react}, and ICAIF retrieval/debate/judge systems~\cite{choi2025finagentbench,shen2025finsearch,sun2025finresearchbench,duan2025factormad} improve how agents fetch, debate, and are scored.
They still assume a usable observation stream at the data end of the stack.
We study that prior stage: compile a world bundle, measure whether models can \emph{see} it, then gate action when they must not act.
Our estimand is therefore orthogonal to memory routers and debate judges: without a typed PIT world, those stacks consume whatever fragment the tool dump provides.

\paragraph{Selective prediction and PIT.}
Abstention can be optimal under noise~\cite{elyaniv2010,geifman2017}; we attach refusal to world quality via a runtime field, not classifier confidence alone.
Macro vintages~\cite{croushore2001} and crypto microstructure~\cite{makarov2020,liu2022} motivate multi-band compilation with disclosed missingness.
Bootstrap discipline for the content probe follows~\cite{white2000,politis1994,harvey2016}.

\section{Observation Layer}
\label{sec:method}

\begin{definition}[Epistemic observation]
\(O_{j,t}=(x,\tau,q,g,r)\):
value, latest-available time, quality, main-view gate, semantic role.
Omitting \((\tau,q,g,r)\) yields a feature dump, not a world observation.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[World bundle]
\(W_t=\Pi_t(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{raw}}_t)\) with scalar quality \(q(W_t)\) (WMI/ACWMI);
\(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}_t=\sigma(W_t)\).
\(\Pi_t\) aligns clocks, gates honesty/missingness, and emits consumer JSON---the handoff to decision.
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Completeness, honesty, quality.}
Ready/limited/missing counts disclose coverage.
\(\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t U_t H_t\) aggregates breadth, stability, and honesty; ACWMI adds regime weight.
Compiled exposes \texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} and \texttt{thin\_world}.

\paragraph{Seeing vs.\ gating.}
The bundle is what the model \emph{sees}; the hard flag \emph{blocks} action when \(q(W_t)\) is too low.
Tilts without a gate are a dump; a gate without a readable bundle is only a switch.

\begin{assumption}[Bounded lag and increments]
Band \(j\) has max observation lag \(\Delta_j\); latent increments satisfy \(\|S_u-S_{u-1}\|\le\bar\delta\).
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}[Lag reconstruction bound]
\label{prop:recon}
Under Assumption~1,
\(\|\widetilde S_t-S_t\|\le C_{\mathrm{del}}\bar\delta+C_{\mathrm{noi}}\varepsilon_t+C_{\mathrm{miss}}m_t\).
Compilation cannot erase delay; it can refuse to pretend the bound is zero.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Intuition.}
Agents that concatenate tool dumps silently absorb delay and missingness into the feature vector.
Prop.~\ref{prop:recon} makes those terms explicit: a thicker raw payload can worsen reconstruction if honesty collapses.
The data end therefore grades readiness and discloses gaps instead of imputing a false full world.

\begin{proposition}[Compilation \(\neq\) dump]
\label{prop:compile}
Enlarging raw span without \(\Pi_t\) need not enlarge usable \(\mathcal{F}^{\mathrm{AI}}\): ungated stale evidence can raise volume while lowering honesty.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[World-conditional abstention]
\label{prop:abstain}
If non-abstain actions exceed a world-dependent cost, the Bayes action is abstain; with costs decreasing in WMI, abstention forms a lower set in world quality~\cite{elyaniv2010}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[LOBO content vs.\ gating]
\label{prop:lobo}
Deleting band \(j\) changes value through a content channel and a gating channel.
Empirically we report content share under an ungated rule so the probe speaks to nonempty fields.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Implication for the runtime.}
Props.~\ref{prop:compile}--\ref{prop:abstain} say the data end must export gates and abstain flags, not tilts alone: compilation without quality disclosure is still a feature dump; disclosure without a hard gate still lets thin worlds drive action.
Prop.~\ref{prop:lobo} motivates the economic check in RQ3: if deleting a durable band does not change the content rule, the bundle is empty of content.

\begin{remark}[Not a generative WM]
We do not learn \(p(s_{t+1}\mid s_t,a_t)\). We deliver a state compiler with a quality-gated refuse interface---the data end of the stack.
\end{remark}

\section{Runtime: Data-End Compile Path}
\label{sec:system}

The anonymized prototype implements the data end as a layered compile path (Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline}, Table~\ref{tab:modules}).
Decision and execution sit downstream; this paper does not claim them.
Paths and product names are omitted for double-blind review.

\subsection{Layered design}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Data layer.} Exchange, macro, and alternative collectors write vintage-aware history (observation timestamps; macro \texttt{available\_at}).
\item \textbf{Store layer.} Embedded stores hold raw series, merged market panels, and compiled analytics (readiness, WMI/ACWMI, world snapshots).
\item \textbf{Logic layer.} BandPIT reconstructs readiness on a previous-close clock; honesty/missingness gates implement \(\Pi_t\); WMI/ACWMI and availability shocks \(O_t\) (band outage events for quasi-exogenous levers) are first-class.
\item \textbf{Service layer.} A read API serves quality-tagged bundles and health objects without restarting collectors for newly arrived rows. Anonymized surface includes world-bundle and decision-bundle reads, health exposing \texttt{should\_ai\_abstain}, a paper world-model time slice, and availability-shock queries---the machine-readable handoff a decision layer would call.
\item \textbf{Consumer layer.} Frozen-prompt LLM/rule agents call an OpenAI-compatible adapter at temperature \(0\) under four arms.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{PIT clock and freshness}
For calendar day \(t\), BandPIT reconstructs the decision information set at \((t{-}1)\,23{:}59\) (payoff is same-day close-to-close return); the bundle records \texttt{decision\_asof}.
Each band is graded ready~/limited~/missing against daily-scale age thresholds (Table~\ref{tab:fresh}).
Missingness is disclosed rather than imputed (Prop.~\ref{prop:recon}).
Breadth, stability, and honesty aggregate into \(\mathrm{WMI}_t=B_t U_t H_t\) (ACWMI adds regime weight).
PIT-safe tilts use only in-clock observations: five-day macro changes, a seven-day stablecoin-flow slope, and \(\mathrm{mom5}\).
The valve sets \texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} when \(\mathrm{WMI}<0.2\) and exposes \texttt{thin\_world}.
Figure~\ref{fig:ready}: macro and alternative stay ready; exchange readiness varies and defines thick/thin days.
Figure~\ref{fig:wmi}: WMI stays below the production gate throughout; ACWMI can look healthier under regime weight, so the runtime exports both and lets the configured index mode drive abstain.
Table~\ref{tab:wmi}: production gate \(0.2\) never opens on this panel; \(\mathrm{WMI}\ge 0.05\) selects the band-thick subset (\({\approx}61\%\) OOS).

\subsection{Four arms and frozen prompts}
Actions \(\in\{\mathrm{bullish},\mathrm{bearish},\mathrm{neutral},\mathrm{abstain}\}\).
\textbf{Compiled:} full bundle; frozen prompt \emph{requires} abstain when \texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} is true (and when the world is judged thin).
\textbf{Ungated:} same content, completeness, and numeric WMI; \emph{no} hard flag; soft prompt asks the model to judge thinness.
\textbf{Raw:} \(\mathrm{mom5}\) only---common price-only wiring for the ladder, not an exhaustive tool-agent baseline; the estimand is the four-arm ranking of the data end, not \(\Delta\)CE vs.\ the strongest agent.
\textbf{Blind:} ``Today is \(d\). How should I trade \(a\)?'' with no feed.
\(\max\mathrm{WMI}\approx 0.093\), so production Compiled abstains on every OOS day---a scarce-support stress test of the valve, not a claim the data end never allows action.
Compiled vs.\ Ungated differ only in enforcement (Listings~\ref{lst:prompt-c}--\ref{lst:prompt-u}); prompts, IS/OOS cut, and seeds are frozen.
On thin Compiled days the evidence-alignment proxy (EAR) is \(1.0\) across vendors: rationales stay bound to disclosed \texttt{evidence\_ids}.

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Compiled (excerpt): hard valve.},label={lst:prompt-c}]
1. If should_ai_abstain is true OR the world is thin,
   you MUST "abstain".
2. Prefer band content over raw momentum
   (macro_tilt, alt_tilt are PIT-safe).
3. Rationale must match evidence_ids.
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={Ungated (excerpt): soft judgment.},label={lst:prompt-u}]
1. Judge whether low WMI / missingness makes action
   unsafe; you MAY "abstain".
2. Prefer band content when you act.
NO hard abstain boolean; NO forced policy.
\end{lstlisting}

\paragraph{Why the hard valve is not a confound.}
Compiled abstention is partly prompt obedience, and that is intentional: the data end's job on thin days is to \emph{block} action, not to hope the model discovers thinness (Prop.~\ref{prop:abstain}).
Ungated measures soft judgment from the same disclosed bundle; Raw measures fragment wiring; Blind measures no feed.
The gating result is that the hard flag is reliable across vendors; soft refusal is not---which is why half-baked context remains dangerous without the valve.

\subsection{Systems validation (engines)}
Paper-lab smoke against the production engines (same thresholds as the live service) returns a wired WMI/ACWMI sample with \texttt{should\_ai\_abstain} consistent with the configured index mode and abstain cutoffs (\(\mathrm{WMI}\) gate \(0.2\); ACWMI gate \(0.35\)).
That check is not a substitute for the four-arm OOS evaluation below; it confirms the data-end indices the consumer bundle exposes are the same objects the runtime compiles.
Availability shocks \(O_t\) are queryable on the service surface for event-study levers; this paper uses them as schema honesty, not as a trading identification claim.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup}
PIT panel \(\sim\)399 days \(\times\) 10 liquid names (\({\approx}3990\) asset-days; 2025-07--2026-08), chronological IS/OOS \(200/200\) days (cut \(2026\)-\(01\)-\(16\)).
Unless noted, live arms share the same stratified \(100\) OOS asset-days (seed fixed), temperature \(0\), and four flash/mini vendors (gpt-5.4-mini, deepseek-v4-flash, glm-5.2, gemini-3.5-flash-lite) via an OpenAI-compatible adapter.
Grounding uses \(50\) OOS days; full-OOS abstention uses \(n{=}2000\); the no-LLM probe uses \(199\) OOS days after return alignment.
Primary metrics: RQ1 grounding F1/tilt accuracy (sufficiency chat secondary); RQ2 thin-world abstain rate (CE as avoided loss, not alpha); RQ3 \(\Delta\)CE of the content rule vs.\ momentum.

\subsection{RQ1 (primary): Seeing---grounding workflow}
\label{sec:grounding}
If the data end works, models must report a \emph{checkable} market view---not invent coverage from a price fragment.

On \(50\) OOS asset-days, each model lists ready/missing bands among exchange/macro/alternative, reports \(\mathrm{macro\_tilt}\) / \(\mathrm{alt\_tilt}\) signs, and judges sufficiency; (i)--(iii) score against PIT ground truth.

Table~\ref{tab:grounding}: Compiled mean ready/missing F1 and tilt-sign accuracy \(0.97\); Raw collapses to \(0.04\), \(0.23\), \(0.19\).
Raw has no readiness or tilt fields, so it cannot recover archive state; Compiled forces use of the disclosed world.

\subsection{Discussion}
\paragraph{What carries the paper.}
(i)~The data end is legible: checkable grounding at \({\approx}0.97\), with sufficiency chat shown to be a poor substitute (RQ1).
(ii)~Thin views must not drive action: soft text fails at \(100\)-day and full-OOS scale; Blind shows models can refuse with no feed, but Raw shows thin fragments make them act and lose; the hard valve works (RQ2).
(iii)~A no-LLM handoff backtest shows production refuse vs.\ nonempty content on the same bundle (RQ3); LOBO content share \(1.0\); open-slice Ungated is a lower bound, not Compiled-open.
Relative to FinMem~/FinAgent and ICAIF agent stacks~\cite{yu2023finmem,zhang2024finagent,choi2025finagentbench,shen2025finsearch,sun2025finresearchbench,duan2025factormad}, we supply the observation stage those systems assume---not a competing decision or execution layer.

\paragraph{Why grounding first.}
Trading PnL confounds observation quality with policy skill.
Grounding asks whether the consumer can audit ready/missing/signed state; Raw cannot, Compiled can.
The valve then asks whether soft judgment stops action on a thin disclosed world; empirically, no---at either sample size.
That ordering matches the stack: a decision engine that cannot see, or that acts on thin support, is not rescued by better execution.

\paragraph{Design.}
World bundle not feature dump; previous-close PIT with explicit freshness; four arms separate seeing+gating, seeing without gating, thin wiring, and no feed.
The evaluation object is the three-band bundle in Table~\ref{tab:dataflow}; WMI is computed over the full schema so missingness stays visible.
Service-layer health and bundle reads make the valve and the view callable by any downstream policy without re-ingesting collectors.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
Paper-lab smoke checks BandPIT/WMI/ACWMI wiring; PIT rebuild and the frozen four-arm harness regenerate the panel and live transcripts used here.
Prompts, IS/OOS cut, seeds, and vendor IDs are fixed; credentials stay in environment variables.
An anonymized harness ships on acceptance.

\paragraph{Scope.}
Estimand is data-end legibility, valve reliability, and a no-LLM handoff probe on the evaluation archive---not decision-layer SOTA, not execution, and not production Compiled-open alpha.
Decision and execution stacks sit downstream of this observation layer.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

End-to-end crypto quant for public LLMs needs a reliable data end before decision or execution.
We contribute that observation layer: compile asynchronous evidence into a consumable PIT world bundle, disclose structural missingness, and hard-refuse when the view is too thin.
Grounding recovers ready/missing/tilt at \({\approx}0.97\) under Compiled vs.\ Raw collapse; sufficiency chat alone fails; the hard valve abstains \(1.0\) on thin days (including full OOS) where soft judgment does not; vintaged tilts are nonempty (\(\Delta\mathrm{CE}{=}0.334\); LOBO content share \(1.0\)).
Build the quant data end first: compile so models can see; refuse when they must not act.
